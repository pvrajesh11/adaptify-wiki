# Auto PPA — Multistate — Coverage Options

Parent: [Auto PPA — Multistate — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source: MULTI PAU Manual 01 21 §2, §3, §4, §6.6

---

## Eligibility (Rule 2)

**Eligible insureds:** Individuals or individual + spouse resident in same household owning an eligible vehicle; joint owners who are resident family members, resident individuals, or non-resident family members/spouses (non-resident spouses require PA 1200 per Rule 4.9); named non-owners (Rule 4.11); additional insureds (Rule 4.8); trustees (Rule 4.10). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §2.1)

**Eligible vehicles:** Private passenger autos, pickups, or vans used primarily for personal, non-commercial purposes; trailers; miscellaneous type vehicles (Rule 11). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §2.2)

**Ineligible:** Vehicles owned by corporations/partnerships/business entities (except farm family); vehicles designed primarily for off-road use; vehicles used principally for commercial purposes (except farming/ranching or specifically allowed). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §2.3)

## Policy Form — Coverage Combinations (Rule 3.1)

PA 0001 may provide: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §3.1)
- Liability only
- Liability + Medical Payments
- Liability + Uninsured Motorist
- Liability + Medical Payments + Uninsured Motorist
- Physical Damage (with or without liability and/or other coverages)
- Underinsured Motorist via endorsement PA 2200

Separate premium charged for each vehicle and coverage.

---

## Uninsured Motorist Coverage (Rule 3.4)

Available when PA 0001 covers liability of described vehicles. Per person / per accident basis. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §3.4)
Combined Single Limit option: PA 0700.

> **AK override:** Mandatory offering — named insured must reject in writing to waive. Must also elect stacked (PA 2161) or non-stacked (PA 2137). See [AK — Coverage Options](../../../product-states/auto-ppa-AK/rating-rules/coverage-options.md).

---

## Underinsured Motorist Coverage (Rule 3.7)

Available when PA 0001 covers liability of described vehicles. Per person / per accident basis. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §3.7)
Applied via endorsement PA 2200. Combined Single Limit option: PA 0800.

> **AK override:** Mandatory offering — named insured must reject in writing to waive. Must also elect stacked (PA 2256) or non-stacked (PA 2237).

---

## Physical Damage (Rule 3.5–3.6)

Comprehensive: direct and accidental loss other than collision. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §3.5)
Collision: direct and accidental loss from collision.
Automatically includes child safety seat expenses and transportation expenses.
Settled at actual cash value (lesser of ACV, repair/replacement cost, or limit).

## Non-Owner Coverages (Rule 6.6)

| Coverage | Eligibility | Available Coverages | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| Named Non-Owner (6.6.1) | Named person without owned vehicle | Liability, Med Pay, UM, UIM | PA 1500 |
| Extended Non-Owned — Named Person (6.6.2) | Named person without owned vehicle | Liability, Med Pay only | PA 1600 |
| Extended Non-Owned — Regularly Furnished (6.6.3) | Vehicle available/furnished for regular use | Liability, Med Pay only | PA 1700 |
| Extended Non-Owned — Public/Livery (6.6.4) | Vehicle used as public/livery conveyance | Liability, Med Pay only | PA 1800 |
| Extended Non-Owned — Auto Business (6.6.5) | Named person employed in auto business | Liability, Med Pay only | PA 1900 |

Physical Damage not available on non-owner coverages.

## Policywriting Rules (Rule 4)

- **Policy terms:** Annual; semi-annual (where permitted); monthly (where permitted) — Rule 4.5
- **Coverage territory:** US, territories, possessions, Puerto Rico, Canada, and while vehicle transported between — Rule 4.6
- **Additional insureds:** PA 1100; no premium — Rule 4.8
- **Joint ownership:** PA 1200; no premium — Rule 4.9 *(AK uses PA 1237)*
- **Accident forgiveness:** PA 9603 — eliminates first at-fault accident from rating — Rule 4.7.4
