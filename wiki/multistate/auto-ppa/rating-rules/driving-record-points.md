# Auto PPA — Multistate — Driving Record Points

Parent: [Auto PPA — Multistate — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source: MULTI PAU Manual 01 21 §6.4, §6.1–6.5

---

## Experience Period

3 years immediately preceding policy inception date or renewal effective date. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §6.4.1)

---

## Point Assignment Triggers

- Convictions for motor vehicle violations
- At-fault accidents where a claim was paid by the company exceeding the approved dollar threshold

When multiple violations from one occurrence: use only the highest point-value violation.
2nd+ violation in experience period: assessed both age-of-violation points AND additional violation surcharge.

## Additional Points

- **4 points** — lapse in mandatory/compulsory insurance of 30+ days, unless driver did not own a vehicle during that period OR lapse was due to active military service/deployment
- **1 point** — conviction not in classification table requiring license suspension/revocation OR financial responsibility filing (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §6.4.1)

---

## Exceptions — Points NOT Assigned For

- Violations/accidents for driver who is named insured of a separate policy or principal driver of a vehicle on a separate policy
- Non-moving violations (vehicle equipment)
- At-fault accidents eliminated by Accident Forgiveness (Rule 4.7.4)
- Accidents where insured auto was lawfully parked and stationary (rolling vehicle: points to driver who parked it)
- Accidents where insured driver is reimbursed by or has judgment against responsible party
- Accidents covered under UM or UIM coverage
- Accidents covered under Comprehensive Coverage
- Rear-end collisions where insured was struck from behind without driver conviction + other driver convicted
- Emergency response accidents (paid/volunteer: police, fire, first-aid, law enforcement)
- Additional state page exceptions may apply

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §6.4.1)

---

## Financial Responsibility Filing (Rule 6.4.2)

Applied to any driver for whom financial responsibility must be filed under state law on inception or renewal date. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §6.4.2)

> **AK override:** Rule does not apply in Alaska.
> **AL override:** Rule does not apply in Alabama.

---

## Unverifiable Driving Record (Rule 6.4.3)

Applied to driver with no violations/accidents reported and driving history cannot be verified through company records, state MVR, or commercial records. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_MULTI_manual_v01-21 §6.4.3)

> **AK status:** Not mentioned in AK state manual — verify whether multistate rule applies or is excluded in AK.

## Driver Discounts (Rule 6.3)

| Discount | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| Driver Training (6.3.1) | Drivers under 21; 30+ classroom hours, 6+ in-vehicle or 12+ simulator hours |
| Defensive Driving (6.3.2) | Any driver; approved course within 3 years; no commercial license |
| Good Student (6.3.3) | Drivers 16+; full-time student; upper 20% / B average / 3.0 GPA / Dean's List |
| Distant Driver — Student Away (6.3.4.1) | Resident driver at school 100+ miles from vehicle garage |
| Distant Driver — Deployed (6.3.4.2) | Active military in different location from vehicle garage |
| Safe Driver (6.3.5) | No driving record points in 3 years preceding inception/renewal |
