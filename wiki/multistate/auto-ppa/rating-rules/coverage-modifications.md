## Coverage Modifications (Rule 8)

| Rule | Coverage | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | Limited Mexico Coverage | PA 6601 | Within 25 miles of US border; 10 consecutive days max |
| 8.2 | Mexico Collision Coverage | PA 7611 | Collision Coverage for loss in Mexico |
| 8.3.1 | Stated Maximum Value | PA 7601 | Settled at lesser of stated value, ACV, or repair cost |
| 8.3.2 | Agreed Value | PA 7602 | Settled at lesser of agreed value or repair cost |
| 8.3.3 | Collision Deductible Waiver | PA 7603 | Waives deductible when loss meets criteria |
| 8.3.4 | Diminution of Value | PA 7604 | Covers diminished value under Physical Damage |
| 8.3.5 | New Vehicle Replacement Cost | PA 7605 | Full replacement cost on qualifying total loss |
| 8.3.6 | Diminishing Deductible | PA 7606 | Amends deductible application after loss |
| 8.3.7 | Loss Settlement Amendment | PA 7607 | No payment for pre-existing unrepaired damage; no charge |
| 8.4 | Auto Loan/Lease | PA 7608 | Payment above ACV to cover loan/lease balance on total loss |
| 8.5.1 | Full Safety Glass | PA 7609 | Safety glass without deductible; requires Comprehensive |
| 8.5.2 | Full Windshield | PA 7610 | Windshield without deductible; requires Comprehensive |
| 8.6 | Rental Vehicle Physical Damage | PA 7612 | Physical Damage for rented vehicle; includes loss-of-use |
| 8.7 | Vehicle Sharing Physical Damage | PA 7613 | Physical Damage for described vehicle in sharing program |
| 8.8 | Participating Coverage | PA 7614 | Stated value; deductible is percentage of stated value |

## Coverage Exclusions (Rule 10)

| Rule | Exclusion | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10.1 | Federal Employees — Government Business | PA 4501 | No charge |
| 10.2 | Intra-Family Liability Exclusion | PA 4502 | Excludes BI to named insured/family members |
| 10.3 | Custom Equipment Exclusion | PA 4503 | Eliminates automatic $1,000 coverage |
| 10.4 | Foreign Made/Discontinued Makes Exclusion | PA 4504 | Unavailability of parts/services |
| 10.5 | Named Driver Exclusion | PA 4800 | Excluded driver removed from premium determination |

> **AK overrides:**
> - Rule 10.2 (Intra-Family Liability Exclusion): **Does not apply in Alaska**
> - Rule 10.5 Named Driver Exclusion form: **PA 4324** (AK-specific form, not PA 4800)
