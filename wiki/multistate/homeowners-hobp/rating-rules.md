## Known State Overrides

| Topic | Multistate Rule | AK | AL | FL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mandatory endorsements (Rule 4.2) | None specified | 13 required (HO 0814, HO 1300–1308, HO 1323, HO 1335, HO 1347, HO 4505) | 13 required (HO 0814/0801, HO 1300–1308, HO 1323, HO 1335, HO 1347, HO 4505) | 14 required (HO 0814/0809, HO 1300–1308, HO 1323, HO 1335, HO 1347, HO 4505) |
| Claim Free Discount (Rule 6.9) | No catastrophic exclusion | Catastrophic claims (≥$25M event) excluded | Same | Same |
| Risk Group Assignment (Rule 6.11) | Standard | Catastrophic event exclusion added | Same | Same |
| Owner Occupancy (Rule 4.19) | Not in multistate | Option to exclude coverage 61+ days after insured vacates | Same | Same |
| Roof Surfacing ACV — All Perils (Rule 8.10) | Not in multistate | ACV option for all perils (HO 4836) | Same | Same |
| Cosmetic Damage Exclusion (Rule 8.11) | Not in multistate | Exclude cosmetic wind/hail damage to surfacing (HO 2002) | Same | Same |
| Innocent insured / abuse exception | No exception | Domestic violence exception (AS 18.66) — HO 0854 02 21 | Abuse exception (Alabama Protection From Abuse Act) — HO 0801 09 20 | Domestic violence exception (Florida statute) — HO 0809 02 22 |
| Subrogation | Standard | Innocent insured subject to DV cannot waive recovery right | Same (abuse context) | Same (domestic violence context) |
| Renewal notice | Not in base | 20 days before >10% increase or material coverage reduction | Not specified in state pages | 30 days before premium increase renewal |
| Suit Against Us | Standard contractual period | 3 years from claim denial; defers to AK law if shorter | Not specified in state pages | Florida statutory period (contractual period removed) |
| Settling/cracking exclusion | Standard language | Replaced with enumerated cause-neutral list (HO 0003/0005) | Unchanged | Unchanged |
| Dominant/efficient proximate cause | Not in base | Added to property exclusion intro (all forms) | Unchanged | Unchanged |
| Pollutant definition | Standard | Expanded: adds "radioactive"; broader waste definition | Unchanged | Unchanged |
| Cancellation/nonrenewal | Standard | Replaced — AK-specific grounds, notice periods, return premium | Unchanged | Unchanged |
| Appraisal | Standard | Replaced — AK-specific: 10-day selection, 15-day statement, umpire fee allocation | Unchanged | Unchanged |
