## Optional Property Coverages (Rule 9)

Only coverages under Rules 9.8.3, 9.9.2, 9.9.6, and 9.20 are permitted on HO 0008 policies. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §9)

### Earthquake (Rule 9.1)

Available on HO 0001/0002/0003/0004/0005/0006. Endorsement HO 2754.

- Coverage for direct physical loss caused by earthquake.
- Coverage for exterior masonry veneer is optional (classify as Frame if excluded).
- Separate earthquake deductible: 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, or 25%.
- Only one earthquake deductible option per policy.
- Premium for HO 0001/0002/0003/0005 subject to year-of-construction and number-of-stories modification factors.
- Frame construction retrofit credit available when: home built prior to 1979, tied to foundation, cripple walls braced with plywood, and water heater secured to building frame.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §9.1)

### Water Back Up And Sump Discharge Or Overflow (Rule 9.2)

All forms. Endorsement HO 2708. Limits: $5,000 / $10,000 / $15,000 / $20,000 / $25,000. Special deductible applies; available options are the all other perils deductible options (Rule 7.2). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §9.2)

### Limited Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, Or Dry Rot (Rule 9.20)

All forms. Endorsement HO 2089. Must coincide with limited liability coverage (Rule 10.15). Property
aggregate limits available: $10,000 / $25,000 / $50,000. Aggregate applies to all loss, cleanup costs,
access costs, and testing costs caused by bacteria/fungi/wet rot/dry rot. Bacteria/fungi from fire or
lightning not subject to aggregate. Rate is per policy. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §9.20)

### Coverage For Resident Of Assisted Living Facility (Rule 9.21)

All forms. Endorsement HO 6235. Person must be a relative of insured not in the household, residing in an
assisted living facility (not hospice, prison, or rehab facility). Default limits: $10,000 Coverage C (with
special limits); $6,000 Coverage D ($500/month max); $100,000 Coverage L. Coverage C may be increased;
Coverage L may be reduced or increased. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §9.21)

### Student Living Away From The Described Location (Rule 9.22)

All forms. Endorsement HO 6036. Student must: be away at school; have been a household member before moving;
not be an insured as defined (e.g., part-time student or student over 25). Must be a relative of named insured
or under 21 and in the care of the insured or a household relative. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §9.22)
