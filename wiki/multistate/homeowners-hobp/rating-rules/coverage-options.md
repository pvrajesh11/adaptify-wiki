## Principal Property Coverages — Mandatory (Rule 3.2)

Forms HO 0001/0002/0003/0005/0008 provide all principal property coverages. HO 0004 provides only
Coverages C and D. HO 0006 provides only Coverages A, C, and D. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §3.2)

| Coverage | Description |
|---|---|
| Coverage A — Residence | Covers the residence on the described location including additions and built-in components. On HO 0006: fixtures, alterations, and additions that are part of the condo/co-op unit, structures owned solely by the insured, and other property that is the insured's insurance responsibility. |
| Coverage B — Related Private Structures | Covers unattached structures on the described location related to the residence. Excludes structures rented to non-tenants (unless used solely for private garage purposes), used for business direction/operation, or used for business property storage. Includes fences, driveways, sidewalks, and permanently installed outdoor fixtures. |
| Coverage C — Personal Property | Covers personal property owned or used by an insured. Property usually at another residence limited to 10% of Coverage C limit or $1,000, whichever is greater. |
| Coverage D — Additional Living Costs And Loss Of Rent | Covers increased living costs and loss of fair rental value when a covered loss makes the described location unfit for use as a residence. |

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §3.2.1–3.2.4)

## Principal Liability Coverages — Mandatory (Rule 3.5)

Coverages L and M may only be omitted when contributing insurance applies (Rule 4.17) or when the policy
covers an additional or secondary location (Rule 12). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §3.5)

| Coverage | Description |
|---|---|
| Coverage L — Personal Liability | Pays on behalf of the insured for damages due to bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence related to an insured premises or the insured's personal activities. |
| Coverage M — Medical Payments To Others | Pays medical expenses for non-insureds injured in connection with an insured premises or the insured's personal activities. |

## Basic Limits (Rule 3.6)

**Property Coverages:**

| Form | Coverage A | Coverage B | Coverage C | Coverage D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HO 0001/0002/0003 | Refer to Company | 10% of Cov A | 50% of Cov A¹ | 30% of Cov A² |
| HO 0004 | — | — | Refer to Company | 40% of Cov C |
| HO 0005 | Refer to Company | 10% of Cov A | 70% of Cov A³ | 30% of Cov A² |
| HO 0006 | 10% of Cov C | — | Refer to Company | 40% of Cov C |
| HO 0008 | Refer to Company | 10% of Cov A | 50% of Cov A¹ | 20% of Cov A⁴ |

¹ 30% for 3–4 family dwellings
² 20% for 3–4 family dwellings
³ 50% for 3–4 family dwellings
⁴ 10% for 3–4 family dwellings

**Liability Coverages (all forms):**
- Coverage L: $100,000 each occurrence
- Coverage M: $1,000 per person (at company option, also subject to a per accident limit)

Coverage L may be reduced to $25,000 or $50,000 at company option; Coverage M per person may be reduced
to $500. Both may be increased. Show limits on declarations. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §3.6, §5)

## Loss Settlement Provisions (Rule 3.3)

**Coverages A and B:**
- HO 0001/0002/0003: replacement cost applies when insured to 80% or more of replacement value; otherwise actual cash value.
- HO 0006 Coverage A: replacement cost basis.
- HO 0008: actual cash value basis.

**Coverage C:**
- All forms except HO 0005: actual cash value basis.
- HO 0005: replacement cost basis.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §3.3)

## Policywriting Instructions (Rule 4)

**Policy terms:**
- Annual (Rule 4.3.1) — state pages show annual rating information; minimum term is one year except for common anniversary date alignment.
- Three-year prepaid (Rule 4.3.2) — multiply annual premium by state pages factor; premium paid at inception.
- Three-year deferred (Rule 4.3.3) — premium paid in annual installments; each installment uses rules/rates in effect on anniversary date; endorsement HO 6581.
- Less than one year (Rule 4.3.4) — permitted only to maintain common anniversary dates with other policies; prorate Step 6 premium.

**Premium rounding**: nearest whole dollar; $0.50 or more rounds up. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.5)

**Interpolation**: permitted for limits between two shown base amounts using linear interpolation procedure. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.7)

**Mandatory endorsements**: identified in state pages; no multistate mandatory endorsements specified. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.2)

**Additional interests** — no premium charge unless noted:

| Endorsement | Coverage | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HO 6041 | Interest in Coverages A and B and premises liability | No charge |
| HO 6072 | Interest in Coverages A, B, C, L, and M | No charge |
| HO 6016 | Landlord liability (described location only, while insured leases/occupies) | No charge |
| HO 6033 | Other residents of household (extends insured definition for Cov C, L, M) | Separate charge per person |
| HO 6034 | Described location interest (other than mortgagee or persons covered above) | No charge |

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.9)

**Canine exclusion**: endorsement HO 2001 excludes BI and PD from direct physical contact with described canine(s); insured must sign; no premium charge. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.10)

**Transfer and assignment**: policy may be endorsed to effect location transfer (same state) or assignment on title transfer; adjust premium accordingly. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.12)

**Cancellation and reduction**: mandatory coverages may not be canceled without canceling entire policy; return premiums calculated pro rata. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.13)

**Contributing insurance**: property coverage may be divided between two or more companies under endorsement HO 6578; all companies must share same form/deductible/property endorsements; all liability assumed by one company. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §4.17)

**Additional and secondary locations:**
- Same state by endorsement: HO 6267 (note: flood endorsement HO 2710 unavailable when HO 6267 is attached)
- Same state by separate policy: liability may be deleted from additional location if covered on primary
- Other state: separate policy required; company must be licensed in that state
(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §12)
