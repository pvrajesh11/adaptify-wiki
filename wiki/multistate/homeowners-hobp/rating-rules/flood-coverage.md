# Homeowners HOBP — Multistate — Flood Coverage

Parent: [Homeowners HOBP — Multistate — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source: HO HOBP Multistate Flood Coverage Supplement Rev 06 21

---

## Overview

Optional flood coverage is available on eligible HOBP policies via endorsement HO 2710. This coverage
is separate from the base policy flood exclusion and is subject to its own deductible, limit structure,
and waiting period. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

---

## NFIP Disclaimer

This endorsement does not satisfy any mandatory flood insurance requirement imposed by a federally
regulated lender for a home mortgage or loan. It does not meet the requirements of the National Flood
Insurance Program (NFIP). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

---

## Eligible Policies

Flood coverage under HO 2710 is available only on new business or renewal policies issued with:
- HO 0001 (Basic Form)
- HO 0002 (Broad Form)
- HO 0003 (Special Form)
- HO 0005 (Special Building And Contents Form)

**Not eligible**: HO 0004 (Contents Broad Form) and HO 0006 (Unit-Owners Form).

**Additional restriction**: flood coverage is unavailable when an additional or secondary location is
added to the policy by endorsement HO 6267 (same-state additional location endorsement). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

---

## Flood Definition

Flood is defined as a general and temporary condition of partial or complete inundation of normally dry
land area resulting from: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

- an overflow of inland or tidal waters;
- an unusual and rapid accumulation or runoff of surface waters from any source, including but not limited
  to rainfall and ice melt; or
- mudflow.

**Excluded**: tsunami is expressly excluded from the flood definition.

---

## Waiting Period

There is no coverage for a flood occurrence that begins before, or within **360 hours (15 days)** after,
the flood coverage inception date shown in the endorsement's schedule. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

A limited exception applies — refer to the endorsement's Waiting Period condition for the terms of that
exception. ⚠️ The specific terms of the exception are stated in endorsement HO 2710 and not reproduced
in the supplement or this wiki entry.

---

## Limit Structure

### Principal Property Coverages

The following limits apply to all Principal Property Coverages (Coverages A, B, C, D as applicable to the
policy form): (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

- **Per flood occurrence limit**: caps loss, cost, and expense from any single flood occurrence.
- **Policy aggregate limit**: caps total loss, cost, and expense from all flood occurrences in the policy period.

### Coverage C — Basement Personal Property

A separate and distinct coverage occurrence limit and coverage aggregate limit apply. These are in addition
to, but subject to, the overall per flood occurrence limit and policy aggregate limit.

### Coverage D

A separate and distinct coverage occurrence limit and coverage aggregate limit apply. These are in addition
to, but subject to, the overall per flood occurrence limit and policy aggregate limit.

### Incidental Coverage — Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, Or Dry Rot

Added by the flood endorsement. A separate aggregate limit applies to the total of all loss, cost, or
expense caused by all flood occurrences.

### Limit Hierarchy

- Any applicable coverage occurrence limit is **part of, not in addition to**, the aggregate limit that
  applies to flood losses and the per flood occurrence limit.
- The aggregate limits for Coverage C — Basement Personal Property, Coverage D, and the Bacteria/Fungi/
  Wet Rot/Dry Rot Incidental Coverage are **part of, not in addition to**, the overall policy aggregate
  limit for flood losses.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

---

## Deductible

A special deductible provision applies to the total of all flood losses, **excluding** the Incidental
Property Coverage for Loss Avoidance Measures. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

Available deductible options are those shown in the rating information section of the flood supplement.
⚠️ Specific deductible amounts are in the filed rating information (rate tables), not reproduced here.

---

## Endorsement Requirements

Attach endorsement **HO 2710**. The following entries must be made in the endorsement schedule:

- Flood coverage inception date
- Deductible amount
- Per flood occurrence limit
- Policy aggregate limit
- As applicable: coverage occurrence limit for Coverage C — Basement Personal Property
- As applicable: coverage aggregate limit for Coverage C — Basement Personal Property
- As applicable: coverage occurrence limit for Coverage D
- As applicable: coverage aggregate limit for Coverage D
- Coverage aggregate limit for the Incidental Property Coverage for Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, Or Dry Rot

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

## Interaction With Base Policy

The flood endorsement adds optional flood coverage to the principal property coverages of the base policy.
The endorsement's Incidental Coverage for Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, Or Dry Rot is distinct from the
base policy's Limited Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, Or Dry Rot Coverage (Rule 9.20 / endorsement HO 2089).
⚠️ The interaction between the flood endorsement's bacteria/fungi coverage and the base policy's bacteria/
fungi coverage limit (if any) is not specified in the supplement and should be confirmed with the company.
