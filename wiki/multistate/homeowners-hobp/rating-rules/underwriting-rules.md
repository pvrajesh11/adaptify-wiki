# Homeowners HOBP — Multistate — Underwriting Rules

Parent: [Homeowners HOBP — Multistate — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source: MULTI HOBP 0111 §2

---

## Eligibility By Form (Rule 2)

### HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0008 (Rule 2.1)

Eligible insured: owner-occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling used only for private residential purposes
(except Rule 2.5). Each family unit must be occupied by no more than:
- one family with no more than two boarders or roomers; or
- two families with no boarders or roomers.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.1)

### HO 0004 — Contents Broad Form (Rule 2.2)

Eligible insureds:
- Tenant (non-owner) of a dwelling or apartment; or
- Owner-occupant of a dwelling, condo unit, co-op unit, or building not eligible under Rules 2.1 or 2.3.

Dwelling/apartment/unit must be:
- used only for residential purposes (except Rule 2.5); and
- occupied by no more than one family + two boarders/roomers or two families with no boarders/roomers.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.2)

### HO 0006 — Unit-Owners Form (Rule 2.3)

Eligible insured: owner-occupant of a condominium or co-operative unit used only for residential purposes
(except Rule 2.5). Unit occupied by no more than one family + two boarders/roomers or two families with
no boarders/roomers. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.3)

## Special Eligibility Situations

### Co-owner Occupancy (Rule 2.4)

A policy may be issued to one co-owner of a 2–4 family dwelling when each co-owner occupies a separate
family unit within the dwelling. Dwelling must be used only for private residential purposes (except
Rule 2.5). Use forms HO 0001/0002/0003/0005/0008.

Other co-owner's interest in the dwelling, related private structures, and premises liability may be
covered by the additional insured endorsement (HO 6041, per Rule 4.9.1). A separate HO 0004 policy may
be issued to non-occupant co-owner(s) for personal property, additional living costs, and personal
liability. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.4)

### Incidental Business Occupancies (Rule 2.5)

An insured may conduct certain business activities on the described location without affecting eligibility,
provided the premises is occupied principally for private residential purposes. Refer to company for
eligible occupancies. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.5)

### Other Occupancies (Rule 2.6)

At company option, a policy may be issued to:
- An occupant under a life estate arrangement; or
- An occupant purchasing a dwelling under a long-term contract (seller retaining title must not act as mortgagee).

Dwelling must be used only for private residential purposes (except Rule 2.5); each unit occupied by no
more than one family + two boarders/roomers or two families. Use forms HO 0001/0002/0003/0005/0008.

Owner's/seller's interest may be covered by endorsement HO 6041 (Rule 4.9.1). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.6)

### Dwellings Under Construction (Rule 2.7)

A policy may be issued to the intended owner-occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling under construction.
Intended use must be private residential (except Rule 2.5). Use forms HO 0001/0002/0003/0005/0008.
Refer to company for additional guidelines. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.7)

### Seasonal Dwellings (Rule 2.8)

A policy may be issued to cover a seasonal dwelling that otherwise meets eligibility requirements.
Indicate seasonal occupancy on the declarations. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.8)

## Ineligible Risks (Rule 2.9)

The following risks are ineligible for coverage under this manual:

- Mobile homes, trailer homes, and house trailers — whether or not set on foundations or otherwise made stationary.
- Property to which farm forms or rates apply — except as permitted in Rules 9.13 (Livestock Collision) and 11 (Farm Liability Coverages).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §2.9)

## Occupancy and Use Requirements Summary

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Maximum occupancy per unit | One family + two boarders/roomers, or two families with no boarders/roomers |
| Principal use | Private residential purposes (business incidental per Rule 2.5 permitted) |
| Owner-occupancy | Required for HO 0001/0002/0003/0005/0008 (except co-owner, life estate, and long-term contract situations) |
| Tenant eligibility | HO 0004 only |
| Condo/co-op unit | HO 0006 (owner-occupant) or HO 0004 (tenant/owner not eligible for HO 0006) |

## Property Restrictions

There are no explicit construction type restrictions stated in the multistate manual beyond the
construction classification definitions (which determine rating). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §Construction Definitions)

Key property characteristics that affect rating (not eligibility per se):
- Construction type (frame, masonry, concrete, log, steel — affects relativities)
- External cladding type (affects relativities)
- Roof covering type (affects relativities)
- Fire protection classification (Protected/Partially Protected/Unprotected — affects relativities)
- Solid fuel burning appliance (surcharge, company eligibility review required)
- Swimming pool (surcharge, company safety review required)
- Trampolines (surcharge, company safety review required)

For risk characteristic surcharge factors that apply to rated risks, see [Rating Factors](rating-factors.md).
