# Motor Truck Cargo — Multistate — Underwriting Rules

Parent: [Motor Truck Cargo — Multistate — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source: CIM MTC MU RTG REV 04 18

---

## Rating Method Eligibility

The rating method applied is determined by insured size, not by underwriting choice. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Per Vehicle / Gross Receipts headers)

| Condition | Method |
|---|---|
| Gross receipts ≤ $500,000 OR fewer than 10 power units | Per Vehicle (Method A) |
| Gross receipts > $500,000 OR 10+ power units | Gross Receipts (Method B) |

---

## Owner-Operator Use

The extent of owner-operator use is an underwriting factor evaluated in the basic load determination for both Method A and Method B. Three tiers are defined: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 2.A / Step 2.B)

1. **No owner-operators** — lowest risk tier
2. **Limited use of owner-operators** — background checks on drivers conducted; vehicles inspected
3. **Extensive use of owner-operators with limited supervision** — highest risk tier

---

## Fleet Condition

Fleet condition is evaluated in the basic load determination. Three tiers: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 2.A / Step 2.B)

1. **Newer vehicles with regular, documented inspection** — lowest risk tier
2. **Mix of newer and older vehicles with irregular inspection** — mid tier
3. **Older fleet with poor documentation of inspections** — highest risk tier

---

## Financial Stability

Insured financial stability is evaluated in the basic load determination, based on review of a financial statement or Central Analysis Bureau (CAB) report. Three tiers: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 2.A / Step 2.B)

1. **Excellent**
2. **Good**
3. **Poor**

Note: Method B (Gross Receipts) does not include financial stability as a separate factor in the published step; Method A explicitly lists it. Both reference the same underlying risk features.

---

## Vehicle Protection

Vehicle protection is evaluated in the basic load determination for cargo (Methods A and B) and in mobile/trailer coverages (Methods C and D). Categories: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 2.A)

1. Guards or guards in following vehicle
2. Vehicle alarms and/or tracking equipment (e.g., satellite, black box)
3. No additional protection

For mobile equipment and trailers: secured/fenced lot when not in use qualifies for the better tier.

---

## Subcontractor / Contingent Cargo Underwriting

When contingent coverage is considered, the following factors are evaluated: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 4.A / Step 4.B)

- Whether subcontractor shipments are incidental to, or a substantial part of, the insured's operations
- Whether the insured is named as additional named insured on the subcontractor's policy
- Whether a written brokerage agreement with a hold harmless provision is in place
- Nature of relationship: established (long-term) vs. short-term

These factors drive the contingent coverage modification (0.90–1.25). Favorable terms on all four factors support the lower end of the range.

---

## Target/Hazardous Cargo (Class 5)

When any commodity in the shipment is classified as Class 5 in the Commodity Classification Index, a Target/Hazardous Cargo Modification (1.25–2.00) is required. Factors evaluated: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 6.A / Step 6.B)

- **Over-the-road exposure:** Is driver with vehicle at all times? Is overnight parking in a fenced, secured area?
- **Protection of shipments:** Guards or escort vehicle; vehicle alarms/tracking; or no additional protection
- **Pickup and drop-off locations:** Low-risk area vs. high-risk area (examples: ports, high-crime locality)

Adulterated food coverage for Class 5 food commodities is not rated via the standard schedule — refer to company.

---

## Prohibited Risks / Refer to Company

- Adulterated food coverage: refer to company (no standard rating schedule)
- No other explicit prohibited risk list in the manual; Class 5 cargo requires the target/hazardous modification rather than declination
