# Motor Truck Cargo — Multistate — Coverage Modifications

Parent: [Motor Truck Cargo — Multistate — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source: CIM MTC MU RTG REV 04 18

---

## Named Perils Endorsement (IM 7488)

When the Named Perils Endorsement is written, apply a modification of **0.90** to the premium. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 11.A / Step 11.B)

This endorsement narrows coverage from open perils to named perils.

---

## Refrigeration Breakdown Coverage

Optional coverage added to cargo premium when provided. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 3.A / Step 3.B)

Risk factors evaluated to determine the load:
- Distance traveled: Local (≤50 mi), Intermediate (51–200 mi), Long Haul (>200 mi)
- Temperature alarms: installed on vehicles vs. not installed
- Cold storage contingency: insured has contracts with cold storage warehouses; drivers have maps of cold storage warehouses; no contingency plan

Load (Method A — per vehicle): 200–350 per vehicle
Load (Method B — gross receipts): 0.10–0.15 per $100 gross receipts

**Increased Deductible for Refrigeration Breakdown** — when a separate increased deductible is written for refrigeration breakdown only:

| Deductible Amount | Modification |
|---|---|
| $2,500 | 0.95 |
| $5,000 and over | 0.90 |

The refrigeration breakdown deductible modification applies only to the refrigeration breakdown load before it is added to the basic load.

## Contingent Coverage Modification

Optional; applies when coverage is provided for cargo in the care, custody, and control of a subcontractor. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 4.A / Step 4.B)

Modification range: **0.90–1.25**

Risk factors:
- Subcontractor shipments are incidental vs. substantial part of operations
- Insured is named as additional named insured on subcontractor's policy (yes/no)
- Written brokerage agreement with hold harmless provision (yes/no)
- Established long-term relationship vs. short-term relationship with subcontractor

The modification is applied to the basic load or modified load before multiplying by number of vehicles (Method A) or gross receipts (Method B).

---

## Radius of Operations Modification (Long Haul — Additional Premium)

When long haul coverage includes exposures not contemplated in the basic load, an additional premium modification applies. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 8.A / Step 8.B)

Modification range: **0.05–0.50** (applied as an additional premium factor)

Risk factors:
- Distance: 200–500 miles; 501–1,000 miles; over 1,000 miles
- Vehicle data availability: satellite tracking or black box present vs. not available
- Hours driven: documented vs. not documented

The additional premium is calculated by multiplying the result of Step 7 by this modification, then adding to the Step 7 result (not replacing it).

---

## Terminal Modification (Additional Premium)

When terminal coverage includes exposures not contemplated in the basic load, an additional premium applies. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 9.A / Step 9.B)

Modification range: **0.05–0.30** (applied as an additional premium factor)

Risk factors:
- Construction: fire resistive; non-combustible; frame
- Terminal limit vs. vehicle limit: same vs. higher than vehicle limit
- Special occupancy: terminal used for hazardous materials storage (yes/no)
- Disaster exposure: area prone to disasters (e.g., hurricane, brush fire) vs. not

The additional premium is calculated by multiplying the result of Step 7 by this modification, then adding to the Step 7 result.

## Deductible Modifications

Standard deductible modifications apply to the cargo premium (both Method A and Method B): (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 12.A / Step 12.B)

| Deductible Amount | Modification |
|---|---|
| $500 | 1.10 |
| $2,500 | 0.90 |
| $5,000 | 0.80 |

Note: Refrigeration breakdown has a separate deductible modification (see above) that applies before adding to the basic load.

## Individual Risk Premium Modification (IRPM)

An IRPM may be applied as the final step of the rating procedure for both Method A (Step 13.A) and Method B (Step 13.B), and for mobile/electronic equipment (Step 4.C) and trailer coverage (Step 4.D). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 13.A / Step 13.B / Step 4.C / Step 4.D)

No numerical IRPM range is specified in this manual — the IRPM schedule is a separate rating item.

## Adulterated Food Coverage

If adulterated food coverage is provided: **Refer to Company.** No standard rating procedure is defined in this manual. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Adulterated Food Coverage)
