## Base Coverage Forms

Motor Truck Cargo coverage is written under IM 7450. The base form includes built-in sublimits of $5,000 each for moving equipment and electronic equipment (off-board and on-board). These built-in limits serve as the premium base for those coverages unless limits are changed. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Method C)

The Named Perils Endorsement (IM 7488) narrows coverage from open perils to named perils and applies a 0.90 premium modification. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Step 11.A / Step 11.B)

## Rating Method Selection

Two primary methods exist, selected based on insured size: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Per Vehicle / Gross Receipts headers)

**Per Vehicle Premium Determination (Method A)**
Use when: gross receipts ≤ $500,000 OR fewer than 10 power units.
Premium base: per vehicle limit × number of vehicles.

**Gross Receipts Premium Determination (Method B)**
Use when: gross receipts > $500,000 OR 10 or more power units.
Premium base: insured's annual gross receipts.

## Reporting Form Rules

When reporting conditions are part of the MTC coverage form: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_MULTI_manual_v04-18 §Reporting Form)

- Reporting period may be monthly, quarterly, or annual
- Premium adjustment period may be monthly, quarterly, or annual
- Gross Receipts or Per Vehicle premium can be converted to another reporting base (e.g., sales, mileage)

**Gross Receipts base + Gross Receipts reporting:**
1. Determine premium per Gross Receipts Premium Determination
2. Divide premium by annual gross receipts (per $100) to obtain gross receipts reporting rate
3. Apply rate per $100 to reported gross receipts each period

**Per Vehicle base + Gross Receipts reporting:**
1. Determine annual premium per Per Vehicle Premium Determination
2. Divide by annual gross receipts (per $100) to obtain gross receipts reporting rate
3. Apply rate per $100 to reported gross receipts each period

**Per Vehicle base + Per Vehicle reporting (monthly):**
1. Determine per vehicle premium per Per Vehicle Premium Determination
2. Divide by 12 to obtain monthly per vehicle premium
3. Apply monthly premium to each vehicle listed in the report
4. Sum vehicle premiums for monthly earned premium
