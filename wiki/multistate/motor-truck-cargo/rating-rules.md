## Known State Overrides

| Topic | Multistate Rule | AK | AL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Criminal acts exclusion | No exception | Domestic violence exception for innocent insureds (AS 18.66) — CIM 2003 06 20 | Abuse exception for innocent insureds (Alabama Protection From Abuse Act) — CIM 2001 08 15 |
| Subrogation | Standard | Innocent insured subject to DV cannot waive recovery right — CIM 2003 06 20 | Same (abuse context) — CIM 2001 08 15 |
| Suit Against Us | Fixed contractual period | Replaced with Alaska statute of limitations — CIM 2003 06 20 | Replaced with "time period prescribed by law" (most forms) — CIM 2001 08 15 |
