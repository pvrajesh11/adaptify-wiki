# Uninsured Motorist Coverage

Uninsured Motorist (UM) Coverage pays for compensatory damages that an insured is legally entitled to recover because of bodily injury caused by the owner or operator of an uninsured motor vehicle.

## Alaska (Auto PPA)

**Mandatory offering:** UM is required when PA 0001 covers liability exposures on the declarations. The named insured must reject coverage in writing to waive it. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.4)

**Stacking election required:** When UM is not rejected, the named insured must elect between stacked and non-stacked:
- Non-Stacked → PA 2137
- Stacked → PA 2161

**Combined Single Limit option:**
- Non-Stacked CSL → PA 0737
- Stacked CSL → PA 0760

Limits apply per person / per accident. Available limits: refer to company.
