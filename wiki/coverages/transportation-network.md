# Transportation Network / Ride Sharing Coverage

Transportation Network coverage provides auto coverage for drivers operating vehicles through rideshare platforms (e.g., Uber, Lyft). Coverage is structured across three phases based on the driver's status at the time of a loss.

## Alaska (Auto PPA) — Act 164

Alaska's **Act 164** mandates minimum coverage levels for transportation network drivers across all three phases. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §7.3.2)

The policy maintained by the transportation network driver may provide some or all of the required amounts. If the policy provides coverage, it must be at least equal to Alaska's financial responsibility minimums.

### Phase I — Logged In, No Accepted Request, No Passenger
Driver is logged into the transportation network app but has not yet accepted a ride request.

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Bodily Injury Liability | $50,000/person, $100,000/accident |
| Property Damage Liability | $25,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Endorsement: **PA 6602** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Accepted Passenger

### Phase II — Accepted Request, No Passenger Occupying Vehicle
Driver has accepted a ride request but the passenger has not yet entered the vehicle.

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Liability (BI & PD combined) | $500,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Endorsement: **PA 6603** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Passenger

### Phase III — Passenger Occupying the Vehicle
Passenger is in the vehicle.

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Liability (BI & PD combined) | $500,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (passengers & pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Endorsement: **PA 6604** — Transportation Network Program Coverage

### Premium Determination (all phases)
Primary use classification (Rule 7.3.1) plus applicable ride-sharing use factor from state pages.
