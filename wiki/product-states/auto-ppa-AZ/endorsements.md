## Mandatory Endorsements

| Form | Title | Bureau |
|---|---|---|
| PA 0102 07 18 | Amendatory Endorsement — Arizona | AAIS |

The AAIS Arizona amendatory endorsement is the only AZ-specific endorsement on file. It amends the base AAIS Personal Automobile Policy (PA 0001 07 16) across Definitions, Part A (Liability), Part B (Medical Payments), Part D (Physical Damage), and Policy Conditions. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18)

See form page: [PA 0102 07 18](../../forms/PA-0102-07-18.md)

## Optional Endorsements

No state rating manual is on file for Arizona Auto PPA. Optional endorsements (UM/UIM stacking, transportation network coverage forms, named driver exclusion, etc.) are not enumerated here and would be added when an AZ Auto PPA state manual is ingested.

## Endorsement-Driven Substantive Rules

### Definitions
- **Replacement Auto** — coverage rules vary by coverage type: 14-day request window when at least one declarations auto carries Collision/Comprehensive, otherwise a 4-day window with a $500 deductible. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Definitions)

### Part A — Liability
- **TNC / Public-or-Livery exclusion** with shared-expense carpool and ARS § 43-1201, A.4 volunteer carve-outs. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.1.f)
- **Delivery-in-commerce exclusion** with ARS § 43-1201, A.4 volunteer carve-out only. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.1.g)
- **Intra-family BI exclusion** applies only above the Arizona Financial Responsibility Law minimum. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A new exclusion, §Part A.2.d)
- **Insurance Under More Than One Policy** — pro-rata sharing; non-owned vehicle coverage primary when that vehicle is insured under an auto-business policy and the operating insured is not the auto-business person/employee/agent. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A — How Much We Pay)

### Part B — Medical Payments
- **TNC / public-or-livery in covered auto** (Exclusion 2.e.) — shared-expense carpool and ARS § 43-1201, A.4 carve-outs. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part B.2.e)
- **Delivery-in-commerce in covered auto** (Exclusion 2.f.) — ARS § 43-1201, A.4 carve-out only. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part B.2.f)

### Part D — Physical Damage
- **TNC / public-or-livery** (Exclusion 7) and **delivery-in-commerce** (Exclusion 8) apply to both covered auto and non-owned auto, with the same carve-out structure. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part D.7, §Part D.8)

### Policy Conditions
- **Assignment** replaced; on named-insured death, surviving spouse (resident) treated as named insured, legal representative covered for maintenance/use only. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Assignment)
- **Recoveries (Part B)** replaced; lien/notice procedure when company payment exceeds $5,000 and recipient recovers from another source. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Recoveries)
- **Termination** — adds 7-day premium grace, Automatic Termination, Cancellation (first 60 days any reason / after 60 days seven enumerated grounds), 45-day Nonrenewal notice, Other Termination Conditions (no cancellation/nonrenewal solely on protected characteristics or residence location), and Offer To Renew procedure. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Termination)
