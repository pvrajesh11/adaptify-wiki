## Joint Ownership `[AL-specific]`

A policy may cover multiple owners of a described vehicle, including: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §4.9)
- Two or more persons not married who reside in the same household
- Two or more persons not in the same household who are related by blood, marriage, or adoption

Attach: **PA 1237** — Joint Ownership Coverage - Alabama
Premium: No charge or credit applies.

---

## Named Driver Exclusion `[AL-specific]`

A policy may exclude any or all coverages for any named driver(s). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §10.5)

Attach: **PA 4324** — Named Driver Exclusion - Alabama
Premium: Excluded driver is not included in premium determination (Rule 5).

## Transportation Network / Ride Sharing Coverage `[AL-specific]`

Governed by **Act 164** (Alabama). Minimum coverage levels mandated across three phases. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §7.3.2)

### Phase I — Logged in, no accepted request, no passenger

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Bodily Injury Liability | $50,000/person, $100,000/accident |
| Property Damage Liability | $25,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Attach: **PA 6602** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Accepted Passenger

### Phase II — Accepted request, no passenger in vehicle

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Liability (BI & PD combined) | $500,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Attach: **PA 6603** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Passenger

### Phase III — Passenger occupying the vehicle

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Liability (BI & PD combined) | $500,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (passengers & pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Attach: **PA 6604** — Transportation Network Program Coverage

Premium (all phases): primary use classification (Rule 7.3.1) + applicable ride-sharing use factor from state pages.

The policy may provide some or all required amounts; if provided, must be at least equal to Alabama's financial responsibility minimums.

## Abuse — Physical Damage Exception `[AL-specific]`

Applied via endorsement **PPA 0101 03 18** (Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §1)

The Part D intentional acts exclusion does **not** apply to an innocent insured who suffers a covered loss caused by an act of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute) committed by or at the direction of another insured, provided the innocent insured:
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Files a complaint under the **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** against the abuser and does not voluntarily dismiss the complaint, OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest, less any payment to a mortgagee or party with a legal secured interest.

**Subrogation:** An innocent insured who is the subject of abuse cannot waive subrogation rights. Company retains all rights to recover up to amounts paid. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §2)

## Renewal Notice Requirement `[AL-specific]`

Applied via endorsement **PPA 0101 03 18**. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §3)

If the company intends to renew the policy subject to a premium increase (other than an increase due to a change at the named insured's request), the company must give the named insured at least **30 days' prior notice** before the renewal effective date.

---

## Suit Against Us — Alabama Statute of Limitations `[AL-specific]`

Applied via endorsement **PPA 0101 03 18**. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §4)

No suit may be brought against the company unless all applicable property coverage terms have been complied with AND the suit is brought within the time period **prescribed by law** for commencement of such action. (Base form uses a fixed contractual time period; AL endorsement substitutes the applicable statute of limitations under Alabama law.)
