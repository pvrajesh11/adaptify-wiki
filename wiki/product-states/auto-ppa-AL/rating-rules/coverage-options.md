# Auto PPA — Alabama — Coverage Options

Parent: [Auto PPA — Alabama — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source: PAU AL MAN-1.5 §3.1, §3.4, §3.7, §3.8, §3.9, §3.10, §5.4
Multistate base: [Auto PPA — Multistate — Coverage Options](../../../multistate/auto-ppa/rating-rules/coverage-options.md)

---

## Policy Form

Base form: **PA 0001 — Personal Auto Policy**

Available coverage combinations: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §3.1)
- Liability only
- Liability + Medical Payments
- Liability + Uninsured Motorist
- Liability + Medical Payments + Uninsured Motorist
- Physical Damage (with or without liability and/or other coverages)

A separate premium is charged for each vehicle and each coverage on the declarations.

---

## Uninsured Motorist Coverage `[AL-specific]`

UM Coverage is **mandatory** when PA 0001 covers liability exposures, unless the named insured rejects in writing. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §3.4)

When not rejected, named insured must elect stacked or non-stacked:
- **Non-Stacked** → attach PA 2137 — Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Non-Stacked - Alabama
- **Stacked** → attach PA 2161 — Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Stacked - Alabama

Combined Single Limit option:
- Non-Stacked CSL → attach PA 0737
- Stacked CSL → attach PA 0760

Limits: per person / per accident basis. Available limits: refer to company.

---

## Underinsured Motorist Coverage `[AL-specific]`

UIM Coverage is **mandatory** when PA 0001 covers liability exposures, unless the named insured rejects in writing. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §3.7)

When not rejected, named insured must elect stacked or non-stacked:
- **Non-Stacked** → attach PA 2237 — Underinsured Motorist Coverage — Non-Stacked - Alabama
- **Stacked** → attach PA 2256 — Underinsured Motorist Coverage — Stacked - Alabama

Combined Single Limit option:
- Non-Stacked CSL → attach PA 0837
- Stacked CSL → attach PA 0856

---

## First Party Benefits Coverage `[AL-specific]`

Three tiers at named insured's option: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §3.8)

| Tier | Benefits Included | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Medical expenses only | PA 2637 |
| Additional | Medical expenses, loss of income, funeral expenses, accidental death | PA 2637 |
| Combination | Same as Additional with higher limits; subject to total benefits maximum | PA 2637 |

## Extraordinary Medical Benefits Coverage `[AL-specific]`

Covers medical expenses exceeding $100,000. Attach **PA 3005**. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §3.9)

## Pedestrian Basic First Party Benefits Coverage `[AL-specific]`

Covers medical expenses from vehicles excluded under First Party Benefits Coverage. Attach **PA 3006**. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §3.10)

---

## Limited Tort Option `[AL-specific]`

Each insured must be given a written choice between: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §5.4)

| Option | Description | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Full Tort (default) | Unrestricted right to sue for all damages | Base rates apply |
| Limited Tort | Gives up right to sue for non-economic damages unless serious injury or exception | Limited Tort Factor applied at Step 1.b. |

Attach: **PA 9902** — Limited Tort Alternative Information Notice - Alabama (also a mandatory endorsement).
