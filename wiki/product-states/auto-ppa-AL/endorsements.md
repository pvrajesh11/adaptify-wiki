Source manual: PAU AL MAN-1.5 — Final
See also: [AL — Rating Rules](rating-rules.md) | [Alabama (AL)](../../states/AL.md)

## Mandatory Endorsements

All policies issued in Alabama must include the following: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §4.2)

| Form | Title |
|---|---|
| PA 0101 | Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama |
| PA 9901 | Insurance Consultation Services Exemption Act Notice |
| PA 9902 | Limited Tort Alternative Information Notice — Alabama |

See form page: [PPA 0101 03 18](../../forms/PPA-0101-03-18.md)

|---|---|---|
| PA 2137 | Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Non-Stacked — Alabama | When named insured elects non-stacked UM |

| PA 0737 | Combined Single Limit — UM — Non-Stacked — Alabama | When CSL option selected with non-stacked UM |
| PA 0760 | Combined Single Limit — UM — Stacked — Alabama | When CSL option selected with stacked UM |
