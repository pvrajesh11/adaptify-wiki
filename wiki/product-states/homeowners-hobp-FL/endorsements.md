## Mandatory Endorsements `[FL-specific]`

The following endorsements are mandatory in Florida (Rule 4.2). Those referencing a form number in their title attach only when the specified form is used; all others attach to every policy.

| Endorsement | Description | Attaches To | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HO 0809 02 22** | Amendatory Endorsement — Florida | Every policy | Innocent insured/abuse; subrogation carve-out; renewal notice; suit period. See [HO-0809-02-22](../../forms/HO-0809-02-22.md) |
| **HO 0814** | Amendatory Endorsement — Florida (prior version) | Every policy | Listed in state pages Rev 01 15; may have been superseded by HO 0809 02 22 in 2026 filing |
| **HO 1300** | Asbestos Exclusion | Every policy | — |
| **HO 1301** | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0001 | HO 0001 policies | — |
| **HO 1302** | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0002 | HO 0002 policies | — |
| **HO 1303** | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0003 | HO 0003 policies | — |
| **HO 1304** | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0004 | HO 0004 policies | — |
| **HO 1305** | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0005 | HO 0005 policies | — |
| **HO 1306** | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0006 | HO 0006 policies | — |
| **HO 1308** | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0008 | HO 0008 policies | — |
| **HO 1323** | Liability Exclusions — Lead | Every policy | — |
| **HO 1335** | Liability Exclusions — Pollutants, Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, And Dry Rot | Every policy | — |
| **HO 1347** | Punitive Or Exemplary Damages Exclusion | Every policy | — |
| **HO 4505** | Windstorm Or Hail Deductible | Every policy | Required; percentage deductible on Coverage A/C; see multistate rating-factors |

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §4.2)

## FL-Specific Optional Endorsements

| Endorsement | Description | Rule | Key Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HO 2002** | Cosmetic Damage Exclusion | 8.11 | Excludes cosmetic wind/hail damage to exterior wall/roof/door/window surfacing; specify surfacing types; door and window must apply together |
| **HO 2003** | Owner Occupancy Requirement | 4.19 | At company option; excludes coverage 61+ days after named insured moves out (other than temporarily); not permitted for construction/seasonal/additional location/rented condo |
| **HO 4836** | Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV All Perils | 8.10 | Converts roof surfacing to ACV for all covered perils; not permitted with ACV or functional RC endorsements for Cov A/B |

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §4.19, §8.10, §8.11)
