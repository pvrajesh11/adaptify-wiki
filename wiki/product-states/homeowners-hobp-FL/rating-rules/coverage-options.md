## Policy Forms `[multistate, unchanged]`

Florida uses the standard multistate policy forms (HO 0001, 0002, 0003, 0004, 0005, 0006, 0008). No FL-specific forms restrictions.

The principal policy form filed in Florida is HO 0003 09 20 (Special Form, edition September 2020). See [HO-0003-09-20](../../../forms/HO-0003-09-20.md).

## Intentional Acts Exclusion — Innocent Insured Exception `[FL override]`

Under Exclusions That Apply To Property Coverages, the Intentional Acts exclusion is amended by HO 0809 02 22 (mandatory in Florida):

The exclusion does **not** apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by an insured who:
- Is the subject of **abuse, as defined by Florida statute**, committed by or at the direction of another insured, AND
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Either files a complaint under the **Florida Protection From Abuse Act** (without voluntary dismissal) OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment may be limited to that insured's insurable interest less any mortgagee or secured interest payment.

**Subrogation:** The innocent insured who is the subject of abuse cannot waive subrogation rights. Company retains all recovery rights up to the amount paid.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22 §1, §2)

## Renewal Notice Requirement `[FL override]`

Added by HO 0809 02 22. If the company intends to renew the policy subject to a premium increase (other than an increase due to a change at the named insured's request), the company must give the named insured at least **30 days' prior notice** before the renewal effective date.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22 §3)
