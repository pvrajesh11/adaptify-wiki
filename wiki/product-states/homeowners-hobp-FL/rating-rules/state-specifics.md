## Claim Free Discount — Catastrophic Claim Exclusion `[FL override]`

**Rule 6.9** is overridden. The first paragraph of the multistate rule is replaced.

A policy is claim free if, during the three-year period just before the date of new application or renewal preparation, all adults insured under the policy had Homeowners or equivalent coverage in force and no claims were paid under such coverage — with one additional Florida exclusion:

**Catastrophic claims are excluded from the claims history count.** A catastrophic claim is one that:
- Resulted from an event that caused **$25 million or more** in total insured property losses, AND
- Affected a **significant number** of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers

Such claims are not considered when determining if a policy is claim free.

All other aspects of the Claim Free Discount (three-year period, no-dollar-amount threshold, all causes of loss) follow multistate rules.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §6.9)

## Risk Group Assignment — Catastrophic Claim Exclusion `[FL override]`

**Rule 6.11** is replaced in its entirety. The Florida rule matches the multistate rule in all respects, with one addition:

**Catastrophic claims are excluded from claims history.** A claim is excluded from the risk group assignment claims history if it resulted from an event that caused **$25 million or more** in total insured property losses and affected a significant number of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers.

This applies in addition to the existing multistate exclusions (mine subsidence, earthquake, sinkhole collapse, scheduled personal property only, medical payments only).

**Risk Group Matrix (Major claims × Minor claims → Risk Group):**

| Minor \ Major | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | G | I | K | M | O | Q | R |
| 1 | H | J | L | N | P | R | S |
| 2 | I | K | M | O | Q | S | T |
| 3 | J | L | N | P | R | T | U |
| 4 | K | M | O | Q | S | U | V |
| 5 | L | N | P | R | T | V | W |
| 6+ | M | O | Q | S | U | W | X |

All other aspects: 3-year experience period; $500 minimum payment threshold (net of subrogation); major claims = fire/liability/theft/vandalism; minor claims = all others; information sources from application/database/company records; if actual history merits lesser premium, refund required.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §6.11)

## Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV For All Covered Perils (Rule 8.10) `[FL-specific]`

**Not in multistate.** Florida adds an option to convert roof surfacing loss settlement from replacement cost to actual cash value for **all covered property losses** (as opposed to the multistate windstorm/hail-only option under Rule 8.9 / HO 4835).

**Applies to:** HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006.

**Not permitted** when the policy includes:
- Actual cash value loss settlement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.1 / HO 4815)
- Functional replacement cost for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.2 / HO 4857)

Premium: Apply factor from state pages per peril.
Endorsement: **HO 4836**

**Comparison to multistate:**
- Multistate Rule 8.9 (HO 4835): windstorm or hail only
- FL Rule 8.10 (HO 4836): all covered perils

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §8.10)

## Cosmetic Damage Exclusion (Rule 8.11) `[FL-specific]`

**Not in multistate.** Florida allows excluding coverage for **cosmetic damage** to covered building surfacing caused by windstorm or hail, when the damage consists solely of cosmetic damage.

**Eligible surfacing types:**
- Exterior wall surfacing
- Roof surfacing
- Exterior door surfacing AND exterior window surfacing (must apply to both types together; cannot be applied separately)

**Premium:** Apply factor from state pages per peril. For premium purposes, exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing are treated as one surfacing type.

**Endorsement:** HO 2002, with an entry or entries indicating the specific surfacing to which the exclusion applies.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §8.11)

## Suit Against Us — Property Coverages `[FL override]`

Applied via mandatory endorsement HO 0809 02 22.

The base policy Suit Against Us condition for property coverages (two-year contractual period) is **deleted and replaced**:

> No suit may be brought against the company unless all of the terms that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with and the suit is brought within the **time period prescribed by law** for the commencement of such action.

This removes the base policy's two-year contractual period. The applicable period is now governed by Florida law (Florida Statute §95.11 — five years for written contracts).

**Note:** The Suit Against Us condition for liability coverages is not amended by this endorsement.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22 §4)
