# Homeowners HOBP — Florida — Underwriting Rules

Parent: [Homeowners HOBP — Florida — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)

---

## Eligibility By Form `[multistate, unchanged]`

Florida follows multistate eligibility rules for all forms (HO 0001/0002/0003/0005/0008: owner-occupant of 1–4 family dwelling; HO 0004: tenant or ineligible owner; HO 0006: condo/co-op owner-occupant). Occupancy limits, co-owner occupancy, incidental business occupancies, other occupancies, dwellings under construction, and seasonal dwellings all per multistate rules. See [Multistate — Underwriting Rules](../../../multistate/homeowners-hobp/rating-rules/underwriting-rules.md).

---

## Ineligible Risks `[multistate, unchanged]`

Mobile homes, trailer homes, and house trailers; property to which farm forms or rates apply (except livestock collision and farm liability) — per multistate Rule 2.9.

---

## Owner Occupancy Requirement — Rule 4.19 `[FL-specific]`

Added in revision 12 13. Applies to: HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006, HO 0008.

**Option available at company discretion:** A policy issued to any of the following persons may be endorsed to exclude coverage for loss to property occurring **61 days or more** after the date the named insured begins living at a location other than the described location on other than a temporary basis:

- Owner-occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling
- Owner-occupant of a condominium or co-operative unit
- Co-owner-occupant of a 2–4 family dwelling
- Occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling under a life estate arrangement
- Occupant purchasing a 1–4 family dwelling under a long-term contract

**Not permitted for:**
- Dwelling under construction
- Seasonal dwelling
- Additional or secondary location
- Condominium or co-operative unit regularly rented or held for rental to others for use as a residence

Premium: No charge or credit.
Endorsement: **HO 2003**
