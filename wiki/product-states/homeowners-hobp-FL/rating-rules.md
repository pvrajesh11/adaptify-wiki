## State Deviations from Multistate Base

The following summarizes how Florida differs from the multistate base. Full detail is in sub-pages.

| Topic | Multistate Rule | FL Override | Sub-Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandatory Endorsements (Rule 4.2) | No multistate mandatory endorsements specified | 14 mandatory endorsements in FL including HO 0814/0809, HO 1300–1308, HO 1323, HO 1335, HO 1347, HO 4505 [FL-specific] | coverage-options |
| Claim Free Discount (Rule 6.9) | No catastrophic claim exclusion | Catastrophic claims ($25M+ total insured losses, significant # of policyholders) excluded from claims history [FL override] | state-specifics |
| Risk Group Assignment (Rule 6.11) | No catastrophic claim exclusion | Same catastrophic claim exclusion added to risk group claims history [FL override] | state-specifics |
| Owner Occupancy Requirement (Rule 4.19) | Not in multistate | At company option: HO 2003 endorsement excludes coverage 61+ days after named insured moves out (on other than temporary basis) [FL-specific] | underwriting-rules |
| Roof Surfacing ACV — All Perils (Rule 8.10) | Windstorm/hail ACV option only (HO 4835) | Adds all-perils ACV option for roof surfacing (HO 4836) [FL-specific] | state-specifics |
| Cosmetic Damage Exclusion (Rule 8.11) | Not in multistate | Can exclude cosmetic damage to exterior wall/roof/door/window surfacing from windstorm or hail (HO 2002) [FL-specific] | state-specifics |
| Intentional Acts Exclusion | Applies to all insureds even non-involved | FL adds innocent insured exception for domestic abuse under Florida statute [FL override] | coverage-options |
| Suit Against Us — Property | Two-year contractual period (with statutory fallback) | Florida statutory period (contractual period removed) [FL override] | state-specifics |
| Renewal Notice | Not in base policy | 30 days' notice required before premium increase renewal [FL override] | state-specifics |
