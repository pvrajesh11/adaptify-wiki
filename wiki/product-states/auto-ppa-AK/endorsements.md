Source manual: PAU AK MAN-1.5 — Final
See also: [AK — Rating Rules](rating-rules.md) | [Alaska (AK)](../../states/AK.md)

## Mandatory Endorsements

All policies issued in Alaska must include the following: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §4.2)

| Form | Title |
|---|---|
| PA 0154 | Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska |
| PA 9901 | Insurance Consultation Services Exemption Act Notice |
| PA 9902 | Limited Tort Alternative Information Notice — Alaska |

|---|---|---|
