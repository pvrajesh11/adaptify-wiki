## Rules That Do Not Apply in Alaska

| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| Intra-Family Liability Exclusion (Rule 10.2) | Multistate rule **does not apply** in Alaska |
| Financial Responsibility Filing (Rule 6.4.2) | Rule **does not apply** in Alaska |

## Joint Ownership `[AK-specific]`

A policy may cover multiple owners of a described vehicle, including: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §4.9)
- Two or more persons not married who reside in the same household
- Two or more persons not in the same household who are related by blood, marriage, or adoption

Attach: **PA 1237** — Joint Ownership Coverage - Alaska
Premium: No charge or credit applies.

---

## Named Driver Exclusion `[AK-specific]`

A policy may exclude any or all coverages for any named driver(s). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §10.5)

Attach: **PA 4324** — Named Driver Exclusion - Alaska
Premium: Excluded driver is not included in premium determination (Rule 5).

## Transportation Network / Ride Sharing Coverage `[AK-specific]`

Governed by **Act 164** (Alaska). Minimum coverage levels mandated across three phases. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §7.3.2)

### Phase I — Logged in, no accepted request, no passenger

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Bodily Injury Liability | $50,000/person, $100,000/accident |
| Property Damage Liability | $25,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Attach: **PA 6602** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Accepted Passenger

### Phase II — Accepted request, no passenger in vehicle

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Liability (BI & PD combined) | $500,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Attach: **PA 6603** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Passenger

### Phase III — Passenger occupying the vehicle

| Coverage | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Liability (BI & PD combined) | $500,000 |
| First Party Medical (driver) | $5,000 |
| First Party Medical (passengers & pedestrians) | $25,000 |

Attach: **PA 6604** — Transportation Network Program Coverage

Premium (all phases): primary use classification (Rule 7.3.1) + applicable ride-sharing use factor from state pages.

The policy may provide some or all required amounts; if provided, must be at least equal to Alaska's financial responsibility minimums.

## Special Vehicle Classes `[AK-specific]`

### Miscellaneous Type Vehicles
Available coverages: Liability, Medical Payments, UM, Physical Damage.
Attach: **PA 8206** — Miscellaneous Type Vehicle Coverage - Alaska (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §11.1)

### Low Speed Vehicles
Available coverages: Liability, Medical Payments, UM, Physical Damage.
Attach: **PA 8214** — Low Speed Vehicle Coverage - Alaska (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §11.2)

### Farm Motor Vehicles
Available coverages: Liability, Medical Payments, UM, Physical Damage.
Attach: **PA 8236** — Farm Motor Vehicle Coverage - Alaska
Premium: Refer to company. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §11.5)
