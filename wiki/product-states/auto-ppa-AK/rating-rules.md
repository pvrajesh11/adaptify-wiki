# Auto PPA — Alaska — Rating Rules

Source manual: PAU AK MAN-1.5 — Final
See also: [[product-states/auto-ppa-AK/endorsements]] | [[states/AK]] | [[products/auto-ppa]]

---

## Policy Form

Base form: **PA 0001 — Personal Auto Policy**

Available coverage combinations:
- Liability only
- Liability + Medical Payments
- Liability + Uninsured Motorist
- Liability + Medical Payments + Uninsured Motorist
- Physical Damage (with or without liability and/or other coverages)

A separate premium is charged for each vehicle and each coverage on the declarations. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.1)

---

## Uninsured Motorist Coverage

UM Coverage is **mandatory** when PA 0001 covers liability exposures, unless the named insured rejects coverage in writing. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.4)

When UM is not rejected, the named insured must elect stacked or non-stacked:
- **Non-Stacked** — attach PA 2137
- **Stacked** — attach PA 2161

Combined Single Limit option available:
- Non-Stacked CSL — attach PA 0737
- Stacked CSL — attach PA 0760

Limits apply per person / per accident. Available limits: refer to company.

See [[coverages/uninsured-motorist]] for cross-state comparison.

---

## Underinsured Motorist Coverage

UIM Coverage is **mandatory** when PA 0001 covers liability exposures, unless the named insured rejects coverage in writing. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.7)

When UIM is not rejected, the named insured must elect stacked or non-stacked:
- **Non-Stacked** — attach PA 2237
- **Stacked** — attach PA 2256

Combined Single Limit option available:
- Non-Stacked CSL — attach PA 0837
- Stacked CSL — attach PA 0856

See [[coverages/underinsured-motorist]] for cross-state comparison.

## First Party Benefits Coverage

Three tiers available at named insured's option: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.8)

- **Basic** — medical expenses only → attach PA 2637
- **Additional** — medical expenses, loss of income, funeral expenses, accidental death → attach PA 2637
- **Combination** — same as Additional with higher limits, subject to total benefits maximum → attach PA 2637

---

## Extraordinary Medical Benefits Coverage

Covers medical expenses in excess of $100,000. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.9)
Attach: **PA 3005**

---

## Pedestrian Basic First Party Benefits Coverage

Covers medical expenses from vehicles excluded from First Party Benefits Coverage. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.10)
Attach: **PA 3006**

---

## Limited Tort Option

Each insured must be given the written choice between: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §5.4)

- **Full Tort** (default) — unrestricted right to sue for all damages; base rates apply
- **Limited Tort** — reduced premium; insured gives up right to sue for non-economic damages unless serious injury or applicable exception applies; Limited Tort Factor applied at Step 1.b. of Calculation of Premium

Attach: **PA 9902** — Limited Tort Alternative Information Notice - Alaska (also a mandatory endorsement)

## Driving Record Points

**Experience period:** 3 years immediately preceding policy inception or renewal date. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §6.4.1)

### Points Assigned For
- Convictions for motor vehicle violations
- At-fault accidents where a claim was paid by the company exceeding the approved dollar threshold (refer to company for threshold)

When multiple violations arise from one occurrence, use only the highest point-value violation.

A 2nd or subsequent violation in the experience period is assessed both the age-of-violation points AND the additional violation surcharge.

### Point Assignment Structure

| Classification | 0–<6 Mo | 0–<1 Yr | 1–<2 Yr | 2–<3 Yr | Add'l Violation Surcharge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speeding Violation (non-Major) | N/A | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Minor Violation (non-Speeding) | N/A | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Major Violation | N/A | 4 | 3 | 3 | 1 |
| At-Fault Accident | N/A | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| No Prior Insurance | 4 | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A |

Violation classification follows the AAMVA Code Dictionary.

### Additional Points
- **4 points** — lapse in mandatory/compulsory insurance of 30+ days immediately preceding policy inception, unless:
  - Driver did not own a vehicle requiring mandatory coverage during that period, OR
  - Lapse was due to active military service or deployment
- **1 point** — conviction not otherwise classified that results in license suspension or revocation

### Points NOT Assigned For
- Violations/accidents for a driver who is the named insured of a separate policy or the principal driver of a vehicle on a separate policy
- Non-moving violations (e.g., equipment violations)
- A single conviction under 75 Pa. C.S. § 1535 during the experience period *(flagged: possible PA statute artifact — verify AK applicability)*
- Two or more convictions under 75 Pa. C.S. § 1535 unless committed by the same insured driver
- At-fault accidents eliminated by Accident Forgiveness (Rule 4.7.4)
- Accidents where the insured auto was lawfully parked and stationary *(if a parked auto rolls and causes an accident, points are assigned to the driver who parked it)*
- Accidents where the insured driver is reimbursed by or has a judgment against another person determined responsible
- Accidents covered under UM or UIM coverage
- Accidents where damage is covered under Comprehensive Coverage
- Rear-end collisions where the insured auto was struck from behind and the insured driver had no moving violation conviction
- Accidents where the other driver was convicted of a moving violation and the insured driver was not
- Emergency response accidents (paid/volunteer police, fire, first-aid, law enforcement) — exception ends when auto ceases being used in response
- Hit-and-run accidents reported to proper authority within 24 hours
- Damage from contact with animals or fowl
- Physical damage from flying gravel, missiles, or falling objects
- Comprehensive claims unless loss was intentionally caused by an insured

### Financial Responsibility Filing
Rule **does not apply** in Alaska. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §6.4.2)

## Transportation Network / Ride Sharing Coverage

Governed by **Act 164** (Alaska). Three phases defined by driver status: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §7.3.2)

See [[coverages/transportation-network]] for full detail.

### Phase I — Logged in, no accepted request, no passenger
- Liability minimum: $50,000/person BI | $100,000/accident BI | $25,000 PD
- First party medical minimum: $5,000 (driver) | $25,000 (pedestrians)
- Policy may provide some or all required amounts; if provided, must meet AK financial responsibility minimums
- Attach: **PA 6602** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Accepted Passenger

### Phase II — Accepted request, no passenger occupying vehicle
- Liability minimum: $500,000 combined BI & PD
- First party medical minimum: $5,000 (driver) | $25,000 (pedestrians)
- Attach: **PA 6603** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Passenger

### Phase III — Passenger occupying the vehicle
- Liability minimum: $500,000 combined BI & PD
- First party medical minimum: $5,000 (driver) | $25,000 (passengers and pedestrians)
- Attach: **PA 6604** — Transportation Network Program Coverage

Premium for all phases: primary use classification (Rule 7.3.1) plus applicable ride-sharing use factor from state pages.

## State-Specific Rules

- **Intra-Family Liability Exclusion** — multistate rule does NOT apply in Alaska. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §10.2)
- **Named Driver Exclusion** — available; excluded driver not included in premium determination. Attach PA 4324. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §10.5)
- **Joint Ownership** — available for multiple vehicle owners (same or different households if related). No premium charge. Attach PA 1237. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §4.9)
