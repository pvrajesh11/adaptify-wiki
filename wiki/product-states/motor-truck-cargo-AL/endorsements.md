## CIM 2001 08 15 — Summary

Three provisions: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15)

1. **Abuse exception to Criminal Acts exclusion** — innocent insured protection under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act. [AL-specific]
2. **Subrogation carve-out for abuse** — innocent insured's right to recover cannot be waived. [AL-specific]
3. **Suit Against Us replacement** — fixed contractual period replaced by time period prescribed by law (statute of limitations). Applies in all coverage forms except Cold Storage Locker, Contingent Cargo, MTC Legal Liability, Riggers' Legal Liability, and Warehouse Legal Liability. [AL-specific]
