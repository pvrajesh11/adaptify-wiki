# Motor Truck Cargo — Alabama — State Specifics

Parent: [Motor Truck Cargo — Alabama — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Endorsement: [CIM 2001 08 15](../../../forms/CIM-2001-08-15.md)

All Alabama-specific provisions are introduced by the mandatory amendatory endorsement CIM 2001 08 15 (edition August 2015).

---

## 1. Criminal Acts Exclusion — Abuse Exception [AL-specific]

**Base rule (multistate):** The Criminal, Fraudulent, Dishonest, or Illegal Acts exclusion bars coverage for losses caused by or at the direction of an insured.

**Alabama override:** When a loss is caused by an act of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute) committed by or at the direction of an insured, the exclusion does NOT apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who:
- Is the subject of the abuse, AND
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Either: files a complaint under the **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** against the abuser and does not voluntarily dismiss the complaint, OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §1)

---

## 2. Subrogation — Abuse Carve-Out [AL-specific]

**Base rule (multistate):** Standard subrogation rights apply.

**Alabama override:** An innocent insured who is the subject of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute) cannot waive the right to recover. The company retains all subrogation rights set forth in the Subrogation condition up to the amount paid for loss caused by an act of abuse.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §2)

---

## 3. Suit Against Us — Statutory Limitations Period [AL-specific]

**Scope:** Applies in all coverage forms except Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage, Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal Liability Coverage.

**Base rule (multistate):** Suit Against Us condition uses a fixed contractual time period.

**Alabama override:** The Suit Against Us condition is deleted and replaced. No suit may be brought unless:
1. All terms of the coverage have been complied with, AND
2. The suit is brought within the **time period prescribed by law** for commencement of such action

Alabama substitutes the applicable statute of limitations for the contractual time period.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §3)
