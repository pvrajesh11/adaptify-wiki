## Mandatory Endorsements

| Form | Title | Bureau |
|---|---|---|
| PA 0104 01 25 | Amendatory Endorsement — California | AAIS |

The AAIS California amendatory endorsement is the only CA-specific endorsement on file. It amends the base AAIS Personal Automobile Policy (PA 0001 07 16) across Definitions, Part A (Liability), and Policy Conditions. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25)

## Endorsement-Driven Substantive Rules

### Definitions
- **"You" / "Your"** — extends to a party who has entered into a domestic partnership recognized by California law with the named insured (resident of the same household). Coverage continues for a former spouse or domestic partner who ceases to be a resident, until the earliest of 90 days after the change in residency, the effective date of another policy showing that person as a named insured, or the end of the policy period. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Definitions.1)
- **"Family member"** — includes persons related to the insured by blood, marriage, **registered domestic partnership under California law**, or adoption; or the insured's ward or foster child. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Definitions.2)

### Part A — Liability
- **Conditional intra-family BI exclusion** — Liability Coverage is excluded for BI to "you" or a "family member" only when the indemnification benefit accrues, directly or indirectly, to "you" or a "family member". Narrower than a categorical intra-family exclusion. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Part A.1)
- **Insurance Under More Than One Policy** replaced — pro-rata damages and defense-cost sharing; non-owned vehicle coverage excess except where that vehicle is insured under an auto-business policy and the operating insured is not the auto-business person/employee/agent (then primary); owned-vehicle coverage excess over an auto-business person's insurance and over premises owners/tenants/lessees in loading/unloading losses. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Part A.2)

### Policy Conditions
- **Recoveries (Part B), item a. deleted** — removes a lien-related sub-item of the base form's Part B Recoveries provision. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Conditions.1)
- **Termination (added)** —
  - **Automatic Termination** at end of period when company offered to renew and named insured did not accept (failure to pay = non-acceptance), or on the effective date of any other insurance obtained on a covered auto.
  - **Cancellation** by named insured by return of policy or written notice; by company by mailed/delivered notice with proof of mailing sufficient. Five enumerated cancellation grounds: nonpayment, fraud, substantial increase in hazard, material misrepresentation (with 20-day cure), driver's license suspension/revocation. Nonpayment notice ≥ 10-day cure; other-cause notice ≥ 20 days; pro-rata return premium.
  - **Nonrenewal** ≥ 30-day notice. Allowed beginning 6 months after original effective date for short-term policies, at end of period for 6-to-12-month policies, or at each anniversary for one-year-or-longer policies. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Conditions.2)
