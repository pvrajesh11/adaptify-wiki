## Mandatory Endorsements (All AK HOBP Policies)

Per Rule 4.2 (Alaska override), all AK HOBP policies must attach the following:

| Endorsement | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| HO 0854 02 21 | Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska | All policies |
| HO 1300 | Asbestos Exclusion | All policies |
| HO 1301 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0001 | Policies with HO 0001 only |
| HO 1302 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0002 | Policies with HO 0002 only |
| HO 1303 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0003 | Policies with HO 0003 only |
| HO 1304 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0004 | Policies with HO 0004 only |
| HO 1305 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0005 | Policies with HO 0005 only |
| HO 1306 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0006 | Policies with HO 0006 only |
| HO 1308 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0008 | Policies with HO 0008 only |
| HO 1323 | Liability Exclusions — Lead | All policies |
| HO 1335 | Liability Exclusions — Pollutants, Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, And Dry Rot | All policies |
| HO 1347 | Punitive Or Exemplary Damages Exclusion | All policies |
| HO 4505 | Windstorm Or Hail Deductible | All policies |

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §4.2)

Note: The state pages (Rev 01 15, effective January 2015) listed HO 0814 as the mandatory Alaska Amendatory Endorsement. HO 0854 02 21 (February 2021 edition) supersedes HO 0814 — no HO 0814 document exists in the vault, and the 2026 SERFF filing (SRFF-TEST-AK-HO-0001) introduces HO 0854 02 21 as the replacement. The table above reflects current practice.

## AK Amendatory Endorsement — HO 0854 02 21

See: [HO-0854-02-21](../../forms/HO-0854-02-21.md)

Filed: 01/01/2026 | Approved: 01/01/2026 | Edition: February 2021
SERFF: SRFF-TEST-AK-HO-0001

This endorsement makes 18 amendments to the base HO policy forms, covering:
- Pollutant definition revision
- Attorney fee provisions (AK Civil Procedure Rule 82) for credit card coverage and liability defense costs
- Settling/cracking exclusion modifications (HO 0003 and HO 0005)
- Efficient proximate/dominant cause rule for property exclusions
- Water exclusion expansion (dam/levee discharge)
- War exclusion enumeration (Coverage L and M)
- Right to counsel at examination under oath
- Sworn statement deadline for property-of-others claims
- Coverage L anti-stacking (proportional share rule)
- Property loss payment timing (30 working days)
- Cancellation and nonrenewal (AK-specific grounds and notice periods)
- Misrepresentation condition replacement
- 20-day advance notice for premium increase >10% or material coverage reduction
- Appraisal procedure (AK-specific timelines, umpire, cost allocation, scope limitation)
- Suit Against Us (3 years from claim denial)

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21)
