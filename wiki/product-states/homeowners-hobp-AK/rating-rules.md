## AK Deviations from Multistate Base

| Topic | Multistate Rule | AK Override | Sub-Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandatory endorsements | None specified multistate | 13 endorsements required (HO 0814, HO 1300–1308, HO 1323, HO 1335, HO 1347, HO 4505) | coverage-options |
| Claim Free Discount (6.9) | No catastrophic event exclusion | Catastrophic claims ($25M+ event) excluded from claims history | state-specifics |
| Risk Group Assignment (6.11) | Standard rule | Rule replaced; adds $25M+ catastrophic event exclusion | state-specifics |
| Owner Occupancy Option (4.19) | Not in multistate | AK-added: can exclude coverage 61+ days after named insured vacates | underwriting-rules |
| Roof Surfacing ACV — All Perils (8.10) | Not in multistate | AK-added: ACV roof surfacing for all covered perils (HO 4836) | state-specifics |
| Cosmetic Damage Exclusion (8.11) | Not in multistate | AK-added: exclude cosmetic windstorm/hail damage to surfacing (HO 2002) | state-specifics |
| Pollutant definition | Multistate definition | Revised AK definition (adds "radioactive"; expands waste definition) | state-specifics |
| Credit card coverage — attorney fees | No attorney fee provision | AK Civil Procedure Rule 82 attorney fees included up to 82(b)(1) cap | state-specifics |
| Settling/cracking exclusion | Standard exclusion language | Replaced with enumerated list + cause-neutral language (HO 0003/0005) | state-specifics |
| Property exclusion intro (dominant cause) | Standard language | Efficient proximate / dominant cause language added (all forms) | state-specifics |
| Water exclusion item 5) | Standard | Expanded to cover overtop/escape/release/discharge from dams/levees | state-specifics |
| Liability defense — attorney fees | No attorney fee provision | AK Civil Procedure Rule 82 attorney fees included up to 82(b)(1) cap | state-specifics |
| War exclusion (Cov L/M) | Standard | Replaced with enumerated 3-part war/military/insurrection list | state-specifics |
| Examination Under Oath | No right to counsel stated | Insured has right to legal representation during EUO | state-specifics |
| Damage to Property of Others — sworn statement | Standard | 60-day deadline for sworn statement after company request | state-specifics |
| Coverage L — stacking | Standard (varies) | Anti-stacking: proportional share based on limits | state-specifics |
| Payment of loss timing | Standard | Undisputed portion: 30 working days after proof of loss and agreement | state-specifics |
| Cancellation and nonrenewal | Standard | Replaced — AK-specific grounds, notice periods, return premium timing | state-specifics |
| Misrepresentation condition | Standard | Replaced — three-part material/fraudulent/would-not-have-issued test | state-specifics |
| Premium/coverage change notice | Not in multistate | 20 days' notice required for >10% premium increase or material coverage reduction | state-specifics |
| Appraisal | Standard | Replaced — AK-specific: 10-day appraiser selection, 15-day statement, umpire fee allocation, no coverage interpretation | state-specifics |
| Suit Against Us | Standard contractual period | 3 years from claim denial; defers to AK law if statutory period is shorter | state-specifics |
