# Homeowners HOBP — Alaska — Coverage Options

Parent: [Homeowners HOBP — Alaska — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Multistate base: [Coverage Options](../../../../multistate/homeowners-hobp/rating-rules/coverage-options.md)

> Alaska has no deviations to policy forms, basic limits, loss settlement provisions, perils, or
> policywriting instructions from the multistate base. All multistate rules apply [multistate, unchanged].
> The only deviation in this sub-page is the mandatory endorsements requirement.

---

## Mandatory Endorsements — Alaska `[AK-specific]`

Rule 4.2 is overridden in Alaska. All AK HOBP policies must attach the following endorsements.
Endorsements referencing a form number in their title attach only to policies including that form;
all others attach to every policy.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §4.2)

| Endorsement | Description | Attach To |
|---|---|---|
| HO 0854 02 21 | Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska | All policies |
| HO 1300 | Asbestos Exclusion | All policies |
| HO 1301 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0001 | Policies with HO 0001 |
| HO 1302 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0002 | Policies with HO 0002 |
| HO 1303 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0003 | Policies with HO 0003 |
| HO 1304 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0004 | Policies with HO 0004 |
| HO 1305 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0005 | Policies with HO 0005 |
| HO 1306 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0006 | Policies with HO 0006 |
| HO 1308 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0008 | Policies with HO 0008 |
| HO 1323 | Liability Exclusions — Lead | All policies |
| HO 1335 | Liability Exclusions — Pollutants, Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, And Dry Rot | All policies |
| HO 1347 | Punitive Or Exemplary Damages Exclusion | All policies |
| HO 4505 | Windstorm Or Hail Deductible | All policies |

Note: The state pages (Rev 01 15, effective January 2015) list HO 0814 as the mandatory Alaska amendatory endorsement. HO 0854 02 21 (February 2021 edition) supersedes HO 0814 — no HO 0814 form document exists in the vault, and the 2026 SERFF filing (SRFF-TEST-AK-HO-0001) explicitly introduces HO 0854 02 21 as the replacement Alaska Amendatory Endorsement. The table above reflects current practice.
