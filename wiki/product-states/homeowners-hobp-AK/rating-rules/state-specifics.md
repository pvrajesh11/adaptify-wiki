# Homeowners HOBP — Alaska — State-Specific Rules

Parent: [Homeowners HOBP — Alaska — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)

---

## Rating Factor Overrides

### Rule 6.9 — Claim Free Discount `[multistate override]`

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §6.9)

The multistate rule's first paragraph is replaced. Alaska adds a **catastrophic claim exclusion**:

A policy is claim free if, during the three year period before the application/renewal date, all adults insured under the policy had Homeowners or equivalent coverage in force and no claims — **excluding catastrophic claims** — of any dollar amount and for any cause of loss, were paid under such coverage.

**Catastrophic claim:** A claim resulting from an event that caused **$25 million or more** in total insured property losses and affected a significant number of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers is not considered when determining if a policy is claim free.

### Rule 6.11 — Risk Group Assignment `[multistate override]`

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §6.11)

The entire multistate Rule 6.11 is replaced in Alaska. The AK version:
1. Retains the same 7×7 matrix structure (G through X risk groups)
2. Adds the **$25 million catastrophic event exclusion** to the list of excluded claim types (same threshold as Rule 6.9 override)

The exclusion applies to claims that did not result from an event that caused $25 million or more in total insured property losses and affects a significant number of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers.

All other elements of the risk group assignment process (experience period, information sources, major/minor claim definitions, minimum $500 payment threshold, mine subsidence/earthquake/sinkhole collapse/tornado exclusions, scheduled personal property and medical payments exclusions) remain the same.

## Additional AK Rules — Coverage Modifications

### Rule 8.10 — Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV For All Covered Property Losses `[AK-specific]`

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §8.10)

Forms: HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006.

The loss settlement terms for roof surfacing on buildings at the described location may be converted from replacement cost to **actual cash value for loss caused by any covered peril.**

Note: This differs from multistate Rule 8.9 (HO 4835), which converts only windstorm or hail losses to ACV. Rule 8.10 applies to all covered perils.

**Not permitted with:**
- Actual cash value endorsement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.1 / HO 4815)
- Functional replacement cost endorsement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.2 / HO 4857)

**Premium:** Apply factor from AK state pages for this rule per peril.
**Endorsement:** HO 4836

### Rule 8.11 — Cosmetic Damage Exclusion `[AK-specific]`

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §8.11)

A policy may be endorsed to **exclude coverage for cosmetic damage to surfacing of covered buildings** caused by windstorm or hail when the windstorm or hail damage to such surfacing consists solely of cosmetic damage.

**Applicable surfacing types:**
- Exterior wall surfacing
- Roof surfacing
- Exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing (must apply to both; cannot apply separately)

**Premium:** Apply factor from AK state pages per peril. Door and window surfacing are treated as one type for premium determination purposes.
**Endorsement:** HO 2002 — with an entry or entries indicating the specific surfacing to which the exclusion applies.

## Policy Form Amendments — HO 0854 02 21

The following AK-specific policy modifications are applied via endorsement **HO 0854 02 21** (Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska, edition February 2021). See [HO-0854-02-21](../../../forms/HO-0854-02-21.md) for full text.

### Definitions

**Pollutant — revised `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §1)

AK replaces the multistate "pollutant" definition:

> "Pollutant" means any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, or **radioactive** irritant or contaminant, including acids, alkalis, chemicals, fumes, smoke, soot, vapor, and waste. Waste includes materials to be disposed of as well as **recycled, reclaimed, or reconditioned.**

Key additions vs. multistate: "radioactive" added to the types of irritants/contaminants; waste definition expanded to include recycled, reclaimed, and reconditioned materials.

### Property Coverage Modifications

**Settling/Cracking Exclusion — Enumerated List (HO 0003 and HO 0005 only) `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §3, §4)

In HO 0003 and HO 0005, the settling/cracking/shrinking/bulging/expanding exclusion is replaced with an enumerated list of structural elements to which it applies:
bulkheads, ceilings, floors, footings, foundations, patios, paved areas, roofs, and walls — **whether resulting from a natural cause, a manmade cause, or a combination thereof.**

**Property Exclusions — Dominant/Efficient Proximate Cause Rule `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §5, §6)

The introductory paragraphs of the property exclusions section are replaced in all forms to add:

> If a covered peril is the dominant cause of a loss, the company will not deny coverage on the basis that a secondary cause in the chain of causation is not a covered peril.

This applies to all forms. In HO 0003 and HO 0005, the replacement targets item 1. of the exclusions section; in all other forms, the first two introductory paragraphs are replaced.

**Water Exclusion — Item 5) Expanded `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §7)

Item 5) of the water exclusion is replaced to clarify that exclusions 1)–4) apply:
- Whether the excluded event results from a natural cause, manmade cause, or a combination; and
- To water and matter moved by water (whether driven by wind or not) that overtops, escapes from, is released from, or is otherwise discharged from a dam, levee, dike, floodgate, or other device designed or used to retain, contain, or control water.

### Liability Coverage Modifications

**Credit Card Coverage — Attorney Fee Provision `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §2)

The Credit Card; Electronic Fund Transfer Card Or Access Device; Forgery; And Counterfeit Money incidental coverage is amended. In any suit the company defends, defense expense includes attorney's fees for which an insured is liable under **Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82** — capped at fees calculated by the schedule for contested cases in **Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82(b)(1)** applied to the applicable limit.

**Liability Defense — Attorney Fee Provision `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §8)

The Claims And Defense Cost provision under Incidental Liability Coverages is amended identically to the credit card amendment above: attorney fees under Civil Procedure Rule 82 are covered up to the 82(b)(1) contested cases schedule cap.

**War Exclusion (Coverage L and M) — Enumerated `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §9)

Exclusion a. (BI/PD from war/military action) is replaced with an enumerated three-part exclusion covering: (1) war including undeclared or civil war; (2) warlike action by military force; (3) insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or governmental action against these. Nuclear weapon discharge is deemed warlike action even if accidental.

**Coverage L — Anti-Stacking `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §12)

The Insurance Under More Than One Policy provision for Coverage L is replaced with a proportional sharing rule: if other valid and collectible insurance applies (other than excess insurance), the company pays only its proportional share based on the ratio of this policy's limit to total insurance covering the loss.

### Claims Handling Modifications

**Examination Under Oath — Right to Counsel `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §10)

Added to the Examination Under Oath provision: an insured has the right to have legal representation present when examined under oath.

**Damage to Property of Others — Sworn Statement Timing `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §11)

Replaces Other Duties — Damage To Property Of Others: company must be given a sworn statement of loss within **60 days** after company's request (timeline not specified in multistate base).

**Payment of Loss — 30 Working Day Requirement `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §13)

Undisputed portion of an insured loss must be paid within **30 working days** after acceptable proof of loss is received and amount of loss is agreed to in writing. Disputed portion must be paid within 30 working days after entry of final judgment or filing of appraisal award with the company.

### Policy Conditions

**Cancellation and Nonrenewal — Replaced `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §14)

The multistate cancellation condition is entirely replaced. Key AK provisions:

| Situation | Notice Required |
|---|---|
| Policy in effect < 60 days | Company may cancel for any reason |
| Policy in effect ≥ 60 days or renewal — nonpayment of premium | 20 days |
| Conviction of crime increasing hazard; fraud/material misrepresentation | 10 days |
| Grossly negligent act substantially increasing hazards; uninsurable physical change | 30 days |
| Entire abandonment of property | 30 days + notice to lender on file |
| Nonrenewal | 20 days |

- Notice method: first class mail with USPS certificate of mailing, or electronic means with confirmation of receipt.
- Named insureds age 70 or older: notice also sent to any person the insured has designated in writing.
- After 60 days, cancellation is limited to anniversary date (except for the enumerated reasons above).
- "Entire abandonment" defined as property no longer occupied and lacking contents of substantial utility — but not abandoned if reasonably maintained and monitored.
- Unearned premium: pro rata basis; return timing varies by cancellation reason (45 days for nonpayment/conviction/fraud; before effective date for other company-initiated cancellations; 45 days after request or effective date for insured-initiated).

**Misrepresentation, Concealment, Or Fraud — Replaced `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §15)

Replaced with a three-part test: no coverage if the misrepresentation/omission/concealment is (a) fraudulent, (b) material to acceptance of the risk or hazard assumed, or (c) would have caused the company not to issue the policy or to issue it on different terms. Applies even to an insured not involved in the misrepresentation.

**Premium Or Coverage Changes — 20-Day Notice `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §16)

New condition: if the company decides to increase renewal premium by more than **10%** (for any reason other than a coverage or exposure base increase) or to materially restrict or reduce coverage not requested by the named insured, the company must provide at least **20 days' advance written notice** before expiration — to the named insured and to the agent/broker of record (by mail or electronic means).

**Appraisal — Replaced `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §17)

Replaced with an AK-specific procedure:
- Appraiser selection: within **10 days** of written demand
- Loss statement deadline: within **15 days** after umpire selected (unless extended by umpire)
- Umpire if not agreed: either party may ask a judge of a court of record in the state where the described location is located
- Cost allocation: each party pays its own counsel and adjuster; all other appraisal expenses determined by umpire
- **Scope limit:** appraisal may not be used to interpret policy terms, determine causation, or determine whether a loss is covered

**Suit Against Us — 3 Years from Claim Denial `[AK-specific]`**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §18)

Replaced: suit must be brought within **3 years after the date the claim was denied** (after complying with all applicable policy terms). If Alaska law requires a different period, AK law governs.
