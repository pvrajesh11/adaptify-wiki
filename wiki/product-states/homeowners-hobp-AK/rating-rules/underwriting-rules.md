# Homeowners HOBP — Alaska — Underwriting Rules

Parent: [Homeowners HOBP — Alaska — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Multistate base: [Underwriting Rules](../../../../multistate/homeowners-hobp/rating-rules/underwriting-rules.md)

> Alaska adds one optional underwriting rule (Rule 4.19) not present in the multistate base.
> All eligibility requirements, ineligible risks, and occupancy rules are [multistate, unchanged].

---

## Owner Occupancy Requirement Option — Rule 4.19 `[AK-specific]`

Forms: HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006, HO 0008.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §4.19)

At company option, a policy issued to:
- An owner-occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling
- An owner-occupant of a condominium or co-operative unit
- A co-owner-occupant of a 2–4 family dwelling
- An occupant under a life estate arrangement in a 1–4 family dwelling
- An occupant purchasing a 1–4 family dwelling under a long-term contract

may be endorsed to **exclude coverage for loss to property that occurs 61 days or more after the named insured begins living at a location other than the described location on other than a temporary basis.**

### Not Permitted For

- Dwellings under construction
- Seasonal dwellings
- Additional or secondary locations
- Condo/co-op units regularly rented or held for rental to others for use as a residence

### Premium

No premium charge or credit applies.

### Endorsement

Attach **HO 2003**.
