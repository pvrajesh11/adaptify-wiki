## Mandatory Endorsements

All policies issued in Alabama must include the following. Endorsements referencing a form number in their title attach only when that form is included in the policy. All others attach to every policy. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §4.2)

| Form | Title | Attach When |
|---|---|---|
| HO 0814 | Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama | Every policy ⚠️ see note |
| HO 1300 | Asbestos Exclusion | Every policy |
| HO 1301 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0001 | Policy includes HO 0001 |
| HO 1302 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0002 | Policy includes HO 0002 |
| HO 1303 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0003 | Policy includes HO 0003 |
| HO 1304 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0004 | Policy includes HO 0004 |
| HO 1305 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0005 | Policy includes HO 0005 |
| HO 1306 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0006 | Policy includes HO 0006 |
| HO 1308 | Amendment Of Policy Terms — Form HO 0008 | Policy includes HO 0008 |
| HO 1323 | Liability Exclusions — Lead | Every policy |
| HO 1335 | Liability Exclusions — Pollutants, Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, And Dry Rot | Every policy |
| HO 1347 | Punitive Or Exemplary Damages Exclusion | Every policy |
| HO 4505 | Windstorm Or Hail Deductible | Every policy |

⚠️ The state pages (Rev 01 15) list HO 0814 as the mandatory Alabama amendatory endorsement. The 01/01/2026 filing introduced HO 0801 09 20 as the Alabama amendatory endorsement. HO 0801 09 20 likely supersedes HO 0814 for policies written under the 2026 filing approval. Verify current mandatory form for post-2026 policies. See [HO 0801 09 20](../../forms/HO-0801-09-20.md).

## AL-Specific Optional Endorsements

### Owner Occupancy (Rule 4.19)

| Form | Title | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HO 2003 | Owner Occupancy — Alabama | Excludes coverage for property losses 61+ days after named insured vacates; no premium charge/credit |

### Roof Surfacing — ACV (Rule 8.10)

| Form | Title | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HO 4836 | Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV — All Covered Losses | Converts roof surfacing to ACV for all covered perils; rated by peril from state pages |

### Cosmetic Damage Exclusion (Rule 8.11)

| Form | Title | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HO 2002 | Cosmetic Damage Exclusion | Excludes cosmetic wind/hail damage to specified surfacing types; indicate surfacing types on form; rated by peril |
