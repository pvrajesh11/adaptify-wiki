## State Deviations from Multistate Base

| Topic | Multistate Rule | AL Override | Sub-Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandatory endorsements | None specified | 13 mandatory endorsements required [AL-specific] | state-specifics |
| Claim Free Discount (§6.9) | No catastrophic claim exemption | Catastrophic claims (≥$25M event) excluded from claim count [multistate override] | state-specifics |
| Risk Group Assignment (§6.11) | Rule applies per multistate; catastrophic events not exempted | Catastrophic claims (≥$25M event) excluded from risk group claim count [multistate override] | state-specifics |
| Owner Occupancy Requirement (§4.19) | Not in multistate base | Added: option to endorse exclusion for losses 61+ days after named insured vacates [AL-specific] | state-specifics |
| Roof Surfacing ACV — All Perils (§8.10) | Not in multistate base (§8.9 covers wind/hail only) | Added: convert roof surfacing to ACV for any covered peril (HO 4836) [AL-specific] | state-specifics |
| Cosmetic Damage Exclusion (§8.11) | Not in multistate base | Added: exclude cosmetic wind/hail damage to roof/wall/door/window surfacing (HO 2002) [AL-specific] | state-specifics |
