## Owner Occupancy Requirement `[AL-specific]`

Available on Forms HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006, and HO 0008. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §4.19)

At company option, a policy issued to any of the following insured types may be endorsed to exclude coverage for loss to property that occurs 61 days or more after the named insured begins living at a location other than the described location on other than a temporary basis:

- Owner-occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling
- Owner-occupant of a condominium or co-operative unit
- Co-owner-occupant of a 2–4 family dwelling
- Occupant under a life estate arrangement
- Occupant purchasing a dwelling under a long-term contract

**Not permitted when the policy covers:**
- A dwelling under construction
- A seasonal dwelling
- An additional or secondary location
- A condominium or co-operative unit regularly rented or held for rental to others

Premium: No charge or credit.
Endorsement: HO 2003.

## Claim Free Discount `[multistate override]`

The multistate rule (§6.9) is replaced in Alabama. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §6.9)

A policy is claim free if, during the three-year period before the application/renewal preparation date:
- All adults insured under the policy had Homeowners or equivalent coverage in force; AND
- No claims (excluding catastrophic claims, as defined below), of any dollar amount and for any cause of loss, were paid under such coverage.

**Catastrophic claim exemption (AL only):** A claim resulting from an event that caused $25 million or more in total insured property losses AND affected a significant number of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers shall not be considered when determining if a policy is claim free.

The multistate rule does not include this catastrophic exemption.

## Risk Group Assignment `[multistate override]`

The multistate rule (§6.11) is replaced in Alabama. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §6.11)

Alabama replaces the entire multistate rule. The Alabama version is structurally identical to the multistate rule with one key addition: catastrophic event claims are excluded from the claim count.

**Information sources:** application signed by applicant/insured; loss/claims history database; company internal records.

**Experience period:** 3 years before application date (new) or renewal preparation date.

**Claim eligibility for counting — a claim must meet ALL of:**
- Occurred within the three-year experience period
- Pertains to a risk eligible under this manual or equivalent (including Mobile-Homeowners)
- Did not result from mine subsidence, earthquake, or sinkhole collapse
- Was not paid solely for scheduled personal property or medical payments coverage
- Did not result from a catastrophic event (≥$25 million in total insured property losses, affecting a significant number of policyholders and insurers) — **[AL-specific addition]**
- Resulted in payments totaling $500 or more (net of subrogation recoveries)

**Major claims:** fire, liability, theft, or vandalism losses.
**Minor claims:** all other claims.

**Risk Group Matrix:**

| Minor \ Major | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | G | I | K | M | O | Q | R |
| **1** | H | J | L | N | P | R | S |
| **2** | I | K | M | O | Q | S | T |
| **3** | J | L | N | P | R | T | U |
| **4** | K | M | O | Q | S | U | V |
| **5** | L | N | P | R | T | V | W |
| **6+** | M | O | Q | S | U | W | X |

Apply the factor shown in state pages for the applicable risk group letter to adjust premium for each peril.

If, after factor application, actual claims history merits lesser premium, refund the difference per manual rules.

## Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV For All Covered Property Losses `[AL-specific]`

Available on Forms HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, and HO 0006. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §8.10)

Converts loss settlement terms for roof surfacing of buildings on the described location from replacement cost to actual cash value for loss caused by any covered peril.

This is broader than the multistate §8.9 (HO 4835), which converts only windstorm or hail losses to ACV. Rule 8.10 uses form HO 4836 and covers all covered perils.

**Not permitted when policy includes:**
- ACV loss settlement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.1)
- Functional replacement cost for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.2)

Premium: Factor from state pages (by peril).
Endorsement: HO 4836.

## Cosmetic Damage Exclusion `[AL-specific]`

Available on all forms. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §8.11)

A policy may be endorsed to exclude coverage for cosmetic damage to the surfacing of covered buildings caused by windstorm or hail, when the windstorm or hail damage to such surfacing consists solely of cosmetic damage.

Applicable surfacing types (may be applied selectively, except as noted below):
- Exterior wall surfacing
- Roof surfacing
- Exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing (must be applied together; cannot be applied to only one of these two)

Premium: Factor from state pages (by peril). For premium determination, exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing are treated as one surfacing type.
Endorsements: HO 2002; include entries indicating which surfacing type(s) the exclusion applies to.
