## Alabama Deviations

[multistate, unchanged] — No Alabama-specific deviations to underwriting rules (Rule 2 eligibility, ineligible risks, occupancy requirements). All multistate rules apply as written.

Note: The owner occupancy endorsement option (Rule 4.19) added in Alabama affects policy terms mid-term if occupancy changes, but does not alter the eligibility criteria at policy inception. See [State-Specific Rules](state-specifics.md) for Rule 4.19.
