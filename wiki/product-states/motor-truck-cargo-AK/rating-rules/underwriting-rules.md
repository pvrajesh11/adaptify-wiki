## AK Underwriting Rules

[multistate, unchanged] — All underwriting rules, rating method eligibility thresholds, owner-operator tiers, fleet condition tiers, financial stability factors, vehicle protection tiers, subcontractor/contingent cargo underwriting criteria, and coverage modifications from the multistate base apply in Alaska without modification.

See [multistate/motor-truck-cargo/rating-rules/underwriting-rules.md](../../../multistate/motor-truck-cargo/rating-rules/underwriting-rules.md) for full detail.

### Rating Method Eligibility Summary

| Condition | Method |
|---|---|
| Gross receipts ≤ $500,000 OR fewer than 10 power units | Per Vehicle (Method A) |
| Gross receipts > $500,000 OR 10+ power units | Gross Receipts (Method B) |

### Key Underwriting Factors

- Owner-operator use (3 tiers: none / limited with checks / extensive with limited supervision)
- Fleet condition (3 tiers: newer with documented inspection / mixed / older with poor documentation)
- Financial stability: Excellent / Good / Poor (based on financial statement or CAB report)
- Vehicle protection (3 tiers for cargo; 2 tiers for mobile/trailer)
- Subcontractor relationship quality (incidental vs. substantial; additional named insured status; hold harmless agreement; established vs. short-term relationship)
- Target/hazardous cargo (Class 5): modification 1.25–2.00 based on over-the-road exposure, protection, and pickup/drop-off location risk
