# Motor Truck Cargo — Alaska — State-Specific Rules

Parent: [Motor Truck Cargo — Alaska — Rating Rules](../rating-rules.md)
Source form: [CIM 2003 06 20](../../../forms/CIM-2003-06-20.md)

All AK-specific deviations from the multistate base are introduced by the CIM 2003 06 20 Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska (edition June 2020). No rating manual deviations were identified.

---

## 1. Domestic Violence Exception — Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts Exclusion

**[AK-specific]**

The Criminal, Fraudulent, Dishonest, Or Illegal Acts exclusion in the CIM 7002 base form is amended to add an innocent insured exception for domestic violence.

**Rule:** If an insured causes a covered loss through an act of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute), the exclusion does NOT apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who:
- Is the subject of the domestic violence, AND
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Files for a protective order under Alaska Statute 18.66 against the abuser, OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §1)

## 2. Subrogation — Domestic Violence Carve-Out

**[AK-specific]**

**Rule:** An innocent insured who is the subject of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute) cannot waive his or her right to recover. The company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for any loss caused by an act of domestic violence.

This overrides the general subrogation rule in the multistate base, which permits the insured to waive the right to recover from others in writing before a loss occurs.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §2)

## 3. Suit Against Us — Replaced

**[AK-specific] [multistate override]**

**Scope:** Applies in all coverage forms except Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage, Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal Liability Coverage.

**Multistate rule:** No suit unless all terms complied with AND suit brought within two years of insured's first knowledge of loss; if law makes this invalid, shortest period permitted by law applies.

**AK rule (replacement):** No suit unless all terms complied with AND suit brought within the time period prescribed by Alaska law for commencement of such action.

The AK endorsement removes the contractual two-year period and defers entirely to the Alaska statute of limitations. ⚠️ The specific Alaska statute applicable to MTC cargo claims was not identified in the filing documents — verify applicable limitations period with legal counsel.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §3)
