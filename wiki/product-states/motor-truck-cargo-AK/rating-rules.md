## State Deviations from Multistate Base

Alaska applies the multistate CIM MTC MU RTG REV 04 18 rating rules without modification. All AK-specific changes are confined to policy form conditions via the CIM 2003 06 20 amendatory endorsement.

| Topic | Multistate Rule | AK Override | Sub-Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rating methodology | Per Vehicle / Gross Receipts as published | [multistate, unchanged] | coverage-options |
| Commodity classification | Classes 1–5 as published | [multistate, unchanged] | coverage-options |
| Underwriting factors | Owner-operator use, fleet condition, financial stability, vehicle protection | [multistate, unchanged] | underwriting-rules |
| Coverage modifications | Named perils, refrigeration, contingent, radius, terminal, deductible, IRPM | [multistate, unchanged] | underwriting-rules |
| Criminal acts exclusion | Standard — no domestic violence exception | **AK domestic violence exception added** [AK-specific] | state-specifics |
| Subrogation | Insured may waive right to recover in writing before a loss | **Cannot be waived by domestic violence victim** [AK-specific] | state-specifics |
| Suit Against Us | 2 years from insured's first knowledge of loss | **Replaced — defers to Alaska statute of limitations** [AK-specific] | state-specifics |
