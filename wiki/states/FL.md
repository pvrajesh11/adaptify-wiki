## Key Statutes Referenced

- **Florida Protection From Abuse Act** — Governs the protective order process for domestic abuse victims. Referenced in HO 0809 02 22: innocent insured must either file a complaint under this Act (without voluntary dismissal) or seek a warrant and cooperate in prosecution to qualify for the intentional acts exclusion exception. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22)
- **Florida statute — abuse definition** — The Florida statutory definition of "abuse" triggers the innocent insured exception in the homeowners Intentional Acts property exclusion and the Subrogation condition carve-out. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22)
- **Florida Statute §95.11** — Statute of limitations for written contracts. HO 0809 02 22 replaces the base policy's two-year contractual Suit Against Us period for property coverages with the Florida statutory period. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22)

## Notable State-Specific Rules

- **Catastrophic claim exclusion** — Florida overrides the multistate Claim Free Discount (Rule 6.9) and Risk Group Assignment (Rule 6.11) to exclude from claims history any claim resulting from an event causing $25 million or more in total insured property losses affecting a significant number of policyholders and insurers. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §6.9, §6.11)
- **Mandatory endorsements** — 14+ endorsements are mandatory in Florida for all policies or for specific policy forms, including HO 0814/HO 0809 02 22 (FL amendatory), HO 1300–1308 series (form amendments), HO 1323 (lead), HO 1335 (pollutants/bacteria/fungi), HO 1347 (punitive damages), and HO 4505 (windstorm/hail deductible). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §4.2)
- **Owner Occupancy Requirement** (Rule 4.19) — At company option, policies can be endorsed (HO 2003) to exclude coverage for loss occurring 61+ days after named insured begins living elsewhere. Florida-only rule added in revision 12 13. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §4.19)
- **Roof Surfacing ACV — all perils** (Rule 8.10) — Florida adds the option to convert roof surfacing loss settlement from RC to ACV for all covered perils (using HO 4836), in addition to the multistate windstorm/hail-only option (HO 4835). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §8.10)
- **Cosmetic Damage Exclusion** (Rule 8.11) — Florida adds the ability to exclude cosmetic damage to exterior wall, roof, door, and window surfacing caused by windstorm or hail (using HO 2002). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_state-pages_v1215 §8.11)
- **Innocent insured — domestic abuse** — Intentional Acts property exclusion does not apply to innocent insured victim of abuse under Florida statute, subject to protective order/warrant and cooperation conditions. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22)
- **Suit Against Us — statutory period** — Florida replaces the two-year base policy Suit Against Us period for property coverages with the Florida statutory period. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22)
- **Renewal notice** — Company must give 30 days' notice before renewal if a premium increase is imposed (other than at insured's request). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22)
