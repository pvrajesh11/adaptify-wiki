## Key Statutes Referenced

- **Act 164** — Governs transportation network (rideshare) driver coverage requirements in Alabama. Mandates minimum liability and first party medical limits for Phases I, II, and III of rideshare operations. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §7.3.2)
- **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** — Referenced in the AL auto amendatory endorsement (PPA 0101), the AL homeowners amendatory endorsement (HO 0801 09 20), and the AL motor truck cargo amendatory endorsement (CIM 2001 08 15) in relation to innocent insured protection and abuse subrogation provisions. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §1; 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_form_HO-0801-09-20 §1; 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §1)

## Notable State-Specific Rules

### Auto PPA

- Intra-Family Liability Exclusion multistate rule **does not apply** in Alabama. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §10.2)
- Financial Responsibility Filing rule **does not apply** in Alabama. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §6.4.2)
- UM and UIM coverage are mandatory offerings (written rejection required to waive). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §3.4, §3.7)
- Transportation network coverage governed by Act 164 with specific minimum limits per phase. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_manual_v1.5 §7.3.2)
- Innocent insured protection for abuse under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act (physical damage and subrogation). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §1–§2)

### Motor Truck Cargo

- No Alabama rating or underwriting rule deviations from multistate base (REV 04 18). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_filing-summary)
- Mandatory endorsement CIM 2001 08 15 adds innocent insured protection for abuse under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act (criminal acts exclusion exception and subrogation). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §1–§2)
- Suit Against Us condition replaced with a statute-of-limitations-based version in most coverage forms (exception: Cold Storage Locker, Contingent Cargo, MTC Legal Liability, Riggers' Legal Liability, Warehouse Legal Liability). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §3)

### Homeowners HOBP

- 13 mandatory endorsements required, including form-specific amendments (HO 1301–1308), asbestos exclusion, liability exclusions (lead; pollutants/bacteria/fungi/wet rot/dry rot), punitive damages exclusion, and windstorm/hail deductible. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §4.2)
- Claim Free Discount: catastrophic claims (events ≥$25M in total insured property losses) excluded from the claim count. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §6.9)
- Risk Group Assignment: same $25M catastrophic event exclusion added to claim eligibility rules. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §6.11)
- Owner Occupancy Requirement (Rule 4.19): at company option, policy may exclude coverage 61+ days after named insured vacates on a non-temporary basis (HO 2003). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §4.19)
- Roof Surfacing ACV — All Perils (Rule 8.10): may convert roof surfacing to ACV for all covered perils (broader than multistate wind/hail-only option). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §8.10)
- Cosmetic Damage Exclusion (Rule 8.11): may exclude cosmetic wind/hail damage to roof, exterior wall, exterior door, or exterior window surfacing. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_state-pages_v1215 §8.11)
- Innocent insured protection for abuse under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act (property intentional acts exclusion exception and subrogation). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_form_HO-0801-09-20 §1–§2)
