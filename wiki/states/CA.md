## Key Statutes Referenced

- **California domestic partnership law** — Recognized in the AAIS Auto PPA California amendatory endorsement (PA 0104 01 25). The "you"/"your" definition extends to a party who has entered into a domestic partnership recognized by California law with the named insured (resident of the same household). The "family member" definition includes persons related to the insured by registered domestic partnership under California law. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Definitions.1, §Definitions.2)
- **California Insurance Commissioner** — Rule-making authority recognized in the cancellation grounds of PA 0104 01 25: "any other factor found by the Commissioner of Insurance to have a substantial relationship to the risk of loss" is among the material-misrepresentation cancellation triggers. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Conditions.2)

## Notable State-Specific Rules

### Auto PPA

- **Domestic partnership recognition** in core definitions: "you"/"your" extends to a party in a domestic partnership recognized by California law with the named insured; "family member" includes persons related by registered domestic partnership under California law. California is the first state in the wiki to do so. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Definitions.1, §Definitions.2)
- **Former-spouse / former-domestic-partner extension**: coverage for a former spouse or domestic partner who ceases to be a resident extends until the earliest of 90 days after change in residency, the effective date of another policy showing that person as a named insured, or the end of the policy period. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Definitions.1)
- **Conditional intra-family BI exclusion**: Liability Coverage is excluded for BI to "you" or a "family member" only when the indemnification benefit accrues, directly or indirectly, to "you" or a "family member". Narrower than a categorical intra-family exclusion. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Part A.1)
- **Cancellation grounds** are limited to five enumerated grounds: nonpayment, fraud in claim presentation/pursuit, substantial increase in hazard, material misrepresentation (with 20-day cure window), and driver's license suspension/revocation of named insured / household member / regular driver during the policy period or 180 days before the most recent renewal. Non-payment cancellation requires at least a 10-day cure period after the post-due-date notice; other-cause cancellation requires at least 20 days' notice. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Conditions.2.b)
- **Nonrenewal notice**: at least 30 days, mailed or delivered. Permitted at six months (if the policy period is shorter), at end of period (six-to-twelve-month policies), or at each anniversary (one-year-or-longer policies). (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Conditions.2.c)
