### Motor Truck Cargo

- Domestic violence exception to the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion: the exclusion does not apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by an innocent insured who is the subject of domestic violence and who did not cooperate with or contribute to the act. Requires filing for a protective order under Alaska Statute 18.66 or seeking a warrant and cooperating in prosecution. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §1)
- Subrogation: an innocent insured who is the subject of domestic violence cannot waive his or her right to recover. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §2)
- Suit Against Us: the base CIM 7002 two-year contractual limitation is replaced by a reference to the Alaska statute of limitations. Applies in all CIM forms except Cold Storage Locker, Contingent Cargo, MTC Legal Liability, Riggers' Legal Liability, and Warehouse Legal Liability. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §3)
- Rating rules (CIM MTC MU RTG REV 04 18) apply in Alaska without modification — all AK changes are confined to the CIM 2003 06 20 endorsement. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_filing-summary)

## Notable State-Specific Rules

### Auto PPA

- Intra-Family Liability Exclusion multistate rule **does not apply** in Alaska. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §10.2)
- Financial Responsibility Filing rule **does not apply** in Alaska. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §6.4.2)
- UM and UIM coverage are mandatory offerings (written rejection required to waive). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §3.4, §3.7)
- Transportation network coverage governed by Act 164 with specific minimum limits per phase. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_manual_v1.5 §7.3.2)

### Homeowners HOBP

- Claim Free Discount (Rule 6.9) and Risk Group Assignment (Rule 6.11) exclude catastrophic claims from events causing $25M+ in total insured losses. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §6.9, §6.11)
- Owner occupancy option (Rule 4.19) allows endorsement excluding coverage 61+ days after named insured vacates described location. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_state-pages_v1215 §4.19)
- Efficient proximate cause / dominant cause rule applies to all HO property exclusions. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §5, §6)
- Cancellation and nonrenewal governed by AK-specific conditions including 20-day nonrenewal notice, anniversary-date cancellation restriction after 60 days, and additional protections for insureds age 70+. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §14)
- Suit Against Us limited to 3 years from date claim denied (Property Coverages). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §18)
- Attorney fees under Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82 are included in both credit card coverage defense costs and liability defense costs, capped at 82(b)(1) schedule. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §2, §8)
- Premium increases >10% or material coverage reductions require 20 days advance notice. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §16)
