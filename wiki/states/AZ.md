## Key Statutes Referenced

- **Arizona Revised Statutes § 43-1201, A.4** — Defines tax-exempt organizations. Used in the AAIS Auto PPA Arizona amendatory endorsement (PA 0102 07 18) to carve out volunteer activity from the transportation network, public/livery, and food/goods-delivery exclusions across Part A (Liability), Part B (Medical Payments), and Part D (Physical Damage). (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.1.f, §Part A.1.g, §Part B.2.e, §Part B.2.f, §Part D.7, §Part D.8)
- **Arizona Financial Responsibility Law** — Minimum bodily injury and property damage liability limits. Caps the applicability of the Part A intra-family BI exclusion and Exclusion 2.d. in PA 0102 07 18; both apply only to amounts above the statutory minimum. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.2.d, §Part A new exclusion)

## Notable State-Specific Rules

### Auto PPA

- **Replacement-auto rules** vary by coverage type: 14-day request window when at least one declarations auto carries Collision/Comprehensive, otherwise a 4-day window with a $500 deductible on losses inside that window. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Definitions)
- **Transportation network and delivery exclusions** apply across Part A, Part B, and Part D when a vehicle is logged into a TNC as a driver, used as public/livery conveyance, or used in commerce for food/goods delivery. Carve-outs: shared-expense carpool (livery/TNC contexts) and tax-exempt-organization volunteer activity per ARS § 43-1201, A.4 (all contexts). (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.1.f, §Part A.1.g, §Part B.2.e, §Part B.2.f, §Part D.7, §Part D.8)
- **Intra-family BI exclusion** applies only to liability above the Arizona Financial Responsibility Law minimum. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A new exclusion)
- **Termination provision** adds a 7-day premium grace period and an Offer To Renew procedure: failure to pay renewal premium within 7 days lets the company terminate on or after the 8th day. After the first 60 days (or on a renewal/continuation), cancellation is restricted to seven enumerated grounds. Nonrenewal requires 45 days' written notice. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Termination)
- **Cancellation notice methods**: certified mail, USPS certificate of mailing, or first-class with Intelligent Mail barcode tracking. (SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Termination.b)
