# Homeowners (HOBP)

The Homeowners By-Peril Rating Plan (HOBP) provides residential property and liability coverage across
multiple policy forms. It uses a by-peril rating approach in which premium is computed separately for
each covered peril, then combined. HOBP is the homeowners product line written under the Acme Insurance
Company HOBP multistate manual (edition 01 11).

## Policy Forms

| Form | Description | Coverages Provided |
|---|---|---|
| HO 0001 | Basic Form | Coverages A, B, C, D, L, M |
| HO 0002 | Broad Form | Coverages A, B, C, D, L, M |
| HO 0003 | Special Form | Coverages A, B, C, D, L, M |
| HO 0004 | Contents Broad Form | Coverages C, D, L, M |
| HO 0005 | Special Building And Contents Form | Coverages A, B, C, D, L, M |
| HO 0006 | Unit-Owners Form | Coverages A, C, D, L, M |
| HO 0008 | Limited Form | Coverages A, B, C, D, L, M |

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §3.1)

## Coverage Structure

| Coverage | Description |
|---|---|
| Coverage A | Residence (dwelling and built-in components; on HO 0006: fixtures, alterations, additions) |
| Coverage B | Related Private Structures (unattached structures, fences, driveways, outdoor fixtures) |
| Coverage C | Personal Property (owned or used by insured) |
| Coverage D | Additional Living Costs And Loss Of Rent |
| Coverage L | Personal Liability |
| Coverage M | Medical Payments To Others |

Basic limits: Coverage L $100,000 each occurrence; Coverage M $1,000 per person. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §3.6)

## Peril Rating Approach

Premium is determined separately per peril. Rating variables include: rating zone, policy form and peril
subzone relativity, fire protection class, construction/cladding/roof covering type, and liability limit
relativities. Risk characteristic factors (fire protective devices, theft devices, number of families,
solid fuel appliances, swimming pools, trampolines, canines, seasonal residence, claim history, home age/
systems) further adjust the per-peril base premium. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_manual_v0111 §5.1, §6)

## Optional Flood Coverage

Optional flood coverage is available on policies written on forms HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, or HO 0005
via endorsement HO 2710. This coverage does not satisfy NFIP requirements. A 360-hour (15-day) waiting
period applies. See [Flood Coverage](../multistate/homeowners-hobp/rating-rules/flood-coverage.md). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)
