# CIM 7002 01 15 — Motor Truck Cargo Coverage

Product: [Motor Truck Cargo](../products/motor-truck-cargo.md)
Filed in: AK (amended by [CIM 2003 06 20](CIM-2003-06-20.md))

---

## Overview

CIM 7002 01 15 is the base Motor Truck Cargo Coverage form, edition January 2015. It provides open-perils cargo coverage for property in transit on owned vehicles and at terminal locations. The form is written under the Owner's Cargo Coverage framework and covers 11 pages. State-specific amendments are applied via endorsement (in Alaska: CIM 2003 06 20).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-7002-01-15)

## Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Owned vehicle | Any one vehicle, truck, trailer, semitrailer, or combination pulled by one power unit owned or leased and operated by the insured |
| Perishable stock | Property preserved under controlled conditions susceptible to loss/damage if conditions change |
| Pollutant | Any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, or radioactive irritant or contaminant, and electrical/magnetic/sound emissions |
| Specified perils | Fire; lightning; windstorm; hail; collision/overturn/derailment of transporting conveyance; collapse of bridge or culvert; theft |
| Spoilage | Any detrimental change in physical state of perishable stock |
| Terminal | A building where covered property is transferred between vehicles or conveyances, including loading/unloading and temporary storage associated with the transfer |
| Transit | Shipment from point of origin to destination, including ordinary stops/interruptions/delays/transfers incidental to the route (including rest periods), ending upon acceptance by consignee at destination |

## Property Covered

### 1. Property In A Terminal

Covers direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to described property while at a terminal location.

Limitations:
- Only at a described terminal or within 100 feet of it
- Only while in due course of transit
- Property of others covered only to extent of insured's legal liability

### 2. Property In Transit

Covers direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to described property while in due course of transit including loading and unloading.

Limitations:
- Only on or in an owned vehicle
- Property of others covered only to extent of insured's legal liability
- Loading/unloading covered only if adjacent to an owned vehicle (sidewalk, street, loading dock, or similar area)

## Coverage Extensions

Coverage extensions are part of (not in addition to) the applicable property limit. Extensions are not subject to coinsurance. Limits may be overridden on the schedule of coverages.

### Debris Removal

Pays cost to remove debris of covered property caused by a covered peril.
- Does not include extracting pollutants from land/water, or removing/restoring polluted land/water
- Limit: 25% of loss payment; combined loss + debris removal may not exceed property limit
- Additional limit: up to $5,000 extra when debris removal exceeds 25% or combined exceeds property limit
- Reporting requirement: expenses must be reported in writing within 180 days of direct physical loss

### Emergency Removal

Pays for direct physical loss to covered property while being moved or stored to prevent a covered peril loss.
- Time limitation: up to 365 days after property is first moved; does not extend past policy expiration

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-7002-01-15 Coverage Extensions)

## Supplemental Coverages

Supplemental coverage limits are separate from property limits (not added to them). Not subject to coinsurance.

### Pollutant Cleanup And Removal

Pays expense to extract pollutants from land or water when discharge is caused by a covered peril.
- Does not pay: testing, evaluating, observing, or recording pollutant existence/level/effects (exception: testing necessary for extraction)
- Reporting: expenses must be reported in writing within 180 days of covered peril occurrence
- Limit: $10,000 per location per 12-month period

### Property You Have Sold

Pays for direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to covered property insured has sold and is shipping at owner's risk.
- Coverage limitation: only when shipment rejected because property is damaged AND owner has refused to pay

### Rejected Shipments

Pays for direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to outgoing shipments rejected by consignee (including non-deliverable).
- Coverage limitation: only while property is in due course of transit back to insured or awaiting return
- Time limitation: ends 10 days after delivery attempted or made to consignee, unless property is in due course of transit back

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-7002-01-15 Supplemental Coverages)

## Perils Covered

Open perils — risks of direct physical loss unless loss is limited or caused by an excluded peril.

---

## Perils Excluded

### Absolute Exclusions (Anti-Concurrent Causation Language Applies)

- **Civil Authority** — seizure, confiscation, destruction, or quarantine by civil authority (exception: destruction to prevent spread of fire, unless that fire is caused by an excluded peril)
- **Nuclear Hazard** — nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination (direct loss by fire from nuclear hazard is covered)
- **War And Military Action** — war (including undeclared/civil war), warlike action by military force, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or unlawful seizure of power

### Other Exclusions

| Exclusion | Scope | Exception |
|---|---|---|
| Contamination Or Deterioration | Corrosion, decay, fungus, mildew, mold, rot, rust, or inherent quality causing self-damage | If results in a specified peril, that specified peril loss is covered |
| Criminal, Fraudulent, Dishonest, Or Illegal Acts | Acts by insured, others with interest in property, entrusted parties, partners/officers/directors/trustees/members/managers, or their employees/agents | Acts of destruction by employees are covered; theft by employees is not ⚠️ Amended in AK by CIM 2003 06 20 (domestic violence exception) |
| Loss Of Use | Loss of use, delay, or loss of market | None |
| Missing Property | Unexplained disappearance, inventory shortage, or no physical evidence | None |
| Pollutants | Release, discharge, seepage, migration, dispersal, or escape of pollutants | If caused by a specified peril; or under Pollutant Cleanup and Removal supplemental coverage |
| Spoilage | Loss to perishable stock caused by spoilage | None |
| Temperature/Humidity | Humidity, dampness, dryness, or changes/extremes of temperature | If results in a specified peril, that specified peril loss is covered |
| Voluntary Parting | Voluntarily parting with title or possession due to fraudulent scheme, trick, or false pretense | None |
| Wear And Tear | Wear and tear, marring, or scratching | If results in a specified peril, that specified peril loss is covered |

## Duties After Loss

1. **Notice** — prompt notice with description of property; written notice on request; police notice when act is a crime
2. **Protect Property** — take all reasonable steps to protect covered property; insurer pays reasonable costs for emergency measures to prevent further covered peril damage (does not increase limit)
3. **Proof Of Loss** — signed, sworn proof within 60 days of insurer's request; must include: time/place/circumstances; other policies; interests/mortgages/liens; title changes during policy period; estimates, specifications, inventories
4. **Examination** — submit to examination under oath as often as reasonably requested; insurer may examine multiple persons separately
5. **Records** — produce records including tax returns and bank microfilms of canceled checks as often as requested
6. **Damaged Property** — exhibit damaged and undamaged property; allow inspection or samples
7. **Volunteer Payments** — do not voluntarily make payments, assume obligations, or incur expenses except to protect property from further damage
8. **Abandonment** — may not abandon property to insurer without written consent
9. **Cooperation** — cooperate in performing all acts required by the policy

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-7002-01-15 What Must Be Done In Case Of Loss)

## Valuation

- **Actual Cash Value** — value at time of loss with deduction for depreciation
- **Pair Or Set** — reasonable proportion of total pair/set value; not a total loss of pair or set
- **Loss To Parts** — value of only the lost/damaged part or cost to repair/replace it

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-7002-01-15 Valuation)

## How Much We Pay

- **Insurable Interest** — no payment beyond insured's insurable interest
- **Deductible** — applies per occurrence as shown on schedule of coverages
- **Loss Settlement** — lesser of: ACV; cost to repair/replace/rebuild with like kind and quality; or applicable scheduled limit for owned vehicle or terminal location
- **Catastrophe Limit** — maximum regardless of number of vehicles or terminal locations involved
- **Vehicle At Terminal** — when owned vehicle is within terminal building or within 100 feet of it, terminal limit applies; vehicle and terminal limits are not combined
- **Multiple Policies** — proportional share if same terms; excess only if different terms

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-7002-01-15 How Much We Pay)

## Loss Payment

- Insurer options: pay ACV; pay cost of repair/replacement; rebuild/repair/replace with equivalent property within reasonable time; or take property at agreed/appraised value
- Notice of intent to rebuild/repair/replace must be given within 30 days of duly executed proof of loss
- Loss payable 30 days after: satisfactory proof of loss received AND loss amount established by written agreement or appraisal award

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-7002-01-15 Loss Payment)
