# PA 0102 07 18 — Amendatory Endorsement — Arizona

Product: [Auto PPA](../products/auto-ppa.md)
State: [Arizona (AZ)](../states/AZ.md)
Amends: [PA 0001 07 16](PPA-0001-07-16.md)
Bureau: AAIS
Mandatory in AZ: Yes

---

## Overview

PA 0102 07 18 is a seven-page Arizona-specific amendatory endorsement to the AAIS Personal Automobile Policy. Edition July 2018. (Source-document footer also bears legacy code "PA 0102 07 16"; the title-page edition is treated as authoritative.) It amends Definitions, Part A (Liability), Part B (Medical Payments), Part D (Physical Damage), and Policy Conditions. Substantive themes are replacement-auto coverage rules, transportation network and food-/goods-delivery exclusions with statutorily defined volunteer carve-outs, intra-family BI exclusion limited to amounts above the Arizona Financial Responsibility Law minimum, and a comprehensive Termination provision (Automatic, Cancellation, Nonrenewal, Other Termination Conditions, Offer To Renew) including a 7-day premium grace period.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18)

## Amendments

### 1. Definitions — "Replacement Auto" (item b. replaced)

**What changes:** Replacement-auto coverage rules vary by coverage type.

**Rule:**
- **Collision Coverage** — if at least one auto on the declarations carries Collision and the named insured asks for Collision on the replacement within 14 days, coverage begins on date of ownership at the broadest declarations level. If no auto on the declarations carries Collision, the named insured has 4 days to request it; a loss within that 4-day window is covered subject to a $500 deductible.
- **Comprehensive Coverage** — same 14-day / 4-day / $500-deductible structure as Collision.
- **Other Coverages** — replacement auto receives the broadest declarations coverage, beginning on date of ownership.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Definitions)

---

### 2. Part A — Liability: Public/Livery and TNC Exclusion (1.f. replaced)

**What changes:** Excludes liability while a vehicle is used as a public or livery conveyance or while logged into a transportation network program as a driver.

**Carve-outs:** Shared-expense carpool; volunteer activity for a tax-exempt organization as described in Arizona Revised Statutes § 43-1201, A.4.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.1.f)

---

### 3. Part A — Liability: Delivery in Commerce Exclusion (1.g. replaced)

**What changes:** Excludes liability while a vehicle is used in commerce for delivery or transport of food or other goods, including TNC delivery driver use.

**Carve-out:** Volunteer activity per ARS § 43-1201, A.4 only (no shared-expense carve-out).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.1.g)

---

### 4. Part A — Liability: Intra-Family BI Exclusion (added) and Exclusion 2.d. amended

**What changes:** A new exclusion bars Liability Coverage for BI to "you" or a "family member"; Exclusion 2.d. is amended in parallel.

**Rule:** Both apply only to BI liability **above the Arizona Financial Responsibility Law minimum** — limits up to that minimum remain covered.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A.2.d, §Part A new exclusion)

---

### 5. Part A — Insurance Under More Than One Policy (replaced)

**Rule:** Pro-rata sharing among applicable policies; insurance for an owned vehicle is excess over an auto-business person's insurance when that person is operating; insurance for a non-owned vehicle is excess except where the non-owned vehicle is insured under an auto-business policy and the operating insured is not the auto-business person/employee/agent (then primary).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part A — How Much We Pay)

---

### 6. Part B — Medical Payments: TNC and Delivery Exclusions

**What changes:** Exclusions 2.e. (public/livery, TNC in covered auto) and 2.f. (delivery in commerce in covered auto) are replaced.

**Rule:** Same TNC carve-outs as Part A — 2.e. carries shared-expense carpool plus ARS § 43-1201, A.4 volunteer activity; 2.f. carries volunteer-activity exception only.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part B.2.e, §Part B.2.f)

---

### 7. Part D — Physical Damage: Exclusions 7 and 8 (replaced)

**Exclusion 7 (public/livery, TNC):** applies to covered auto and non-owned auto; carve-outs: shared-expense carpool; ARS § 43-1201, A.4 volunteer activity.

**Exclusion 8 (delivery in commerce):** applies to covered auto and non-owned auto; carve-out: ARS § 43-1201, A.4 volunteer activity only.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Part D.7, §Part D.8)

---

### 8. Policy Conditions — Assignment (replaced)

**Rule:** No assignment without written consent. On named-insured death, the policy terms apply to (a) the surviving spouse, if a resident of the same household, treated as named insured; and (b) the legal representative of the deceased, only for legal responsibility related to maintenance/use of a covered auto.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Assignment)

---

### 9. Policy Conditions — Recoveries (Part B) (replaced)

**Rule:** When company payment exceeds $5,000 and the recipient recovers from another source, the company is entitled to the proceeds and may have a lien against recovery. Written notice of lien is sent to the insured, the court, alleged liable persons/entities, and their insurers.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Recoveries)

---

### 10. Policy Conditions — Termination (added)

A 7-day grace period applies to premium payment. The Termination provision comprises five sub-provisions:

**a. Automatic Termination** — Coverage terminates at end of period if the named insured notifies the company in writing of intent not to renew or continue.

**b. Cancellation** — Notice may be sent by certified mail, USPS certificate of mailing, or first-class with Intelligent Mail barcode tracking.
- **First 60 days:** any reason, 10-day notice.
- **After 60 days or on a renewal/continuation:** only seven enumerated grounds:
  1. Nonpayment (8-day post-due-date notice; effective on send date).
  2. Fraudulent misrepresentation.
  3. Suspended/revoked driver's license of named insured, household member, or regular driver.
  4. Physical or mental disability without physician/RN certificate. (Grounds c and d may be waived by named-driver exclusion.)
  5. Certain criminal convictions during policy period or prior 36 months — criminal negligence resulting in death/homicide/assault, DUI, leaving the scene, false statements on license application, reckless driving.
  6. Commercial use.
  7. Director-of-insurance suspension based on financially hazardous condition or solvency/Arizona-law concerns; rehabilitation/receivership.
- 10-day notice for non-payment-based cancellations. Pro-rata refund (90% if named insured cancels).

**c. Nonrenewal** — Written notice 45 days before nonrenewal effective date. Exception: nonpayment uses Offer To Renew procedure.

**d. Other Termination Conditions** — No cancellation or nonrenewal solely for residence location, age, race, color, religion, sex, national origin, or ancestry. May nonrenew for residence outside Arizona, or for failure to pay association dues if the policy was sold via association membership.

**e. Offer To Renew** — If the named insured fails to pay renewal premium within 7 days after due date, the company may terminate on or after the 8th day. Termination is effective the earlier of (1) date notice mailed, or (2) effective date of any other insurance obtained for the covered auto. Does not apply if the named insured has notified the company in writing of other insurance or non-renewal intent.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_AZ_form_PA-0102-07-18 §Conditions — Termination)
