# CIM 2003 06 20 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska

Product: [Motor Truck Cargo](../products/motor-truck-cargo.md)
State: [Alaska (AK)](../states/AK.md)
Amends: [CIM 7002 01 15](CIM-7002-01-15.md)
Mandatory in AK: Yes

---

## Overview

CIM 2003 06 20 is a one-page Alaska-specific amendatory endorsement to the CIM commercial inland marine cargo coverage forms, edition June 2020. It makes three changes: adds a domestic violence exception to the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion (mirroring the parallel PPA 0154 amendment for auto-ppa); amends the Subrogation condition for domestic violence scenarios; and replaces the Suit Against Us condition with an Alaska-specific version deferring to Alaska law.

Amendment 3 (Suit Against Us) applies in all coverage forms except Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage, Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal Liability Coverage.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20)

---

## Amendments

### 1. Perils Excluded — Criminal, Fraudulent, Dishonest, Or Illegal Acts: Domestic Violence Exception

**What changes:** Adds an exception for innocent insureds when loss is caused by domestic violence.

**Rule:** If an insured causes a covered loss through an act of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute), the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion does NOT apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who:
- Is the subject of the domestic violence, AND
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Files for a protective order under Alaska Statute 18.66 against the abuser, OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest in the property.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §1)

---

### 2. Other Conditions — Subrogation: Domestic Violence Carve-Out

**What changes:** Prevents an innocent insured in a domestic violence situation from waiving subrogation rights.

**Rule:** An innocent insured who is the subject of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute) cannot waive his or her right to recover. The company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for loss caused by an act of domestic violence.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §2)

---

### 3. Other Conditions — Suit Against Us (Replaced)

**Scope limitation:** Applies in all coverage forms except Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage, Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal Liability Coverage.

**What changes:** Base form's Suit Against Us condition (two-year contractual time limit) is deleted and replaced.

**Rule:** No suit may be brought against the company unless:
1. All terms of the coverage have been complied with, AND
2. The suit is brought within the time period prescribed by Alaska law for the commencement of such action

The base CIM 7002 condition used a fixed two-year contractual period starting from insured's first knowledge of loss. The AK endorsement defers to the Alaska statute of limitations instead.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AK_form_CIM-2003-06-20 §3)
