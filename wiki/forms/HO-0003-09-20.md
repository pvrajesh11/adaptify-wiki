# HO 0003 09 20 — Special Form

Product: [Homeowners HOBP](../products/homeowners-hobp.md)
Filed in: FL (amended by [HO 0809 02 22](HO-0809-02-22.md) — mandatory Florida endorsement)

---

## Overview

HO 0003 09 20 is the Special Form homeowners policy (HO-3), edition September 2020, 46 pages. It is an ISO-derived all-risks (open perils) homeowners policy providing:

- **Coverages A and B (Dwelling and Related Private Structures):** Open perils — all risks of direct physical loss subject to exclusions
- **Coverage C (Personal Property):** Named perils only
- **Coverage D (Additional Living Costs and Loss of Rent):** Consequential loss from property damage
- **Coverage L (Personal Liability):** Pays on behalf of insured for BI/PD from occurrences
- **Coverage M (Medical Payments to Others):** Pays medical expenses for non-insured injuries on or connected to insured premises

In Florida, this form is amended by mandatory endorsement HO 0809 02 22.

## Principal Property Coverages

### Coverage A — Residence
Covers the residence including attached additions, built-in components/fixtures, and adjacent building materials for construction/alteration/repair. Does not cover land, underground/surface water, trees/plants/shrubs/lawns (except incidental coverages), or grave markers/mausoleums.

### Coverage B — Related Private Structures
Covers unattached structures, fences, driveways, sidewalks, and permanently installed outdoor fixtures. Does not cover structures rented to non-tenants (except sole private garage use), used for business direction/operation, or used for business property storage.

### Coverage C — Personal Property
Covers personal property owned or used by an insured. Guest/domestic employee property may be covered at insured's option. Ten percent limit at other residences (or $1,000 minimum). Special per-occurrence limits: $250 money; $1,500 securities/personal records/watercraft/trailers/in-vehicle electronics/business electronics; $2,500 jewelry/furs/silverware/guns (theft only) and business property on described location. Not covered: motorized vehicles (subject to exceptions), aircraft, hovercrafts, separately insured property, animals.

### Coverage D — Additional Living Costs And Loss Of Rent
Increased living costs and fair rental value when described location made unfit for use by covered loss. Civil authority: up to two weeks when neighboring premises damaged by covered peril. Does not include lease cancellation costs.

## Perils Insured Against

### Coverages A and B — Open Perils
All risks of direct physical loss unless excluded under Coverage A/B-specific exclusions or general property exclusions.

**Coverage A/B-specific exclusions (ensuing loss rule applies):**
- Freezing of plumbing/heating/AC/sprinkler/water heater/appliance (unless heat maintained or water shut off)
- Freezing, thawing, pressure, weight of ice or water to: fences/patios/paved areas/pools; supporting structures; non-supporting retaining walls; docks/piers/wharves
- Theft during construction
- Vandalism when vacant 60+ consecutive days
- Continuous water seepage or humidity/moisture over weeks/months/years (unless unknown to all insureds)
- Collapse (except under Collapse incidental coverage)
- Settling/cracking/shrinking/bulging/expanding of structural components
- Birds, vermin, rodents, insects, insured-owned animals
- Agricultural or industrial smoke
- Pollutants (unless caused by a Coverage C peril)
- Wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, rust, corrosion, smog, root pressure

### Coverage C — Named Perils
Fire or Lightning; Windstorm or Hail; Explosion; Riot or Civil Commotion; Aircraft; Vehicles; Sudden and Accidental Smoke Damage; Volcanic Eruption; Vandalism or Malicious Mischief; Theft (with exclusions); Falling Objects; Weight of Ice/Snow/Sleet; Sudden and Accidental Tearing Apart/Cracking/Burning/Bulging of heating/AC/sprinkling/water heater; Accidental Discharge or Overflow of Water or Steam; Freezing; Sudden and Accidental Electrical Current Damage; Sinkhole Collapse.

## General Property Exclusions (Concurrent Causation)

1. Ordinance or Law
2. Civil Authority (except fire prevention orders)
3. Nuclear Hazard (direct fire from nuclear event covered)
4. War and Military Action
5. Neglect
6. Earth Movement (sinkhole collapse excluded here but covered under Coverage C perils; fire/explosion resulting from earth movement covered)
7. Water — flood, surface water, waves, tides, sewer backup, sump overflow, subsurface water, storm surge (fire/explosion from excluded water event covered; accidental plumbing discharge on described location not excluded)
8. Power Failure from off described location (on-premises direct loss covered)
9. Intentional Acts (by or at direction of any insured; applies to all insureds including non-involved) — **amended in FL by HO 0809 02 22 to add innocent insured/abuse exception**
10. Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, or Dry Rot not directly resulting from a covered peril

Also for Coverage A/B: Weather Conditions contributing to excluded causes; Errors/Omissions/Defects in development/planning/construction.

## Liability Coverages

### Coverage L — Personal Liability
All sums legally owed by an insured for BI or PD from a covered occurrence. Company defends at its expense; stops defending when limit paid by judgment or written settlement.

### Coverage M — Medical Payments To Others
Reasonable medical expenses (medical, surgical, X-ray, dental, ambulance, hospital, nursing, funeral; prosthetics; hearing aids; drugs; eyeglasses) incurred or medically determined within three years of accident. Does not apply to insured household members (except domestic employees). Applies to: persons on insured premises with permission; persons off-premises when BI arises from insured premises condition, insured activity, domestic employee's employment, or insured's animal.

### Incidental Liability Coverages
Business; Claims and Defense Cost (costs, bonds, lost earnings $250/day, post-judgment interest, prejudgment interest); Contracts; Damage to Property of Others ($1,000/occurrence, replacement cost, regardless of legal liability); First Aid Expense; Loss Assessment ($1,500/occurrence); Motorized Vehicles (dead storage, service vehicles, handicapped vehicles, off-road recreational vehicles on insured premises, low-speed battery vehicles, golf carts on golfing establishment or private residential community); Watercraft (sailing vessels, low-HP powerboats, outboards per ownership/acquisition rules).

### Liability Exclusions
Both L and M: War; aircraft/hovercraft; motorized vehicle/watercraft; vicarious child/minor liability for vehicles; professional services; business activities; non-insured premises; expected/intended/criminal/intentional malicious acts (except reasonable force); communicable disease; sexual molestation; physical/mental abuse; corporal punishment; controlled substances.

L only: BI to insured household members; contribution/reimbursement for insured BI; contractual liability (except Contracts incidental); owned property damage; cost to prevent damage to owned property; rented/in-care property damage (except fire/smoke/explosion); domestic employee disease notice requirement (36 months); workers' comp covered injuries; nuclear energy liability insured; HOA assessments (except Loss Assessment).

M only: Insured/regular household residents; workers' comp; domestic employee off-premises not in employment; nuclear.

## Conditions

### All Coverages
Assignment (requires written consent); Cancellation and Nonrenewal (10-day notice first 59 days or nonpayment; 30-day notice other reasons after 60 days; 30-day nonrenewal notice; pro rata return); Change/Modification/Waiver (written only; automatic broad coverage adoption within 60 days); Conformity with Statute; Death; Inspections; Misrepresentation/Concealment/Fraud (no coverage for any insured including non-involved); **Subrogation** (company may require assignment; no coverage if insured impairs recovery; insured may waive in writing before loss; does not apply to Cov M or Damage to Property of Others) — **amended in FL by HO 0809 02 22 to prohibit waiver for innocent insured in domestic abuse situations**.

### Property Only
Abandonment; Appraisal (20 days to select appraisers; 15 days to select umpire; agreement of any two of three sets loss); Loss Payable Clause; Mortgage Clause (payment to mortgagee and insured; mortgagee protected if it notified, paid premium, submitted proof of loss; 10-day cancellation notice to mortgagee; subrogation rights against mortgagee if company pays mortgagee); No Benefit to Bailee; Policy Period; Recoveries; **Suit Against Us** — base: two years after loss (statutory fallback if law makes invalid) — **amended in FL by HO 0809 02 22 to use Florida statutory period only**.

### Liability Only
Bankruptcy of Insured; Duties of Injured Person (Cov M); Policy Period; Suit Against Us (liability terms complied with; liability fixed by final judgment or written agreement).
