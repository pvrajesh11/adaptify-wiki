# CIM 2001 08 15 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama

Product: [Motor Truck Cargo](../products/motor-truck-cargo.md)
State: [Alabama (AL)](../states/AL.md)
Amends: CIM 7002 01 15 (Motor Truck Cargo Coverage Form)
Mandatory in AL: Yes

---

## Overview

CIM 2001 08 15 is a one-page Alabama-specific amendatory endorsement to the Motor Truck Cargo policy. Edition August 2015. It makes three changes to the base policy: adds an abuse exception to the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion for innocent insureds under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act; amends the Subrogation condition for abuse situations; and replaces the Suit Against Us condition in most coverage forms with a statute-of-limitations-based version.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15)

---

## Amendments

### 1. Perils Excluded — Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts: Abuse Exception

**What changes:** Adds an exception for innocent insureds when loss is caused by an act of abuse.

**Rule:** If an insured causes a loss through an act of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute), the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion does NOT apply to the innocent insured who:
- Is the subject of the abuse, AND
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Either files a complaint under the **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** (and does not voluntarily dismiss it), OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment to a mortgagee or holder of a legal secured interest.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §1)

---

### 2. Other Conditions — Subrogation: Abuse Carve-Out

**What changes:** Innocent insured subject to abuse cannot waive the right to recover.

**Rule:** Company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for any loss caused by an act of abuse. The innocent insured's right to recover cannot be waived.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §2)

---

### 3. Other Conditions — Suit Against Us (Replacement)

**Scope:** Applies in all coverage forms **except**: Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage, Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal Liability Coverage.

**What changes:** Deletes the base form's Suit Against Us condition and replaces it with an Alabama-specific version.

**Rule:** No suit may be brought against the company unless:
1. All terms of the coverage have been complied with, AND
2. The suit is brought within the **time period prescribed by law** for commencement of such action

Base form used a fixed contractual time period; Alabama endorsement defers to the applicable statute of limitations.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_motor-truck-cargo_AL_form_CIM-2001-08-15 §3)

## Comparison to Analogous AL Endorsements

The abuse provisions in this endorsement parallel those in the AL Auto PPA endorsement (PPA 0101 03 18) and AL Homeowners endorsement (HO 0801 09 20), applying the same Alabama Protection From Abuse Act framework to MTC coverage.

| Provision | CIM 2001 08 15 (MTC) | PPA 0101 03 18 (Auto PPA) |
|---|---|---|
| Innocent insured trigger | "act of abuse" (Alabama statute) | "act of abuse" (Alabama statute) |
| Protective action required | Complaint under AL Protection From Abuse Act (not dismissed) OR warrant + cooperate | Same |
| Subrogation | Cannot waive; company retains rights | Same |
| Suit Against Us | "prescribed by law" | "prescribed by law" |
| Renewal notice | Not included | 30 days prior |
