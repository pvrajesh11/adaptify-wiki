# PPA 0154 01 20 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska

Product: [Auto PPA](../products/auto-ppa.md)
State: [Alaska (AK)](../states/AK.md)
Amends: [PPA 0001 07 16](PPA-0001-07-16.md)
Mandatory in AK: Yes

---

## Overview

PPA 0154 01 20 is a one-page Alaska-specific amendatory endorsement to the Personal Automobile Policy (PPA 0001). Edition January 2020. It makes four changes to the base policy: adds a domestic violence exception to the Part D intentional acts exclusion, amends the subrogation condition for domestic violence situations, adds an AK-required renewal notice provision, and replaces the Suit Against Us condition with an Alaska-specific version.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0154-01-20)

---

## Amendments

### 1. Part D — Intentional Acts Exclusion: Domestic Violence Exception

**What changes:** Adds an exception for innocent insureds when loss is caused by domestic violence.

**Rule:** If an insured causes a covered Part D loss through an act of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute), the intentional acts exclusion does NOT apply to the innocent insured who:
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Files for a protective order under Alaska Statute 18.66, OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured is limited to that person's insurable interest, less any payment to mortgagee or holder of a legal secured interest in the property.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0154-01-20 §1)

---

### 2. Subrogation Condition: Domestic Violence Carve-Out

**What changes:** Innocent insured in a domestic violence situation cannot waive their right to recover.

**Rule:** Company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for any loss caused by an act of domestic violence. The innocent insured's subrogation rights cannot be waived.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0154-01-20 §2)

---

### 3. Renewal Notice Condition (Added)

**What changes:** Adds an Alaska-required renewal notice requirement not in the base form.

**Rule:** If the company intends to renew the policy with a premium increase (other than an increase due to a change at the named insured's request), the company must give at least **30 days' prior notice** before the renewal effective date.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0154-01-20 §3)

---

### 4. Suit Against Us Condition (Replaced)

**What changes:** Base form's Suit Against Us condition is deleted and replaced.

**Rule:** No suit may be brought against the company unless:
1. All terms that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with, AND
2. The suit is brought within the time period prescribed by **Alaska law** for commencement of such action

Base form used a fixed contractual time period; Alaska endorsement defers to Alaska statute of limitations instead.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0154-01-20 §4)
