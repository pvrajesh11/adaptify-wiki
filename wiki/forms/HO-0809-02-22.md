# HO 0809 02 22 — Amendatory Endorsement — Florida

Product: [Homeowners HOBP](../products/homeowners-hobp.md)
State: [Florida (FL)](../states/FL.md)
Amends: [HO 0003 09 20](HO-0003-09-20.md)
Mandatory in FL: Yes (Rule 4.2)

---

## Overview

HO 0809 02 22 is a one-page Florida mandatory amendatory endorsement to the homeowners Special Form, edition February 2022. It makes four targeted changes to the base policy driven by Florida statutory and regulatory requirements:

1. Adds an innocent insured exception to the Intentional Acts property exclusion for domestic abuse situations
2. Amends the Subrogation condition to prevent innocent insured in abuse situations from waiving recovery rights
3. Adds a renewal premium increase notice requirement
4. Replaces the two-year Suit Against Us property condition with the Florida statutory period

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22)

---

## Amendment 1 — Intentional Acts Exclusion: Innocent Insured/Domestic Abuse Exception

**Where:** Exclusions That Apply To Property Coverages — Intentional Acts

**Change:** An exception is added to the exclusion.

**Rule:** If the loss is caused by an act of **abuse, as defined by Florida statute**, committed by or at the direction of an insured, the Intentional Acts exclusion does **not** apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who:
- Is the subject of the abuse, AND
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act that caused the loss, AND
- Either:
  - Files a complaint under the **Florida Protection From Abuse Act** against the abuser and does not voluntarily dismiss the complaint, OR
  - Seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest for the act causing the loss and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that insured's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest in the property.

**Subject to all other policy terms.**

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22 §1)

---

## Amendment 2 — Subrogation: Domestic Abuse Carve-Out

**Where:** Policy Conditions — Conditions That Apply To All Coverages — Subrogation

**Change:** An addition is made to the Subrogation condition.

**Rule:** An innocent insured who is the subject of abuse, as defined by Florida statute, **cannot waive** his or her right to recover. The company retains all rights under the Subrogation condition with regard to recovering, up to the amount paid, for any loss caused by an act of abuse.

**Practical effect:** The base policy permits an insured to waive subrogation rights in writing before a loss without affecting coverage. This amendment removes that waiver ability specifically when the innocent insured is a domestic abuse victim, preserving the company's ability to subrogate against the abuser.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22 §2)

---

## Amendment 3 — Renewal Notice Condition (Added)

**Where:** Policy Conditions — Conditions That Apply To All Coverages (new condition added)

**Change:** A new condition is added.

**Rule:** If the company intends to renew this policy subject to a **premium increase**, other than an increase due to a change made at the named insured's request, the company must give the named insured notice at least **30 days** before the renewal effective date.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22 §3)

---

## Amendment 4 — Suit Against Us: Property Coverages (Replaced)

**Where:** Policy Conditions — Conditions Applicable To Property Coverages Only — Suit Against Us

**Change:** The base form's Suit Against Us property condition is deleted and replaced.

**New text:** No suit may be brought against the company unless all of the terms that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with and the suit is brought within the **time period prescribed by law** for the commencement of such action.

**What this changes:** The base HO 0003 09 20 provides a two-year contractual Suit Against Us period for property coverages (with a statutory fallback if state law renders the two-year period invalid). The Florida endorsement removes the two-year contractual period entirely, substituting the Florida statutory period directly. Under Florida Statute §95.11, the applicable period for written contracts is generally five years.

**Note:** The Suit Against Us condition for Liability Coverages (under Conditions Applicable To Liability Coverages Only) is **not** amended.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_FL_form_HO-0809-02-22 §4)
