# PA 0104 01 25 — Amendatory Endorsement — California

Product: [Auto PPA](../products/auto-ppa.md)
State: [California (CA)](../states/CA.md)
Amends: [PA 0001 07 16](PPA-0001-07-16.md)
Bureau: AAIS
Mandatory in CA: Yes

---

## Overview

PA 0104 01 25 is a three-page California-specific amendatory endorsement to the AAIS Personal Automobile Policy. Edition January 2025. It amends Definitions ("you"/"your" and "family member"), Part A (Liability), and Policy Conditions (deletes Part B Recoveries item a. and adds a Termination provision). The distinctive theme is explicit recognition of **registered domestic partnership under California law** in core definitions; an intra-family liability exclusion that is conditional on indemnification accruing to the named insured or a family member rather than categorical; and California-specific cancellation grounds and notice periods.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25)

---

## Amendments

### 1. Definitions — "You" / "Your" (replaced)

**Rule:** "You" and "your" mean (a) the named insured; and (b) the spouse — or a party who has entered into a domestic partnership recognized by California law with the named insured — if a resident of the same household.

Coverage extends to a former spouse or domestic partner who ceases to be a resident of the named insured's household during the policy period (or before inception), but only until the earliest of:
1. 90 days after the change in residency,
2. The effective date of another policy showing that person as a named insured, or
3. The end of the policy period.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Definitions.1)

---

### 2. Definitions — "Family Member" (replaced)

**Rule:** A "family member" is a resident of the household who is (a) related to the insured by blood, marriage, **registered domestic partnership under California law**, or adoption; or (b) the insured's ward or foster child.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Definitions.2)

---

### 3. Part A — Liability: Intra-Family BI Exclusion (added)

**Rule:** No Liability Coverage for any insured for "bodily injury" to "you" or a "family member" **when the benefits of such indemnification accrue either directly or indirectly to "you" or a "family member"**.

The California intra-family exclusion is **conditional on accrual**, not categorical: BI to a family member remains covered when the indemnification benefit does not flow back to the named insured or a family member.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Part A.1)

---

### 4. Part A — Insurance Under More Than One Policy (replaced)

**Rule:**
- **a.** Pro-rata sharing among self-insurance, certificates of deposit, bonds, and other liability insurance applicable to an auto accident. Except as in c., insurance for a non-owned vehicle (including a temporary substitute for a covered auto) is excess over other collectible insurance.
- **b.** Pro-rata sharing of defense costs when both primary and excess policies apply.
- **c.** Insurance for a non-owned vehicle is **primary** when (1) that vehicle is insured under an auto-business policy and (2) the operating insured is not the auto-business person/employee/agent.
- **d.** Insurance for an owned vehicle is excess over (1) an auto-business person's insurance when that person is operating, and (2) an owner/tenant/lessee of premises on which a loading/unloading loss arises.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Part A.2)

---

### 5. Policy Conditions — Recoveries (Part B), item a. deleted

**What changes:** Removes one sub-item of the base form's Part B Recoveries provision (a lien-related sub-item).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Conditions.1)

---

### 6. Policy Conditions — Termination (added)

**a. Automatic Termination** — Coverage terminates (1) at end of period when the company offered to renew or continue and the named insured did not accept (failure to pay required premium when due is non-acceptance); or (2) on the effective date of any other insurance obtained on a covered auto, but only with respect to similar insurance.

**b. Cancellation**
- The named insured may cancel by returning the policy or giving written notice with a future stop date.
- The company may cancel by mailed/delivered written notice; proof of mailing is sufficient. Policy period ends on the effective date of cancellation.
- The company may cancel only if:
  1. Required premium has not been paid when due.
  2. Fraud in the presentation or pursuit of a claim.
  3. Substantial increase in the hazard insured.
  4. Material misrepresentation of safety record / annual miles driven / years of driving experience / record of prior automobile insurance claims / any other factor found by the Commissioner of Insurance to have a substantial relationship to the risk of loss — with a 20-day cure window after notice receipt.
  5. Driver's license of named insured, household member, or regular driver suspended or revoked during the policy period or 180 days prior to the most recent renewal/effective date if not previously disclosed. Cancellation does not become effective if the suspension is removed before the cancellation effective date.
- **Nonpayment notice:** provided after the due date, with at least a 10-day cure period and a stated cancellation date and reason.
- **Other-cause cancellation:** at least 20 days' notice.
- Pro-rata return premium; payment or tender of unearned premium is not a condition of cancellation.

**c. Nonrenewal** — At least 30 days' mailed/delivered written notice; proof of mailing is sufficient. Policy period ends on the nonrenewal effective date. The company may nonrenew:
- Beginning 6 months after the original effective date if the policy period is less than 6 months;
- At end of policy period if the period is at least 6 months but less than one year; or
- At each anniversary of the original effective date if the period is one year or more.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-18_auto-ppa_CA_form_PA-0104-01-25 §Conditions.2)
