# PPA 0101 03 18 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama

Product: [Auto PPA](../products/auto-ppa.md)
State: [Alabama (AL)](../states/AL.md)
Amends: [PPA 0001 07 16](PPA-0001-07-16.md)
Mandatory in AL: Yes

---

## Overview

PPA 0101 03 18 is a one-page Alabama-specific amendatory endorsement to the Personal Automobile Policy (PPA 0001). Edition March 2018. It makes four changes to the base policy: adds an abuse exception to the Part D intentional acts exclusion under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act, amends the subrogation condition for abuse situations, adds an AL-required renewal notice provision, and replaces the Suit Against Us condition with an Alabama-specific version.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18)

---

## Amendments

### 1. Part D — Intentional Acts Exclusion: Abuse Exception

**What changes:** Adds an exception for innocent insureds when loss is caused by abuse.

**Rule:** If an insured causes a covered Part D loss through an act of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute), the intentional acts exclusion does NOT apply to the innocent insured who:
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act, AND
- Files a complaint under the **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** against the abuser and does not voluntarily dismiss the complaint, OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured is limited to that person's insurable interest, less any payment to mortgagee or holder of a legal secured interest in the property.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §1)

---

### 2. Subrogation Condition: Abuse Carve-Out

**What changes:** Innocent insured in an abuse situation cannot waive their right to recover.

**Rule:** Company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for any loss caused by an act of abuse. The innocent insured's subrogation rights cannot be waived.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §2)

---

### 3. Renewal Notice Condition (Added)

**What changes:** Adds an Alabama-required renewal notice requirement not in the base form.

**Rule:** If the company intends to renew the policy with a premium increase (other than an increase due to a change at the named insured's request), the company must give at least **30 days' prior notice** before the renewal effective date.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §3)

---

### 4. Suit Against Us Condition (Replaced)

**What changes:** Base form's Suit Against Us condition is deleted and replaced.

**Rule:** No suit may be brought against the company unless:
1. All terms that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with, AND
2. The suit is brought within the time period **prescribed by law** for commencement of such action

Base form used a fixed contractual time period; Alabama endorsement defers to applicable statute of limitations instead.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AL_form_PPA-0101-03-18 §4)

## Comparison to AK Endorsement (PPA 0154 01 20)

| Provision | AL (PPA 0101 03 18) | AK (PPA 0154 01 20) |
|---|---|---|
| Innocent insured trigger | "act of abuse" (Alabama statute) | "act of domestic violence" (Alaska statute) |
| Innocent insured condition | Complaint under Alabama Protection From Abuse Act (not voluntarily dismissed) OR seek warrant + cooperate | Protective order under AK Statute 18.66 OR seek warrant + cooperate |
| Subrogation | Cannot waive; company retains rights for "act of abuse" | Cannot waive; company retains rights for "act of domestic violence" |
| Renewal notice | 30 days prior | 30 days prior |
| Suit Against Us | "prescribed by law" | "prescribed by Alaska law" |
