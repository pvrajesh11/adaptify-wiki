# PPA 0001 07 16 — Personal Automobile Policy

Product: [Auto PPA](../products/auto-ppa.md)
Filed in: AK (amended by [PPA 0154 01 20](PPA-0154-01-20.md) — page not yet created)

---

## Overview

PPA 0001 07 16 is the base Personal Automobile Policy form, edition July 2016. It provides four coverage parts under a single policy document:

| Part | Coverage |
|---|---|
| A | Liability — bodily injury and property damage |
| B | Medical Payments |
| C | Uninsured Motorist |
| D | Physical Damage — Comprehensive and Collision |

A separate premium is charged for each vehicle and coverage. State-specific amendments are applied via endorsement (in Alaska: PPA 0154 01 20).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0001-07-16)

## Coverage Territory

United States, its territories and possessions, Puerto Rico, Canada, and while the vehicle is being transported between any of these. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0001-07-16 Conditions)

## Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Covered Auto | Vehicle on declarations; newly acquired auto (4-day liability / 14-day all coverages); temporary substitute auto; owned trailer |
| Family Member | Resident of named insured's household related by blood, marriage, or adoption; includes ward/foster child |
| Newly Acquired Auto | Additional or replacement private passenger auto, pickup, or van; 4 days liability only or 14 days all coverages |
| Occupying | In, upon, getting in/on, getting out/off |
| Performance Activity | Competitive or timed event including racing, rallying, autocross; excludes parades and highway safety |
| Temporary Substitute Auto | Non-owned vehicle used while covered auto is out of normal use for breakdown, repair, servicing, loss, or destruction |
| Transportation Network Program | Online-enabled app connecting customers with drivers for prearranged transportation for compensation |

## Part A — Liability

### Insuring Agreement

Pays compensatory damages (including prejudgment interest in judgment) for which an insured is legally liable due to bodily injury or property damage from an auto accident. Company has duty to settle or defend at its option; defense is at company expense by company-chosen counsel. Defense duty ends when company pays the applicable limit via judgment or written settlement.

### Supplemental Payments (outside the limit)

- Up to $250 for bail bonds required by an auto accident with resulting covered BI/PD
- Appeal bond premiums and bonds to release attachments (up to applicable limit)
- Up to $250/day per person for actual lost earnings at company request
- Post-judgment interest until company offers to pay up to the limit
- Other reasonable expenses at company request

### Who Is Insured

- Named insured or family member for any auto or trailer
- Any person using a covered auto
- Any person/entity vicariously liable for a covered person's acts
- Any person/entity vicariously liable for named insured/family member in a non-covered auto (not when they own or hire the auto)

### Key Exclusions

- Expected, intended, or directed bodily injury or property damage
- Vehicle use without reasonable entitlement (exception: family member using owned covered auto)
- Property damage to property owned or transported by the insured
- Property damage to property rented to or in the care of insured (exception: residence or private garage)
- Employee bodily injury in course of employment
- **Transportation network program** — any period logged in as driver, with or without passengers (exception: shared-expense carpool, volunteer/charitable with no or expense-reimbursement-only compensation)
- **Delivery/transport of food or goods in commerce** — any period logged into TNP as driver (same exception)
- Auto business (exception: named insured/family member using own covered auto)
- Non-auto business use (exception: farming/ranching, private passenger autos, pickups, vans, trailers)
- Nuclear energy (if also covered under nuclear energy policy)
- Performance activity
- Motorcycles, sub-four-wheel vehicles, off-road vehicles (exception: medical emergency, trailers, non-owned golf carts)
- Non-covered auto owned by or regularly available to named insured or family member
- Covered auto enrolled in personal vehicle sharing program while used by non-insured
- Punitive or exemplary damages

### Limits

- BI per person: all damages (care, loss of services, death) for one person per accident; includes all derivative claims
- BI per accident (subject to per person): all BI for two or more persons per accident
- PD per accident: all property damage per accident
- Limits apply regardless of number of insureds, claims, vehicles, or vehicles involved
- Coverage for non-owned or temporary substitute vehicle is **excess** over other collectible insurance

## Part B — Medical Payments

### Insuring Agreement

Pays reasonable expenses for necessary medical and funeral services from bodily injury caused by auto accident. Expenses must be incurred for services rendered within 3 years of the accident.

### Who Is Insured

- Named insured or family member while occupying any motor vehicle or trailer, or as pedestrian struck by one
- Any other person while occupying a covered auto

### Key Exclusions

- Bodily injury in course of employment where workers' comp applies
- Nuclear, radiation, war
- Occupying a motorcycle or sub-four-wheel vehicle
- Vehicle used as residence or premises
- TNP/livery use while logged in as driver; delivery/transport in commerce
- Non-auto business use in a business (exception: private passenger auto, pickup/van, trailer)
- Performance activity
- Owned or regularly available non-covered vehicle

### Limit

Per insured per accident. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0001-07-16 Part B)

## Part C — Uninsured Motorist

### Insuring Agreement

Pays compensatory damages for bodily injury to which an insured is legally entitled from owner/operator of an uninsured motor vehicle. Company may settle or defend at its option.

### Key Exclusions

- Occupying/struck by a motor vehicle owned by insured and not insured for UM under this policy
- Family member struck by named-insured-owned vehicle insured for UM on a primary basis under another policy
- Settlement that prejudices company's right of recovery
- Vehicle use without reasonable entitlement
- TNP/livery use while logged in as driver; delivery/transport in commerce

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0001-07-16 Part C)

> **AK mandatory offering:** Uninsured Motorist Coverage is a mandatory offering in Alaska — named insured must reject in writing to waive. See [AK — Coverage Options](../product-states/auto-ppa-AK/rating-rules/coverage-options.md).

## Part D — Physical Damage

### Insuring Agreement

- **Comprehensive:** Direct and accidental loss to covered auto not caused by collision; includes contact with bird or animal, glass breakage.
- **Collision:** Direct and accidental loss caused by collision with another object or overturn.
- Automatically included with Part D: child safety seat replacement; transportation expenses.

### Settlement

Settled at the lesser of actual cash value, cost of repair, or applicable limit. ACV determined considering depreciation and physical condition.

### Key Exclusions

- Expected, intended, or directed loss
- Mechanical breakdown, road wear (exception: total loss from theft)
- Nuclear, radiation, war
- Electronic equipment not permanently installed
- Custom furnishings/equipment in or on pickup/van (exception: factory-installed)
- TNP/livery; delivery/transport in commerce
- Performance activity
- Personal vehicle sharing program while used by non-insured

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_auto-ppa_AK_form_PPA-0001-07-16 Part D)

## Conditions

| Condition | Summary |
|---|---|
| Duties After Accident or Loss | Prompt notice; cooperate with investigation/defense/settlement; send legal papers; submit to examination under oath |
| Legal Action Against Us | Must fully comply with policy terms before suing company |
| Subrogation | Insured must preserve and transfer subrogation rights to company |
| Policy Period and Territory | Per declarations; US, territories, PR, Canada, in-transit |
| Termination | Named insured may cancel; company may cancel; nonrenewal notice requirements apply |
| Transfer of Interest | Policy transfers invalid without written company consent |
