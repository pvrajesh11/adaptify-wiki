# HO 0854 02 21 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska

Product: [Homeowners HOBP](../products/homeowners-hobp.md)
State: [Alaska (AK)](../states/AK.md)
Mandatory in AK: Yes — supersedes HO 0814 (state pages Rev 01 15 listed HO 0814; this February 2021 edition replaces it per the 2026 SERFF filing)
Pages: 6

---

## Overview

HO 0854 02 21 is a six-page Alaska-specific amendatory endorsement to the Homeowners HOBP policy, edition February 2021. It makes 18 numbered amendments covering definitions, property and liability coverage modifications, claims handling procedures, and policy conditions. Filed and approved effective 01/01/2026 under SERFF SRFF-TEST-AK-HO-0001.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21; 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_filing-summary)

---

## Amendments

### 1. Pollutant Definition — Revised

Replaces the multistate "pollutant" definition. AK definition adds "radioactive" irritants/contaminants and expands "waste" to include recycled, reclaimed, and reconditioned materials.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §1)

### 2. Credit Card Coverage — Attorney Fee Provision

Amends Incidental Property Coverages — Credit Card; EFT; Forgery; Counterfeit Money. In any suit the company defends, defense expense includes attorney's fees for which an insured is liable under **Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82**, capped at the 82(b)(1) contested cases schedule applied to the applicable limit.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §2)

### 3. HO 0003 — Settling/Cracking Exclusion: Replaced

Replaces the HO 0003 exclusion with an enumerated list (bulkheads, ceilings, floors, footings, foundations, patios, paved areas, roofs, walls) and adds cause-neutral language (natural, manmade, or combination).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §3)

### 4. HO 0005 — Settling/Cracking Exclusion: Replaced

Same as Amendment 3, applied to HO 0005 (Coverage A, B, and C Exclusions).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §4)

### 5. All Forms Except HO 0003/0005 — Property Exclusions Intro: Replaced

Replaces the first two introductory paragraphs of property exclusions to add the efficient proximate / dominant cause rule: coverage cannot be denied if a covered peril is the dominant cause, even if a secondary excluded cause is in the chain.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §5)

### 6. HO 0003 and HO 0005 — Property Exclusions Intro: Replaced

Same dominant cause language applied to HO 0003 and HO 0005 (at item 1. of their exclusions section).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §6)

### 7. Water Exclusion — Item 5) Replaced

Expands water exclusion item 5): exclusions apply whether caused by natural or manmade causes, and apply to water that overtops, escapes, is released from, or is discharged from a dam, levee, dike, floodgate, or similar water retention/control device.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §7)

### 8. Liability — Claims And Defense Cost: Attorney Fee Provision

Amends Claims And Defense Cost under Incidental Liability Coverages identically to Amendment 2 (Civil Procedure Rule 82 attorney fees, capped at 82(b)(1) schedule).

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §8)

### 9. War Exclusion (Coverage L and M) — Enumerated

Replaces exclusion a. for Coverage L and M with a three-part enumeration: (1) war/undeclared/civil war; (2) warlike action by military force; (3) insurrection/rebellion/revolution/usurped power. Nuclear weapon discharge deemed warlike even if accidental.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §9)

### 10. Examination Under Oath — Right to Counsel

Adds to the EUO provision: an insured has the right to have legal representation present during examination under oath.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §10)

### 11. Damage to Property of Others — Sworn Statement Timing

Replaces Other Duties — Damage To Property Of Others: sworn statement of loss must be provided within **60 days** after the company's request.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §11)

### 12. Coverage L — Anti-Stacking Rule

Replaces Insurance Under More Than One Policy for Coverage L with a proportional sharing rule: company pays only its share based on ratio of this policy's Coverage L limit to total insurance covering the loss.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §12)

### 13. Payment of Loss — 30 Working Day Requirement

Replaces property loss payment provision: undisputed portion paid within **30 working days** after acceptable proof of loss received and amount agreed in writing; disputed portion within 30 working days after final judgment or appraisal award filing.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §13)

### 14. Cancellation and Nonrenewal — Replaced

Entirely replaces the cancellation/nonrenewal condition with AK-specific rules:

**Mid-term (< 60 days):** Company may cancel for any reason.

**After 60 days / renewals:** Company may cancel only at anniversary date except for:
- Nonpayment of premium → 20 days notice
- Conviction of crime with element increasing hazard; fraud/misrepresentation → 10 days notice
- Grossly negligent act; physical change making property uninsurable → 30 days notice
- Entire abandonment → 30 days notice + lender notification

**Nonrenewal:** 20 days notice (not required if company manifested renewal willingness, expiring premium unpaid, or renewal premium not paid).

Named insureds age 70+: notice also mailed/transmitted to designated person.

Unearned premium returned pro rata: timing varies by cancellation reason.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §14)

### 15. Misrepresentation, Concealment, Or Fraud — Replaced

Replaces the misrepresentation condition with a three-part test: (a) fraudulent; (b) material to risk acceptance or hazard assumed; (c) would have caused company to not issue or modify the policy. Applies to all insureds including those not involved in the misrepresentation.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §15)

### 16. Premium Or Coverage Changes — Notice Requirement Added

New condition: company must provide at least **20 days' advance written notice** before expiration if it intends to increase renewal premium by more than **10%** (excluding coverage/exposure increases) or materially restrict/reduce coverage not requested by the named insured. Notice to named insured and agent/broker of record.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §16)

### 17. Appraisal — Replaced

Replaces appraisal condition with AK-specific procedure:
- Appraiser selection: within 10 days of written demand
- Loss statement: within 15 days of umpire selection (unless extended)
- Umpire selection: by judge of court of record in state of described location if appraisers disagree
- Costs: each party pays its own counsel/adjuster; other expenses allocated by umpire
- Scope: appraisal may not interpret policy terms, determine causation, or determine coverage

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §17)

### 18. Suit Against Us — 3 Years from Denial

Replaces Suit Against Us (Property Coverages): suit must be brought within **3 years after the date the claim was denied** (after complying with all policy terms). AK law governs if it requires a different period.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AK_form_HO-0854-02-21 §18)
