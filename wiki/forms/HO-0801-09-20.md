# HO 0801 09 20 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama

Product: [Homeowners HOBP](../products/homeowners-hobp.md)
State: [Alabama (AL)](../states/AL.md)
Mandatory in AL: Yes (per 01/01/2026 filing; see ⚠️ note below)
Pages: 1

---

## Overview

HO 0801 09 20 is an Alabama-specific amendatory endorsement to the Homeowners policy. Edition September 2020, introduced in the 01/01/2026 AL filing. It makes four changes: adds an abuse exception to the property intentional acts exclusion under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act, amends the subrogation condition for abuse situations, adds an AL-required renewal notice provision, and replaces the Suit Against Us property condition with an Alabama-specific version deferring to the statutory limitations period.

⚠️ The AL state pages (Rev 01 15) list HO 0814 (not HO 0801 09 20) as the mandatory Alabama amendatory endorsement. HO 0801 09 20 was introduced in the January 2026 filing and likely supersedes HO 0814. Verify which form applies to policies issued under the 2026 filing versus earlier policy years.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_form_HO-0801-09-20)

## Amendments

### 1. Intentional Acts Exclusion — Abuse Exception

**Section amended:** Exclusions That Apply To Property Coverages — Intentional Acts

**What changes:** Adds an exception for innocent insureds when loss is caused by an act of abuse.

**Rule:** If an insured causes a covered property loss through an act of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute), the intentional acts exclusion does NOT apply to the innocent insured who:
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act causing the loss, AND
- Either files a complaint under the **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** against the abuser (and does not voluntarily dismiss the complaint), OR seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest and cooperates in the prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest in the property.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_form_HO-0801-09-20 §1)

---

### 2. Subrogation Condition — Abuse Carve-Out

**Section amended:** Policy Conditions — Conditions That Apply To All Coverages — Subrogation

**What changes:** Innocent insured in an abuse situation cannot waive subrogation rights.

**Rule:** Company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for any loss caused by an act of abuse. The innocent insured's right to recover cannot be waived.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_form_HO-0801-09-20 §2)

---

### 3. Renewal Notice Condition (Added)

**Section added to:** Policy Conditions — Conditions That Apply To All Coverages

**What changes:** Adds an Alabama-required renewal notice requirement not present in the base policy.

**Rule:** If the company intends to renew the policy subject to a premium increase — other than an increase due to a change made at the named insured's request — the company must give at least **30 days' prior notice** before the renewal is effective.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_form_HO-0801-09-20 §3)

---

### 4. Suit Against Us Condition (Replaced)

**Section amended:** Policy Conditions — Conditions Applicable To Property Coverages Only — Suit Against Us

**What changes:** Base form's Suit Against Us condition is deleted and replaced with an Alabama-specific version.

**Rule:** No suit may be brought against the company unless:
1. All terms that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with, AND
2. The suit is brought within the time period **prescribed by law** for the commencement of such action.

The base form uses a fixed contractual time period; the Alabama endorsement substitutes the applicable statute of limitations under Alabama law.

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_AL_form_HO-0801-09-20 §4)
