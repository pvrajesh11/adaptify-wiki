# CIM 2003 06 20 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska

## Source Document Summary

Alaska-specific amendatory endorsement to the CIM commercial inland marine cargo coverage forms. One page
(Page 1 of 1), edition 06 20 (June 2020). Makes three changes: (1) adds domestic violence exception to the
Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion, (2) amends the Subrogation condition for domestic
violence scenarios, and (3) replaces the Suit Against Us condition with an Alaska-specific version.

Applies to all coverage forms except Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage, Motor Truck
Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal Liability Coverage
(Amendment 3 scope restriction).

---

## Extracted Content

### Amendment 1 — Perils Excluded: Criminal, Fraudulent, Dishonest, Or Illegal Acts (Domestic Violence Exception)

The Criminal, Fraudulent, Dishonest, Or Illegal Acts exclusion is amended to add:

If a loss is caused by an act of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute) committed by or at the direction of an insured, the exclusion does NOT apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who is the subject of the domestic violence and who did not cooperate with or contribute to the act.

**Conditions for innocent insured protection:**
- Must file for a protective order under Alaska Statute 18.66 against the abuser, OR
- Seek a warrant for the abuser's arrest for the act causing the loss AND cooperate in the prosecution of the abuser

**Payment limitation:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest in the property.

### Amendment 2 — Other Conditions: Subrogation (Domestic Violence)

The Subrogation condition is amended to add:

An innocent insured who is the subject of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute) cannot waive his or her right to recover. The company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for any loss caused by an act of domestic violence.

### Amendment 3 — Other Conditions: Suit Against Us (Replaced)

Scope: applies in all coverage forms EXCEPT: Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage,
Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal
Liability Coverage.

The Suit Against Us condition is deleted and replaced with:

> Suit Against Us — No one may bring a legal action against us under this coverage unless:
> (a) all of the terms of this coverage have been complied with; and
> (b) the suit has been brought within the time period prescribed by Alaska law for the commencement of such action.

The base CIM 7002 condition used a fixed two-year contractual period; the AK endorsement defers to the Alaska statute of limitations instead.
