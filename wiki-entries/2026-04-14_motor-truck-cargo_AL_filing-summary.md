## Key State-Specific Elements

The filing introduces two Alabama-specific provisions via CIM 2001 08 15:

1. **Abuse protection** — exception to the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion for innocent insureds when loss is caused by an act of abuse as defined by Alabama statute. Requires the innocent insured to take protective action under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act. Payment capped at insurable interest.
2. **Suit Against Us** — deleted and replaced with a version that substitutes the contractual time period with the time period **prescribed by law** (applicable statute of limitations). Applies in all coverage forms except Cold Storage Locker, Contingent Cargo, Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability, Riggers' Legal Liability, and Warehouse Legal Liability.

Additionally, the Subrogation condition is amended: an innocent insured who is the subject of abuse cannot waive their right to recover.
