# PA 0104 01 25 — Amendatory Endorsement — California

## Source Document Summary

California-specific amendatory endorsement to PA 0001. Three pages. Edition 01 25 (January 2025).
Amends Definitions ("you", "your", "family member"), Part A (Liability), and Policy Conditions (Part B Recoveries item a. deletion plus a Termination provision).
Distinctive theme: explicit recognition of **registered domestic partnership under California law** in core definitions; intra-family liability exclusion that turns on indemnification accruing to the named insured/family member; California-specific cancellation grounds and notice periods.

---

## Extracted Content

### Definitions

1. **"You" / "your" replaced** — means (a) the named insured; and (b) the spouse OR a party who has entered into a domestic partnership recognized by California law with the named insured, if a resident of the same household. Coverage extends to a former spouse or domestic partner who ceases to be a resident during the policy period (or before inception) until the earliest of: 90 days after change in residency; the effective date of a new policy showing that person as a named insured; or the end of the policy period.
2. **"Family member" replaced** — a resident of the household who is (a) related to the insured by blood, marriage, **registered domestic partnership under California law**, or adoption; or (b) the insured's ward or foster child.

### Part A — Liability Coverage (2 amendments)

1. **New exclusion added** — no Liability Coverage for any insured for BI to "you" or a "family member" **when the benefits of such indemnification accrue either directly or indirectly to "you" or a "family member"**. (California intra-family exclusion is conditional on accrual, not categorical.)
2. **Insurance Under More Than One Policy replaced** — pro-rata sharing rule for primary coverage; insurance "we" provide for a non-owned vehicle (including a temporary substitute) is excess over other collectible insurance, except as in c. below; pro-rata defense-cost sharing when both primary and excess policies apply; non-owned vehicle insurance is **primary** when that vehicle is insured under an auto-business policy and the operating insured is not the auto-business person/employee/agent; insurance for an owned vehicle is excess over (1) an auto-business person's insurance when that person is operating, and (2) an owner/tenant/lessee of premises on which a loading/unloading loss arises.

### Policy Conditions (2 amendments)

1. **Recoveries (Part B), item a. deleted** — removes one sub-item of the base form's Part B Recoveries provision (lien-related sub-item).
2. **Termination provision added**, comprising:
   - **Automatic Termination** — coverage terminates (1) at end of period when company offered to renew/continue and named insured did not accept (failure to pay required premium = non-acceptance); or (2) on the effective date of any other insurance obtained on a covered auto, but only with respect to similar insurance.
   - **Cancellation** — named insured may cancel by returning the policy or written notice with future stop date. Company may cancel by mailed/delivered written notice; proof of mailing is sufficient; policy period ends on the effective date of cancellation. Company may cancel only if: nonpayment; fraud in claim presentation/pursuit; substantial increase in hazard; material misrepresentation about safety record / annual miles / driving experience / prior claims / Commissioner-recognized substantial-relationship factors (with 20-day cure window after notice receipt); driver's license of named insured / household / regular driver suspended or revoked during the policy period or 180 days before the most recent renewal/effective date if not previously disclosed (cancellation does not become effective if the suspension is removed before the cancellation effective date). Nonpayment notice: provided after the due date, with at least 10-day cure period and stated cancellation date and reason. Other-cause cancellation requires 20-day notice. Pro-rata return premium; payment/tender of unearned premium is not a condition of cancellation.
   - **Nonrenewal** — at least 30-day mailed/delivered written notice; proof of mailing is sufficient. Period ends on nonrenewal effective date. Company may nonrenew (a) beginning 6 months after original effective date if the policy period is < 6 months; (b) at end of policy period if 6 months ≤ period < 1 year; or (c) at each anniversary of the original effective date if period ≥ 1 year.
