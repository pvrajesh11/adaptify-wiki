### Rule 6.9 — Claim Free Discount `[multistate override]`

The multistate rule's first paragraph is replaced. The AK version adds a catastrophic claim exclusion:

> A policy is claim free if, during the three year period before application/renewal, all adults insured under the policy had Homeowners or equivalent coverage in force and no claims **(excluding catastrophic claims as described below)**, of any dollar amount and for any cause of loss, were paid under such coverage.

**Catastrophic claim exclusion:** A claim resulting from an event that caused **$25 million or more** in total insured property losses and affected a significant number of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers shall **not** be considered when determining if a policy is claim free.

### Rule 6.11 — Risk Group Assignment `[multistate override]`

The entire multistate rule is replaced in Alaska. Key differences:

1. **Catastrophic event exclusion added:** Claims from events causing $25 million or more in total insured property losses are excluded from claims payment history (same threshold as Rule 6.9 override).
2. **Risk group matrix is published in the Alaska state pages** (7×7 matrix, Major Claims columns 0–6+, Minor Claims rows 0–6+, groups G through X). Matrix is identical in structure to multistate.

The replacement text explicitly includes the matrix:

| Minor\Major | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |

## ADDITIONAL RULES (AK-only)

### Rule 4.19 — Owner Occupancy Requirement `[AK-specific]`

Forms: HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006, HO 0008.

At company option, a policy issued to an owner-occupant (or co-owner-occupant, life estate occupant, or long-term contract occupant) may be endorsed to **exclude coverage for loss to property occurring 61 days or more after the named insured begins living at a location other than the described location** on other than a temporary basis.

**Not permitted for:**
- Dwellings under construction
- Seasonal dwellings
- Additional or secondary locations
- Condo/co-op units regularly rented or held for rental

**Premium:** No charge or credit.
**Endorsement:** HO 2003

### Rule 8.10 — Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV For All Covered Property Losses `[AK-specific]`

Forms: HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006.

Loss settlement for roof surfacing on buildings at the described location may be converted from replacement cost to **actual cash value for loss caused by any covered peril** (not limited to windstorm/hail as in the multistate Rule 8.9).

**Not permitted with:**
- ACV endorsement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.1)
- Functional replacement cost endorsement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.2)

**Premium:** Apply factor from AK state pages per peril.
**Endorsement:** HO 4836

Note: Multistate Rule 8.9 (HO 4835) applies only to windstorm/hail losses; AK Rule 8.10 (HO 4836) applies to all covered perils. These are distinct endorsements.

### Rule 8.11 — Cosmetic Damage Exclusion `[AK-specific]`

A policy may be endorsed to **exclude coverage for cosmetic damage to surfacing of covered buildings** caused by windstorm or hail when such damage consists solely of cosmetic damage.

**Applicable surfacing types:**
- Exterior wall surfacing
- Roof surfacing
- Exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing (must be applied together; cannot be applied separately)

**Premium:** Apply factor from AK state pages per peril. Door and window surfacing treated as one type for premium purposes.
**Endorsement:** HO 2002 (with entry indicating specific surfacing to which exclusion applies)
