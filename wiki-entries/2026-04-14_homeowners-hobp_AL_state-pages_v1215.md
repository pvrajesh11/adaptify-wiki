## §6.9 — Claim Free Discount `[multistate override]`

The multistate rule is replaced by an Alabama-specific version. Key difference: Alabama adds an exemption for **catastrophic claims**.

A policy is claim free if, during the three-year period before the application/renewal preparation date:
- All adults insured under the policy had Homeowners or equivalent coverage in force; AND
- No claims (excluding catastrophic claims as defined below), of any dollar amount and for any cause of loss, were paid under such coverage.

**Catastrophic claim exemption:** A claim resulting from an event that caused $25 million or more in total insured property losses AND affected a significant number of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers shall NOT be considered when determining if a policy is claim free.

Multistate rule has no catastrophic claim exemption.

## §6.11 — Risk Group Assignment `[multistate override]`

The multistate rule is replaced in its entirety. Key difference: Alabama adds a **catastrophic event exclusion** from the claim count (same $25 million threshold as §6.9).

Alabama risk group assignment rules:

**Claims payment history sources:** application signed by applicant/insured; loss/claims history database; company internal records.

**Experience period:** 3 years before application date (new) or renewal preparation date.

**Claim eligibility for counting:**
- Risk eligible for this manual or equivalent property/liability program (including Mobile-Homeowners)
- Not from mine subsidence, earthquake, or sinkhole collapse
- Not paid solely for scheduled personal property or medical payments coverage
- **Not from a catastrophic event** (causing $25 million+ in total insured property losses, affecting a significant number of policyholders and insurers) — `[AL-specific addition vs. multistate]`
- Resulted in payments totaling $500 or more (net of subrogation recoveries)

**Claim types:**
- Major claims: fire, liability, theft, or vandalism losses
- Minor claims: all other claims

**Risk Group Matrix (7×7):**

| Minor \ Major | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | G | I | K | M | O | Q | R |
| **1** | H | J | L | N | P | R | S |
| **2** | I | K | M | O | Q | S | T |
| **3** | J | L | N | P | R | T | U |
| **4** | K | M | O | Q | S | U | V |
| **5** | L | N | P | R | T | V | W |
| **6+** | M | O | Q | S | U | W | X |

If post-factor determination shows actual history merits lesser premium, difference must be refunded.

## §4.19 — Owner Occupancy Requirement `[AL-specific addition]`

Added rule (not in multistate base). Available on Forms HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006, and HO 0008.

At company option, a policy issued to any of the following may be endorsed to exclude coverage for loss to property occurring 61 or more days after the named insured begins living at a location other than the described location on other than a temporary basis:
- Owner-occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling
- Owner-occupant of a condominium or co-operative unit
- Co-owner-occupant of a 2–4 family dwelling
- Occupant under a life estate arrangement
- Occupant purchasing a dwelling under a long-term contract

**Not permitted when policy covers:**
- Dwelling under construction
- Seasonal dwelling
- Additional or secondary location
- Condominium or co-operative unit regularly rented or held for rental to others

Premium: No charge or credit.
Endorsement: HO 2003.

## §8.10 — Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV For All Covered Property Losses `[AL-specific addition]`

Added rule (not in multistate base, which has only §8.9 for wind/hail ACV). Available on Forms HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, and HO 0006.

Converts loss settlement terms for roof surfacing from replacement cost to actual cash value for loss caused by **any covered peril** (broader than the multistate §8.9 which applies only to windstorm or hail).

**Not permitted when policy includes:**
- ACV loss settlement endorsement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.1)
- Functional replacement cost endorsement for Coverages A and B (Rule 8.1.2)

Premium: Factor from state pages (by peril).
Endorsement: HO 4836.

---

## §8.11 — Cosmetic Damage Exclusion `[AL-specific addition]`

Added rule (not in multistate base). All forms.

A policy may be endorsed to exclude coverage for cosmetic damage to the surfacing of covered buildings caused by windstorm or hail, when wind/hail damage consists solely of cosmetic damage.

Applicable surfacing types (may be applied selectively, except as noted):
- Exterior wall surfacing
- Roof surfacing
- Exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing (these two must be applied together; cannot be applied separately)

Premium: Factor from state pages (by peril). For premium determination, exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing are treated as one surfacing type.
Endorsements: HO 2002; indicate the specific surfacing type(s) to which the exclusion applies.
