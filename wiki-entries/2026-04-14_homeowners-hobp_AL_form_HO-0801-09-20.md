# HO 0801 09 20 — Amendatory Endorsement Alabama — Ingest Entry

Source: `Homeowners/AL/Forms/HO 0801 09 20  Amendatory Endorsement  Alabama.docx`

Form is one page. Edition: September 2020.

---

## Full Transcription

### Section 1 — Intentional Acts Exclusion: Abuse Exception

Under Exclusions That Apply To Property Coverages, Intentional Acts is amended to add:

> However, if the loss is caused by an act of abuse, as defined by Alabama statute, committed by or at the direction of an "insured", this exclusion will not apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another "insured", who is the subject of the abuse, and who did not cooperate with or contribute to the act that caused the loss. The innocent "insured" must either file a complaint under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act against the abuser, and not voluntarily dismiss the complaint, or seek a warrant for the abuser's arrest for the act causing the loss and cooperate in the prosecution of the abuser.
>
> Subject to all other "terms" of this policy, "our" payment to an "insured" who did not cooperate in or contribute to the act that caused the loss may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest in the property.

### Section 2 — Subrogation Condition: Abuse Carve-Out

Under Policy Conditions, Conditions That Apply To All Coverages, Subrogation is amended to add:

> An innocent "insured" who is the subject of abuse, as defined by Alabama statute, cannot waive his or her right to recover. "We" retain all rights set forth by this Subrogation condition with regard to "our" right to recover, up to the amount "we" pay, for loss caused by an act of abuse.

### Section 3 — Renewal Notice Condition (Added)

The following is added under Policy Conditions, Conditions That Apply To All Coverages:

> Renewal -- If "we" intend to renew this policy subject to an increase in premium, other than an increase due to a change made at "your" request, "we" will give "you" notice at least 30 days before the renewal is effective.

### Section 4 — Suit Against Us Condition (Replaced)

Under Policy Conditions, Conditions Applicable To Property Coverages Only, Suit Against Us is deleted and replaced by:

> Suit Against Us -- No suit may be brought against "us" unless all of the "terms" that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with and the suit is brought within the time period prescribed by law for the commencement of such action.

## Structural Analysis

| Section | What Changes | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intentional acts property exclusion | Adds abuse exception for innocent insured; requires Alabama Protection From Abuse Act complaint OR warrant + prosecution cooperation |
| 2 | Subrogation condition | Innocent insured (abuse subject) cannot waive recovery rights; company retains subrogation |
| 3 | Policy conditions | Adds 30-day renewal notice if premium increases (at-company-initiative increases only) |
| 4 | Suit Against Us (property) | Replaces fixed contractual time period with "prescribed by law" (deferring to AL statute of limitations) |
