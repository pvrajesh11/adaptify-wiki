# PPA 0154 01 20 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska

## Source Document Summary

Alaska-specific amendatory endorsement to PPA 0001. One page (Page 1 of 1). Edition 01 20 (January 2020).
Amends four provisions: (1) Part D intentional acts exclusion (domestic violence exception for innocent insureds),
(2) Subrogation condition (domestic violence carve-out), (3) adds AK renewal notice condition,
(4) replaces Suit Against Us condition with AK-specific version.

---

## Extracted Content

### Amendment 1 — Part D Intentional Acts Exclusion (Domestic Violence Exception)

Part D Physical Damage exclusion for intentional acts is amended to add:

If a loss is caused by an act of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute) committed by or at the direction of an insured, the exclusion does NOT apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who is the subject of the domestic violence and who did not cooperate with or contribute to the act.

**Conditions for innocent insured protection:**
- Must file for a protective order under Alaska Statute 18.66 against the abuser, OR
- Seek a warrant for the abuser's arrest for the act causing the loss AND cooperate in prosecution

**Payment limitation:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest.

### Amendment 2 — Subrogation Condition (Domestic Violence)

Subrogation condition is amended to add:

An innocent insured who is the subject of domestic violence (as defined by Alaska statute) cannot waive his or her right to recover. Company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for loss caused by an act of domestic violence.

### Amendment 3 — Renewal Notice Condition (Added)

New condition added: If the company intends to renew the policy subject to a premium increase (other than an increase due to a change made at the named insured's request), the company must give the named insured at least 30 days' notice before the renewal is effective.

### Amendment 4 — Suit Against Us Condition (Replaced)

Original Suit Against Us condition is deleted and replaced:

No suit may be brought against the company unless all terms that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with AND the suit is brought within the time period prescribed by Alaska law for the commencement of such action.

(Replaces the base form's fixed time period with reference to Alaska statute of limitations.)
