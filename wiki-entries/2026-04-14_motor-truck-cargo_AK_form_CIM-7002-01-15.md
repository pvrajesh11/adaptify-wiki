# CIM 7002 01 15 — Motor Truck Cargo Coverage

## Source Document Summary

Base Motor Truck Cargo Coverage form. 11 pages, edition 01 15 (January 2015). Owner's Cargo Coverage.
Provides open-perils cargo coverage for property in transit on owned vehicles and at terminal locations.
Amended in Alaska by CIM 2003 06 20.

### Property Covered

**1. Property In A Terminal**
- Covers direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to described property while at a terminal location
- Coverage limitations: (a) only at a described terminal or within 100 feet of it; (b) only while in due course of transit; (c) property of others covered only to extent of insured's legal liability

**2. Property In Transit**
- Covers direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to described property while in due course of transit including loading and unloading
- Coverage limitations: (a) only on or in an owned vehicle; (b) property of others covered only to extent of insured's legal liability; (c) loading/unloading covered only if adjacent to an owned vehicle (sidewalk, street, loading dock, or similar area)

### Property Not Covered

1. Art, Antiques And Fur — objects of art, antiques, fur garments
2. Carrier For Hire — property of others for which insured is responsible as a carrier for hire, or as arranger of transportation (carloader, consolidator, broker, freight forwarder, shipping association)
3. Contraband — contraband or property in course of illegal transportation or trade
4. Exports And Imports — exported/imported property: (a) covered under any ocean marine cargo policy; or (b) while on ocean or air conveyance
5. Jewelry, Stones And Metals — jewelry, precious or semi-precious stones, gold, silver, platinum, or other precious metals or alloys
6. Lease Agreement — property for which insured is contractually liable under a lease agreement with any transportation carrier
7. Live Animals — animals including cattle or poultry (exception: death caused or made necessary by a specified peril)
8. Mail — mail shipments in custody of U.S. Postal Service
9. Money And Securities — accounts, bills, currency, food stamps, evidences of debt, lottery tickets not held for sale, money, notes, securities
10. Samples — samples while in custody of a sales representative
11. Storage — property held in storage

### Coverage Extensions

Coverage extensions are part of (not in addition to) the applicable property limit, unless otherwise indicated. Extension limits may be overridden on the schedule of coverages. Extensions are not subject to coinsurance.

**1. Debris Removal**
- Pays cost to remove debris of covered property caused by a covered peril
- Does not include: extracting pollutants from land/water; removing/restoring/replacing polluted land/water
- Limit: 25% of loss payment; combined loss + debris removal may not exceed property limit
- Additional limit: up to $5,000 extra when debris removal exceeds 25% or combined exceeds property limit
- Reporting requirement: expenses must be reported in writing within 180 days of direct physical loss

**2. Emergency Removal**
- Pays for direct physical loss to covered property while being moved or stored to prevent a covered peril loss
- Time limitation: up to 365 days after property is first moved; does not extend past policy expiration

### Supplemental Coverages

Supplemental coverage limits are separate from (not part of) property limits. A supplemental coverage limit is the only limit available for the described coverage — it is not added to the property limit. Not subject to coinsurance.

**1. Pollutant Cleanup And Removal**
- Pays expense to extract pollutants from land or water if discharge/dispersal/seepage/migration/release/escape is caused by a covered peril during the policy period
- Does not pay: testing, evaluating, observing, or recording existence/level/effects of pollutants (exception: testing necessary for extraction)
- Reporting requirement: expenses must be reported in writing within 180 days of covered peril occurrence
- Limit: $10,000 per location per 12-month period

**2. Property You Have Sold**
- Pays for direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to covered property insured has sold and is shipping at owner's risk
- Coverage limitation: only when shipment rejected because property is damaged AND owner has refused to pay

**3. Rejected Shipments**
- Pays for direct physical loss caused by a covered peril to outgoing shipments rejected by consignee (including non-deliverable shipments)
- Coverage limitation: only while property is in due course of transit back to insured, or awaiting return shipment to insured
- Time limitation: ends 10 days after delivery attempted or made to consignee, unless property is in due course of transit back

### Perils Covered

Open perils — risks of direct physical loss unless loss is limited or caused by an excluded peril.

### Perils Excluded

**Absolute exclusions (anti-concurrent causation language applies):**
- Civil Authority — seizure, confiscation, destruction, or quarantine by order of civil authority (exception: destruction by civil authority to prevent spread of fire, unless fire is caused by an excluded peril)
- Nuclear Hazard — nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination (direct loss by fire resulting from nuclear hazard is covered)
- War And Military Action — war (including undeclared/civil war), warlike action by military force, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or unlawful seizure of power

**Other exclusions:**
- Contamination Or Deterioration — corrosion, decay, fungus, mildew, mold, rot, rust, or inherent quality causing self-damage (exception: if results in a specified peril, that specified peril loss is covered)
- Criminal, Fraudulent, Dishonest, Or Illegal Acts — loss caused by/resulting from criminal, fraudulent, dishonest, or illegal acts committed alone or in collusion with another by: the insured; others with interest in property; others to whom insured entrusts property; partners/officers/directors/trustees/joint venturers/members/managers; or employees/agents of any of the above (exception: acts of destruction by employees are covered; theft by employees is not)
- Loss Of Use — loss of use, delay, or loss of market
- Missing Property — unexplained or mysterious disappearance, shortage discovered on inventory, or no physical evidence of what happened
- Pollutants — release, discharge, seepage, migration, dispersal, or escape of pollutants (exception: if caused by a specified peril; or under Supplemental Coverage — Pollutant Cleanup and Removal)
- Spoilage — loss to perishable stock caused by spoilage
- Temperature/Humidity — humidity, dampness, dryness, or changes in or extremes of temperature (exception: if results in a specified peril, that specified peril loss is covered)
- Voluntary Parting — voluntary parting with title or possession because of fraudulent scheme, trick, or false pretense
- Wear And Tear — wear and tear, marring, or scratching (exception: if results in a specified peril, that specified peril loss is covered)

### What Must Be Done In Case Of Loss (Duties)

1. **Notice** — prompt notice including description of property involved; written notice on request; police notice when act is a crime
2. **Protect Property** — take all reasonable steps to protect covered property; insurer pays reasonable costs for necessary repairs/emergency measures to prevent further damage from a covered peril (does not increase limit); does not pay for repairs to property not already damaged by a covered peril
3. **Proof Of Loss** — signed, sworn proof of loss within 60 days after insurer's request; must include: time/place/circumstances; other policies; insured's interest and interests of others including mortgages/liens; changes in title during policy period; estimates, specifications, inventories, and other information required to settle
4. **Examination** — submit to examination under oath as often as reasonably requested; insurer may examine multiple persons separately
5. **Records** — produce records including tax returns and bank microfilms of canceled checks as often as reasonably requested
6. **Damaged Property** — exhibit damaged and undamaged property as often as reasonably requested; allow inspection or samples
7. **Volunteer Payments** — do not voluntarily make payments, assume obligations, pay/offer rewards, or incur expenses except to protect property from further damage
8. **Abandonment** — may not abandon property to insurer without written consent
9. **Cooperation** — cooperate in performing all acts required by the policy

### Valuation

1. **Actual Cash Value** — value at time of loss with deduction for depreciation
2. **Pair Or Set** — lost/damaged article of a pair or set valued at reasonable proportion of total pair/set value; not a total loss of pair or set
3. **Loss To Parts** — lost/damaged part of multi-part item valued at only the lost/damaged part or cost to repair/replace it

### How Much We Pay

1. **Insurable Interest** — insurer pays no more than insured's insurable interest
2. **Deductible** — insurer pays only loss exceeding the deductible shown on the schedule of coverages per occurrence
3. **Loss Settlement Terms** — lesser of: (a) ACV; (b) cost to repair/replace/rebuild with like kind and quality; or (c) the applicable schedule limit for owned vehicle or terminal location
   - **Catastrophe Limit** — insurer pays no more than the catastrophe limit regardless of how many owned vehicles or terminal locations are involved
   - **Vehicle At Terminal** — when owned vehicle is within terminal building or within 100 feet of terminal building, the terminal location limit applies; vehicle and terminal limits are not combined
4. **Multiple Coverages** — no more than the actual claim when more than one coverage insures the same loss
5. **Multiple Policies** — (a) proportional share when other policy covers on same terms; (b) excess only when other policy covers on different terms (no more than applicable limit)

### Loss Payment

1. **Insurer's Options** — pay value of lost/damaged property; pay cost of repair/replacement; rebuild/repair/replace with equivalent property within reasonable time; or take all or any part of property at agreed or appraised value
   - Notice of intent to rebuild, repair, or replace must be given within 30 days after receipt of duly executed proof of loss
2. **Your Losses** — all losses adjusted with insured; payment to insured unless another loss payee is named
   - Loss payable 30 days after: (a) satisfactory proof of loss received; AND (b) loss amount established by written agreement or appraisal award filing
3. **Property Of Others** — may be adjusted and paid to insured on behalf of owner, or directly to owner; if insurer pays owner, insurer has no obligation to also pay insured; insurer may defend suits by owners at its expense

### Other Conditions

1. **Appraisal** — either party may demand appraisal if they disagree on loss amount or property value; each selects a competent independent appraiser (with 20 days' notice); appraisers select a competent impartial umpire (within 15 days; if unable to agree, judge of court of record at property location selects); appraisers determine loss amounts separately; agreement of any two is binding; each party pays its appraiser; umpire and other appraisal costs split equally
2. **Benefit To Others** — insurance shall not directly or indirectly benefit anyone having custody of insured's property
3. **Conformity With Statute** — any condition in conflict with applicable law is amended to conform
4. **Estates** — on insured individual's death: person with custody of property until legal representative is qualified/appointed, or the legal representative, is covered as insured for covered property only; coverage does not extend past policy period
5. **Misrepresentation, Concealment, Or Fraud** — coverage void as to all insureds if any insured willfully conceals/misrepresents a material fact or if there is fraud or false swearing before or after a loss
6. **Policy Period** — insurer pays for covered loss occurring during the policy period
7. **Recoveries** — both parties must notify promptly of any recovery or payment by responsible parties; recovery expenses reimbursed first; insured may keep recovered property but must refund claim amount paid (or lesser agreed amount); if claim was reduced by deductible or other limiting terms, recovery is prorated between parties based on respective interest in the loss
8. **Restoration Of Limits** — a loss payment does not reduce applicable limits
9. **Subrogation** — insurer may require assignment of insured's right of recovery against others; insured must secure insurer's rights; coverage voided if insured impairs the right to recover; insured may waive right to recover from others in writing before a loss occurs
10. **Suit Against Us** — no legal action unless: (a) all terms of coverage have been complied with; AND (b) suit brought within two years after insured first has knowledge of the loss; if applicable law makes this limitation invalid, suit must begin within the shortest period permitted by law
    ⚠️ This condition is replaced in Alaska by CIM 2003 06 20 — see [CIM-2003-06-20.md](CIM-2003-06-20.md)
11. **Territorial Limits** — covers property while in the United States of America, its territories and possessions, Canada, and Puerto Rico
