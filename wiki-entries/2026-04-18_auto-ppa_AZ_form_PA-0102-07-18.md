# PA 0102 07 18 — Amendatory Endorsement — Arizona

## Source Document Summary

Arizona-specific amendatory endorsement to PA 0001. Seven pages. Edition 07 18 (July 2018; footer trailer also bears legacy code "PA 0102 07 16").
Amends Definitions, Part A (Liability), Part B (Medical Payments), Part D (Physical Damage), and Policy Conditions.
Substantive themes: replacement-auto rules; transportation network program (TNC) and food/goods delivery exclusions with carve-outs for shared-expense carpools and tax-exempt-organization volunteer activity (per Arizona Revised Statutes § 43-1201, A.4); intra-family liability exclusion limited to amounts above the Arizona Financial Responsibility Law minimum; expanded cancellation/nonrenewal/termination conditions including 7-day grace period.

## Extracted Content

### Definitions — "Replacement Auto" (item b. replaced)

Replacement-auto coverage rules vary by coverage type:

- **Collision Coverage** — if at least one auto on declarations has Collision and named insured asks for it on the replacement within 14 days, coverage begins on date of ownership at the broadest declarations level. If no Collision on any declarations auto, named insured has 4 days to request; if a loss occurs before request and within the 4-day window, a $500 deductible applies.
- **Comprehensive Coverage** — same 14-day / 4-day / $500-deductible structure as Collision.
- **Other Coverages** — replacement auto receives the broadest declarations coverage, beginning on date of ownership.

### Part A — Liability Coverage (5 amendments)

1. **Exclusion 1.f. (public/livery, TNC) replaced** — excludes liability while vehicle is used as public or livery conveyance or while logged into a transportation network program as a driver. Exception: shared-expense carpool; volunteer activity for a tax-exempt organization as described in Arizona Revised Statutes § 43-1201, A.4.
2. **Exclusion 1.g. (delivery in commerce) replaced** — excludes liability while vehicle is used in commerce for delivery/transport of food or other goods, including TNC delivery driver use. Exception: same volunteer activity carve-out (no shared-expense exception here).
3. **Exclusion 2.d. amended** — applies only to BI liability above the Arizona Financial Responsibility Law minimum.
4. **New exclusion added** — no Liability Coverage for BI to "you" or a "family member" (intra-family). Applies only above the Arizona Financial Responsibility Law minimum.
5. **Insurance Under More Than One Policy replaced** — pro-rata sharing rule; insurance for a vehicle "you" own is excess over an auto-business person's insurance when that person is operating; insurance for a non-owned vehicle is excess except where the non-owned vehicle is insured under an auto-business policy and the operating insured is not the auto-business person/employee/agent (then primary).

### Part B — Medical Payments (2 amendments)

1. **Exclusion 2.e. (public/livery in covered auto, TNC) replaced** — same TNC carve-outs as Part A 1.f.: shared-expense carpool; volunteer activity per ARS § 43-1201, A.4.
2. **Exclusion 2.f. (delivery in commerce in covered auto) replaced** — volunteer-activity exception only.

### Part D — Physical Damage (exclusions 7. and 8. replaced)

- **Exclusion 7 (public/livery, TNC)** — applies to covered auto and non-owned auto; carve-outs: shared-expense carpool; ARS § 43-1201, A.4 volunteer activity.
- **Exclusion 8 (delivery in commerce)** — applies to covered auto and non-owned auto; carve-out: ARS § 43-1201, A.4 volunteer activity only.

### Policy Conditions (3 amendments)

1. **Assignment replaced** — no assignment without written consent. On named-insured death, terms apply to (a) surviving spouse if a resident of the same household, treated as named insured, and (b) legal representative of the deceased, only for legal responsibility related to maintenance/use of a covered auto.
2. **Recoveries (Part B) replaced** — when company payment exceeds $5,000 and the recipient recovers from another source, company is entitled to the proceeds and may have a lien against recovery; written notice of lien sent to insured, court, alleged liable persons/entities, and their insurers.
3. **Termination (added)** — 7-day grace period for premium payment. Comprises Automatic Termination, Cancellation, Nonrenewal, Other Termination Conditions, and Offer To Renew sub-provisions:
   - **Automatic Termination** — coverage terminates at end of period if named insured notifies in writing of intent not to renew/continue.
   - **Cancellation** — notice by certified mail, USPS certificate of mailing, or first-class with Intelligent Mail barcode tracking. Within first 60 days: any reason, 10-day notice. After 60 days or on a renewal/continuation, only seven enumerated grounds: nonpayment (8-day post-due-date notice; effective on send date); fraudulent misrepresentation; suspended/revoked driver's license of named insured / household / regular driver; physical/mental disability without physician/RN certificate; certain criminal convictions during policy period or prior 36 months (criminal negligence resulting in death/homicide/assault, DUI, leaving the scene, false statements on license application, reckless driving) — c) and d) waivable by named driver exclusion; commercial use; director-of-insurance suspension based on financially hazardous condition or solvency/Arizona-law concerns; rehabilitation/receivership. 10-day notice for non-payment-based cancellations. Pro rata refund (90% if named insured cancels).
   - **Nonrenewal** — written notice 45 days before nonrenewal effective. Exception: nonpayment uses Offer To Renew procedure.
   - **Other Termination Conditions** — no cancellation/nonrenewal solely for residence location, age, race, color, religion, sex, national origin, or ancestry. May nonrenew for residence outside Arizona, or for failure to pay association dues if the policy was sold via association membership.
   - **Offer To Renew** — if named insured fails to pay renewal premium within 7 days after due date, company may terminate on or after the 8th day. Termination effective the earlier of (1) date notice mailed, or (2) effective date of any other insurance obtained for the covered auto; does not apply if the named insured has notified the company in writing of other insurance or non-renewal intent.
