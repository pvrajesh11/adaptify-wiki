# PPA 0101 03 18 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama

## Source Document Summary

Alabama-specific amendatory endorsement to PPA 0001. One page (Page 1 of 1). Edition 03 18 (March 2018).
Amends four provisions: (1) Part D intentional acts exclusion (abuse exception for innocent insureds under the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act), (2) Subrogation condition (abuse carve-out), (3) adds AL renewal notice condition, (4) replaces Suit Against Us condition with AL-specific version referencing state law.

---

## Extracted Content

### Amendment 1 — Part D Intentional Acts Exclusion (Abuse Exception)

Part D Physical Damage exclusion for intentional acts is amended to add:

If a loss is caused by an act of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute) committed by or at the direction of an insured, the exclusion does NOT apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who is the subject of the abuse and who did not cooperate with or contribute to the act.

**Conditions for innocent insured protection:**
- Must file a complaint under the **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** against the abuser and not voluntarily dismiss the complaint, OR
- Seek a warrant for the abuser's arrest for the act causing the loss AND cooperate in prosecution

**Payment limitation:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest.

### Amendment 2 — Subrogation Condition (Abuse Carve-Out)

Subrogation condition is amended to add:

An innocent insured who is the subject of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute) cannot waive his or her right to recover. Company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for loss caused by an act of abuse.

### Amendment 3 — Renewal Notice Condition (Added)

New condition added: If the company intends to renew the policy subject to a premium increase (other than an increase due to a change made at the named insured's request), the company must give the named insured at least 30 days' notice before the renewal is effective.

### Amendment 4 — Suit Against Us Condition (Replaced)

Original Suit Against Us condition is deleted and replaced:

No suit may be brought against the company unless all terms that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with AND the suit is brought within the time period **prescribed by law** for the commencement of such action.

(Replaces the base form's fixed time period with reference to applicable statute of limitations under Alabama law.)

## Comparison to AK Endorsement (PPA 0154 01 20)

| Provision | AL (PPA 0101 03 18) | AK (PPA 0154 01 20) |
|---|---|---|
| Innocent insured trigger | "act of abuse" (Alabama statute) | "act of domestic violence" (Alaska statute) |
| Innocent insured condition | File complaint under Alabama Protection From Abuse Act (and not voluntarily dismiss) OR seek warrant + cooperate | File protective order under AK Statute 18.66 OR seek warrant + cooperate |
| Subrogation | Cannot waive; company retains rights for "act of abuse" | Cannot waive; company retains rights for "act of domestic violence" |
| Renewal notice | 30 days prior to renewal effective date | 30 days prior to renewal effective date |
| Suit Against Us | "time period prescribed by law" | "time period prescribed by Alaska law" |

**Key distinction:** AL uses the broader term "abuse" and references the Alabama Protection From Abuse Act; AK uses "domestic violence" and references AK Statute 18.66.
