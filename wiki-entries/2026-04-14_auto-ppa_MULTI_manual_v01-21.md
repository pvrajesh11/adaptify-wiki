# Auto PPA — Multistate — Underwriting & Rating Manual 01 21

## Source Document Summary

Multistate Personal Auto underwriting manual defining rules that apply across all states by default.
State pages and state-specific manuals override these rules where noted. Edition 01 21 (January 2021).

---

## Extracted Content

### Eligibility (Rule 2)

**Eligible insureds:**
- Individuals, or individuals and spouses resident in same household, who own an eligible vehicle
- Two or more individuals jointly owning an eligible vehicle who are resident family members, resident individuals, or non-resident family members (non-resident spouses require Joint Ownership endorsement PA 1200 per Rule 4.9)
- Named individuals without vehicle ownership (non-owner coverages, Rule 4.11)
- Additional insureds for their liability (Rule 4.8)
- Trustees (Rule 4.10)

**Eligible vehicles:**
- Private passenger type autos, pickups, or vans used primarily for personal, non-commercial purposes
- Trailers used with private passenger autos, pickups, or vans
- Miscellaneous type vehicles (Rule 11)

**Ineligible risks:**
- Vehicles owned by corporations, partnerships, or business entities (except farm family co-partnerships or farm family corporations)
- Vehicles designed primarily for off-road use (except eligible miscellaneous type vehicles per Rule 11)
- Vehicles used principally for commercial or other business purposes (except farming/ranching or specifically allowed commercial purposes)

### Policy Form (Rule 3.1)

PA 0001 may provide:
- Liability only
- Liability + Medical Payments
- Liability + Uninsured Motorist
- Liability + Medical Payments + Uninsured Motorist
- Physical Damage (with or without liability and/or other coverages)
- Underinsured Motorist via endorsement PA 2200

Separate premium charged for each vehicle and coverage per Rule 5.

---

### Coverage Rules

**Part A — Liability (Rules 3.2)**
- Bodily Injury Liability: per person / per accident basis
- Property Damage Liability: per accident basis
- Combined Single Limit option: PA 0600

**Part B — Medical Payments (Rule 3.3)**
- Available when PA 0001 covers liability of described vehicles
- Per person basis

**Part C — Uninsured Motorist (Rule 3.4)**
- Available when PA 0001 covers liability of described vehicles
- Per person / per accident basis
- Combined Single Limit option: PA 0700

**Part D — Physical Damage (Rule 3.5)**
- Comprehensive: direct and accidental loss other than collision
- Collision: direct and accidental loss from collision
- Automatically includes child safety seat expenses and transportation expenses
- Settled at actual cash value (lesser of ACV, repair/replacement cost, or limit)

**Underinsured Motorist (Rule 3.7)**
- Available when PA 0001 covers liability of described vehicles
- Per person / per accident basis
- Combined Single Limit option: PA 0800
- Applied via endorsement: PA 2200

No multistate mandatory endorsements specified. Mandatory endorsements identified in state pages only.

### Policywriting Rules (Rule 4)

- **Policy terms:** Annual, semi-annual (where permitted), monthly (where permitted) — Rule 4.5
- **Coverage territory:** US, territories, possessions, Puerto Rico, Canada, and while vehicle transported between these — Rule 4.6
- **Additional insureds:** PA 1100; no premium charge — Rule 4.8
- **Joint ownership:** PA 1200; no premium charge — Rule 4.9
- **Accident forgiveness:** PA 9603; eliminates first at-fault accident from rating — Rule 4.7.4

### Driving Record Points (Rule 6.4.1)

Experience period: 3 years preceding inception or renewal date.

Points assigned for convictions for motor vehicle violations and at-fault accidents where claim was paid and exceeds approved dollar threshold.

When multiple violations from one occurrence: use only highest point violation.
2nd+ violation in experience period: assessed both age-of-violation points AND additional violation surcharge.

**Multistate point structure:**

| Classification | 0–<6 Mo | 0–<1 Yr | 1–<2 Yr | 2–<3 Yr | Add'l Violation Surcharge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speeding Violation (non-Major) | N/A | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Minor Violation (non-Speeding) | N/A | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Major Violation | N/A | 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| At-Fault Accident | N/A | 3 | 3 | 2 | 0 |
| No Prior Insurance | 4 | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A |

Additional points:
- 4 points — insurance lapse 30+ days (unless no vehicle owned or active military)
- 1 point — conviction not classified requiring license suspension/revocation OR financial responsibility filing

**Points NOT assigned for:**
- Violations/accidents for driver who is named insured of separate policy or principal driver of vehicle on separate policy
- Non-moving violations
- At-fault accidents eliminated by Accident Forgiveness (Rule 4.7.4)
- Lawfully parked/stationary vehicle accidents (rolling vehicle: points to driver who parked it)
- Accidents reimbursed by or judgment against responsible party
- Accidents covered by UM or UIM
- Accidents covered by Comprehensive
- Struck-in-rear without driver conviction + other driver convicted
- Emergency response accidents (police, fire, first-aid, law enforcement)
- Additional state page exceptions may apply

**Financial Responsibility Filing (Rule 6.4.2):**
Applied to any driver for whom financial responsibility must be filed under state law.

**Unverifiable Driving Record (Rule 6.4.3):**
Applied to driver with no violations/accidents reported and driving history cannot be verified through company records, state MVR, or commercial records.

### Driver Characteristic Rules (Rule 6)

**Driver discounts:**
- Driver Training Discount (6.3.1) — drivers under 21 with qualifying driver education (30+ classroom hours, 6+ in-vehicle or 12+ simulator hours)
- Defensive Driving Discount (6.3.2) — any driver completing approved course within 3 years, no commercial license
- Good Student Discount (6.3.3) — drivers 16+ who are full-time students in upper 20% / B average / 3.0 GPA / Dean's List
- Distant Driver Factor — Student Away (6.3.4.1): 100+ miles from garage; Driver Deployed (6.3.4.2): active military in different location
- Safe Driver Discount (6.3.5) — no driving record points in 3 years preceding inception/renewal

**Driver vehicle assignment (Rule 6.5):**
- Equal drivers/vehicles: each driver assigned to one vehicle
- More drivers than vehicles: highest classification driver assigned to highest rated vehicle, in order
- More vehicles than drivers: unassigned vehicles use average factor

**Non-owner coverages (Rule 6.6):**
- Named Non-Owner (6.6.1): Liability, Med Pay, UM, UIM — PA 1500
- Extended Non-Owned — named person (6.6.2): Liability, Med Pay only — PA 1600
- Extended Non-Owned — regularly furnished vehicle (6.6.3): Liability, Med Pay only — PA 1700
- Extended Non-Owned — public/livery conveyance (6.6.4): Liability, Med Pay only — PA 1800
- Extended Non-Owned — auto business (6.6.5): Liability, Med Pay only — PA 1900

### Vehicle Rules (Rules 7)

**Primary use classifications (Rule 7.3.1):**
- Business/Artisan: occupational/professional/business duties (not commuting or TNP)
- Farm: principally garaged on farm/ranch, used primarily in farm/ranch operations
- Personal Use: personal transportation including commuting

**Transportation Network (Rule 7.3.2):**
- Phase I (logged in, no accepted request): PA 6602
- Phase II (accepted request, no passenger): PA 6603
- Phase III (passenger in vehicle): PA 6604
- Delivery/Transport (fulfillment delivery): PA 6605

**Vehicle discounts (Rule 7.4):**
- Airbag Discount (7.4.1)
- Anti-lock Brakes Discount (7.4.2)
- Anti-Theft Device Discount (7.4.3)
- Advanced Driver Assistance Systems Discount (7.4.4)

**Vehicle surcharges (Rule 7.5):**
- Branded Title (7.5.1)
- Vehicle Performance Modifications (7.5.2) — refer to company

**Multi-Vehicle Policy Factor (Rule 7.6):** Applies when 2+ private passenger autos/pickups/vans on policy.
