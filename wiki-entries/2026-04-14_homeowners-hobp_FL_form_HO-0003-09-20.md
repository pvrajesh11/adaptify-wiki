# Homeowners HOBP — Florida — Form HO 0003 09 20 — Special Form

Source: HO 0003 09 20 Special Form.docx (46-page policy form)

---

## Form Overview

HO 0003 09 20 is the Special Form homeowners policy (HO-3), edition September 2020. It provides:
- Coverages A and B: open perils (all risks of direct physical loss, subject to exclusions)
- Coverage C: named perils
- Coverages D, L, M: additional living costs/loss of rent, personal liability, medical payments to others

Filed in Florida. Amended by mandatory endorsement HO 0809 02 22 (Amendatory Endorsement — Florida).

## Principal Property Coverages

### Coverage A — Residence
Covers the residence on the described location including attached additions, built-in components, fixtures, and building materials/supplies on or adjacent to the described location for use in construction/alteration/repair. Does not cover land, underground/surface water, trees/plants/shrubs/lawns (except as provided in incidental coverages), or grave markers/mausoleums.

### Coverage B — Related Private Structures
Covers unattached related private structures on the described location. Also covers fences, driveways, sidewalks, and other permanently installed outdoor fixtures. Excludes structures: rented to non-tenants (except sole private garage use); used for business direction/operation; used for business property storage (except personal vehicles and non-motorized property owned by insured, not exceeding 5-gallon portable fuel containers).

### Coverage C — Personal Property
Covers personal property owned or used by an insured. At insured's option: guest/domestic employee property covered while in any insured-occupied residential premises; other persons' property covered while in the described location portion occupied by an insured.

**Limitation at other residences:** 10% of Coverage C limit or $1,000 (whichever greater). Does not apply to property removed due to renovation making described location unfit, or property in newly acquired principal residence for 30 days.

**Special limits (per occurrence):**
- $250: money, bank notes, bullion, coins, medals, smart cards, stored-value cards
- $1,500: securities, stamps, letters of credit, personal records, tickets, accounts, deeds, passports, manuscripts (includes reproduction costs)
- $1,500: electronic devices in/on motorized vehicle/watercraft operable from vehicle power
- $1,500: business-use electronic devices away from described location (operable from vehicle power)
- $1,500: watercraft including furnishings, equipment, engines, motors, trailers, semitrailers
- $1,500: other trailers and semitrailers (not watercraft trailers)
- $2,500: jewelry, watches, precious/semiprecious stones, gems, furs (theft only)
- $2,500: silverware, goldware, platinumware, pewterware, plated items (theft only)
- $2,500: guns and related items (theft only)
- $2,500/$500: business property on/away from described location

**Not covered:** Separately described and specifically insured property; animals, birds, fish, insects; motorized vehicles (including parts and accessories, except handicapped-use vehicles and insured-owned off-road service vehicles not required to be registered); aircraft; hovercrafts; roomers/boarders/tenants' property (except relatives); rented property (except occasional residential rental, up to two roomers/boarders, and rental units covered under incidental coverage); credit card losses (see incidental coverage); grave markers; land; underground/surface water; trees/plants/shrubs/lawns.

### Coverage D — Additional Living Costs And Loss Of Rent
Pays necessary and reasonable increase in living costs for household to maintain normal standard of living when described location made unfit for use as residence by covered loss. Time: period reasonably required to make fit for use or permanently relocate. Also pays fair rental value of portions rented to others (deducting charges that don't continue). Civil authority: pays up to two weeks if neighboring premises directly damaged by covered peril and civil authority prohibits use of described location. Does not pay for lease cancellation costs.

## Perils Insured Against

### Coverages A and B — Open Perils

**Coverage A and B:** Insured for all risks of direct physical loss unless excluded.

**Exclusions applying to Coverage A and B only (in addition to general property exclusions):**

1. Freezing/discharge/overflow from plumbing/heating/AC/sprinkling/water heater/domestic appliance (unless heat maintained or water shut off)
2. Freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of ice or water to: fences, patios, paved areas, swimming pools; supporting structures (bulkheads, footings, foundations, walls); non-supporting bulkheads/retaining walls; docks/piers/wharves
3. Theft in or to residence under construction
4. Vandalism/malicious mischief if residence vacant more than 60 consecutive days before loss
5. Continuous or repeated water discharge/seepage/leakage or humidity/moisture/vapor over weeks/months/years (unless no insured knew or could reasonably expect)
6. Collapse or structural integrity impairment (except under Collapse incidental coverage)
7. Settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expanding of bulkheads, ceilings, floors, footings, foundations, patios, paved areas, roofs, or walls
8. Birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or insured-owned animals
9. Smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations
10. Pollutants (unless discharge caused by Coverage C peril)
11. Wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, rust/corrosion, smog, root pressure

**Exceptions to Coverage A/B exclusions:** Ensuing covered loss from items 2–11 is covered. Also: water/steam accidentally discharging from plumbing/systems on described location due to causes excluded under items 8–11 is covered (including cost to remove and replace building parts for access).

### Coverage C — Named Perils

Fire or Lightning; Windstorm or Hail (not interior building damage without exterior breach, not watercraft outside fully enclosed building); Explosion; Riot or Civil Commotion; Aircraft (including missiles and spacecraft); Vehicles; Sudden and Accidental Damage from Smoke (including fumes, smoke, soot from boiler/furnace); Volcanic Eruption (not earthquake/land shock waves); Vandalism or Malicious Mischief; Theft (including attempted theft and likely theft; with exclusions for: insured's own theft, theft during construction, stone from setting, credit card theft, theft from rented portion, off-premises theft of trailers/campers/watercraft/property at non-temporary insured-owned premises); Falling Objects (not interior without exterior breach; not falling object itself); Weight of Ice, Snow, or Sleet (damage inside building); Sudden and Accidental Tearing Apart, Cracking, Burning, or Bulging of steam/hot water/AC/sprinkling system or water heater (not from freezing); Accidental Discharge or Overflow of Water or Steam from plumbing/heating/AC/sprinkling/water heater/domestic appliance (not chronic seepage, not from off-premises, not to system itself); Freezing (when heat maintained or water shut off); Sudden and Accidental Damage from Artificially Generated Electrical Currents (not tubes/transistors/electronic components in electronic apparatus); Sinkhole Collapse (sudden settlement/collapse from subterranean voids from water on limestone/rock; not cost of filling sinkholes).

## General Property Exclusions (Concurrent Causation)

Apply regardless of other contributing causes, in any sequence:

1. **Ordinance or Law** — enforcement of codes; loss in value; pollutant-related code requirements (except Increased Cost — Ordinance or Law incidental coverage)
2. **Civil Authority** — confiscation, destruction, or seizure by civil authority (except fire prevention orders)
3. **Nuclear Hazard** — reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination (direct fire from nuclear event covered)
4. **War and Military Action** — war, undeclared war, military force, insurrection, revolution
5. **Neglect** — insured's failure to protect covered property
6. **Earth Movement** — earthquake, volcanic land shock waves, landslide, mudflow, subsidence, erosion, any earth sinking/rising/shifting (sinkhole collapse under Coverage C still covered; fire/explosion resulting from earth movement covered)
7. **Water** — flood; surface water; waves/tidal wave/tsunami; tides; tidal water; overflow of any body of water; spray; storm surge; water backing up through sewers/drains or from sump/sump pump; water below surface of ground; matter carried by excluded water (fire/explosion from excluded water event covered; theft covered; accidental discharge from plumbing on described location not excluded)
8. **Power Failure** — failure of power/utility service from off described location (on-premises direct loss covered)
9. **Intentional Acts** — any act by or at direction of an insured with intent to cause loss (applies even to non-involved insureds)
10. **Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, or Dry Rot** — existence or activity not directly resulting from a covered peril (direct loss caused by covered peril resulting from bacteria/fungi is covered)

Also (Coverage A/B, ensuing loss rule applies):
- **Weather Conditions** — contributing to an excluded cause/event
- **Errors, Omissions, and Defects** — acts/decisions of any person or defects in development/planning/construction/maintenance (except Collapse incidental coverage)

## Liability Coverages

### Coverage L — Personal Liability
Pays all sums for which an insured is legally liable for BI or PD caused by an occurrence to which this coverage applies. Company defends at its expense, may settle, and stops defending when limit paid by judgment or written settlement.

### Coverage M — Medical Payments To Others
Pays necessary medical expenses incurred or medically determined within three years of accident causing BI. Does not apply to insureds or regular household residents (except domestic employees). Applies to: persons on insured premises with insured's permission; persons away from insured premises when BI arises from insured premises condition, insured activity, domestic employee course of employment, or insured's animal.

### Incidental Liability Coverages

1. **Business** — pays BI/PD from occasional residential rental of insured premises, residential roomers (max 2), school/studio/office/garage rental, or business activities of insureds under 21 in part-time self-employed business without employees
2. **Claims and Defense Cost** — taxed costs; bond premiums (up to limit); insured's expenses at company request ($250/day lost earnings); post-judgment interest; prejudgment interest
3. **Contracts** — BI/PD under written contract related to insured premises ownership/maintenance/use, or written assumption of others' liability
4. **Damage to Property of Others** — $1,000 per occurrence; replacement cost; regardless of legal liability; excludes: property covered under property coverages (excess coverage available); property owned by or rented to insured or insured's household residents; intentional acts by insureds 13+; business-related acts; arising out of aircraft/hovercraft/motorized vehicle/watercraft (except non-owned recreational off-road or non-owned service vehicles not required to be registered)
5. **First Aid Expense** — expenses for first aid to non-insureds for covered BI
6. **Loss Assessment** — $1,500 per occurrence; association levy for BI/PD to which Cov L/M applies, or director/officer/trustee acts for association (elected, uncompensated except expense reimbursement); not for govt-levied assessments
7. **Motorized Vehicles** — BI/PD from: vehicle in dead storage on insured premises; vehicle used only to service insured premises (if on insured premises) or designed for off-road use and servicing insured premises or others' premises; handicapped-assist vehicle; recreational off-road vehicle (not owned by insured or owned but on insured premises); battery-only low-speed vehicle (not bicycle/moped/golf cart); motorized golf cart (insured-owned, ≤4 persons, ≤25 mph, within golfing establishment or private residential community with legal road access). Excludes: registered for public roads; required to be registered at occurrence location; racing/demolition/stunt activities; rented to others; carrying people/cargo for fee; business use (except golf cart on golfing establishment)
8. **Watercraft** — BI/PD from: watercraft in storage; sailing vessels <26 feet or ≥26 feet and not owned/rented by insured; non-sailboats with inboard/inboard-outdrive ≤50 HP not owned or >50 HP not owned/rented; outboards ≤25 HP total; outboards >25 HP not owned; outboards >25 HP acquired during policy period; outboards >25 HP acquired before policy period if listed on declarations or request received within 45 days

### Liability Exclusions

**Applying to both Cov L and Cov M:** War and military action; aircraft/hovercraft BI/PD (except domestic employee in course of employment); motorized vehicle/watercraft BI/PD (except domestic employee and incidental vehicle/watercraft coverages); vicarious liability for child/minor's aircraft/hovercraft or motorized vehicle/watercraft use (except to extent covered by incidental coverages); professional services; business BI/PD (except golf cart on golfing establishment and incidental business coverage); non-insured premises owned/rented/rented-to-others; expected/directed/intended BI/PD or criminal or intentional malicious acts (except reasonable force to protect); communicable disease; sexual molestation; physical or mental abuse; corporal punishment; controlled substances (except legitimate prescription use).

**Additional exclusions for Cov L only:** BI to named insured/resident relatives/household minors (insured defined classes a-e); contribution/reimbursement claims for BI to insured; contractual liability (except Contracts incidental coverage); property damage to property owned by insured; cost to prevent injury/damage to owned property; property damage to property rented/occupied/used/in care of insured (except fire/smoke/explosion); domestic employee sickness/disease/death unless written notice within 36 months of policy period in which injury occurred; workers' compensation covered injuries; nuclear energy liability policy insureds; assessments except under Loss Assessment incidental coverage.

**Additional exclusions for Cov M only:** Injury to insured or regular residents (except domestic employee); workers' comp covered injuries; domestic employee injuries away from insured premises not in course of employment; nuclear reaction/radiation/radioactive contamination.

## Duties After Loss or Occurrence

### Property Coverages
Prompt notice to company (or agent); written notice if requested; police notice for theft; card company notice for credit card/EFT losses. Protect property from further damage; make necessary repairs; keep cost records. Cooperate with investigation. Inventory damaged personal property (quantity, description, ACV, loss amount; attach bills/receipts). Show damaged property; allow samples. Provide records/documents (including tax returns, bank records). Submit to examination under oath (separate examinations permitted). Provide signed, sworn proof of loss within 60 days of company's request (including time/cause, interests of all parties, identifying other policies and title/use changes, providing plans/estimates/inventory/living cost receipts/rental value records/credit card evidence). Assist with right of recovery.

### Liability Coverages
Written notice as soon as practical with: name and policy number; time, location, and details of occurrence; names and addresses of known claimants and witnesses. No volunteer payments. Cooperate with investigation/defense/settlement. Promptly provide copies of all notices, demands, and legal papers. Assist in settling claims, enforcing recovery rights, conducting suits, securing evidence, obtaining witnesses. For Damage to Property of Others: sworn statement of loss within 60 days; show damaged property.

## How Much We Pay

**Our Limit:** Lesser of applicable limit or amount under loss settlement terms; no insured paid more than their interest in the property.

**Deductible:** Applies to all principal and incidental property coverages except Credit Card/EFT/Forgery, Fire Department Service Charge, and Refrigerated Property. Per occurrence; separately at each covered location; only one deductible at each location.

**Replacement Cost Terms:** Apply to buildings under Cov A or B with permanent foundation and roof. Excludes window air-conditioners, awnings/canopies, appliances, carpets, antennas. 80% coinsurance: if limit is less than 80% of full replacement cost at time of loss, pay greater of ACV or proportionate cost (limit / 80% of replacement cost × repair cost). If limit ≥ 80%, pay actual cost to repair or replace. Payable after repair/replacement completed when cost exceeds lesser of $2,500 or 5% of limit (ACV paid in advance). Replacement cost excludes foundation/underground costs below basement floor or below ground inside foundation walls, and underground flues/pipes/wiring/drains. Insured may claim ACV and later claim additional RC amount if notified within 180 days of loss.

**Actual Cash Value Terms:** Apply to all property not subject to RC terms. Smaller of: cost to repair/replace with like kind and quality; or ACV just before loss.

**Loss to Pair or Set:** Replace/repair item only, or pay difference in ACV before and after loss.

**Loss to Parts:** Pay value of lost/damaged part or cost to repair/replace it.

**Coverage L:** Limit per occurrence regardless of number of insureds, claimants, suits, or policy periods. All BI/PD from one accident or repeated exposures = one occurrence.

**Coverage M:** Per person per accident. If per-accident limit shown on declarations, applies per accident. Payment is not an admission of liability.

**Severability:** Liability coverages apply separately to each insured; does not increase the per-occurrence limit.

**Other Insurance:** Property — pro rata with other insurance; excess over association/HOA insurance (except Association Deductible incidental coverage). Coverage L — excess over other valid and collectible insurance (except specific excess policies). Warranties/service plans — this policy is excess. Government funds — pro rata.

## Payment Of Loss

**Property:** Adjusted with insured. Paid within 60 days of acceptable proof of loss plus agreement, final judgment, or appraisal award. Payment to insured unless loss payee or other entitled party named. Company may pay in money or rebuild/repair/replace (written notice of intent within 30 days of acceptable proof of loss). Company may take damaged property at agreed/appraised value. Coverage D loss paid monthly if described location unfit for more than one month.

**Liability:** Person with judgment or written agreement (claimant/insured/company) is entitled to recover to extent of coverage.

**Damage to Property of Others:** At company option, adjusted with and paid to insured on behalf of owner or directly to owner.

## Policy Conditions

### All Coverages
Assignment; Cancellation and Nonrenewal (10-day notice for first 59 days or nonpayment; 30-day notice for other cancellations after 60 days in effect; 30-day nonrenewal notice; pro rata return premium); Change/Modification/Waiver (must be written; automatic broad coverage adoption within 60 days if no additional premium, unless part of overall program revision); Conformity with Statute; Death (coverage for legal representative; insured includes household members at time of death and temporary custodians); Inspections (right but not obligation; not a warranty); Misrepresentation/Concealment/Fraud (no coverage for any insured if any insured willfully concealed/misrepresented or fraudulently conducted; applies even to non-involved insureds); Subrogation (right to assign subrogation rights up to amount paid; no coverage if insured impairs recovery rights; insured may waive in writing before loss; does not apply to Cov M or Damage to Property of Others).

### Property Coverages Only
Abandonment (cannot abandon without company agreement); Appraisal (either party may demand; each selects appraiser within 20 days; appraisers select umpire within 15 days or court appoints; agreement of any two of three sets loss amount; each party pays own appraiser; shared umpire/other costs; appraisal cannot interpret terms, determine causation, or determine coverage); Loss Payable Clause; Mortgage Clause (loss paid to mortgagee and insured as interests appear; claim denial does not apply to mortgagee if mortgagee has notified of changes, paid premium on demand, and submitted proof of loss; 10-day notice of cancellation/nonrenewal to mortgagee; subrogation rights against mortgagee); No Benefit to Bailee; Policy Period; Recoveries (mutual notification; proper costs paid first; insured may keep recovered property but must return claim amount or lesser agreed amount; if claim was reduced by deductible/limits, recovery prorated by interest); **Suit Against Us — Two years** after loss (if state law makes this invalid, statutory period applies) — *amended by HO 0809 02 22 in Florida to use statutory period*.

### Liability Coverages Only
Bankruptcy of Insured (does not relieve company); Duties of Injured Person — Cov M (written proof of claim; authorize medical records; submit to medical exams); Policy Period; Suit Against Us — all liability terms complied with; liability fixed by final judgment or written agreement of claimant/insured/company; no joinder or impleader right.

## Florida Amendment

This form is amended by **HO 0809 02 22 — Amendatory Endorsement — Florida** which:
1. Adds innocent insured exception to Intentional Acts property exclusion (domestic abuse under Florida statute)
2. Adds subrogation carve-out for innocent insured in abuse situations
3. Adds renewal notice requirement (30 days for premium increases not at insured's request)
4. Replaces the two-year Suit Against Us property condition with the Florida statutory period
