# Homeowners HOBP — Multistate — Flood Coverage Supplement Rev 06 21

## Source Document Summary

Multistate supplement to the HOBP By-Peril Rating Plan manual governing optional flood coverage added
by endorsement HO 2710. Revision 06 21 (June 2021). Describes eligibility, flood definition, limit
structure, waiting period, deductible structure, and endorsement requirements. Rating information is
filed and approved separately and shown on the manual page (numerical factors not included in this entry).

---

## Extracted Content

### NFIP Disclaimer

This endorsement does not satisfy any mandatory flood insurance requirement by a federally regulated
lender for a home mortgage or loan. It does not meet the requirements of the National Flood Insurance
Program (NFIP). (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

---

### Eligible Policies

Optional flood coverage is available only on new business or renewal policies issued with form HO 0001,
HO 0002, HO 0003, or HO 0005. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

HO 0004 (tenant/contents) and HO 0006 (unit-owners) are not eligible for flood coverage under this supplement.

**Additional restriction**: flood coverage is unavailable when an additional or secondary location is added
to the policy by endorsement HO 6267. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

---

### Flood Definition

Flood is defined as a general and temporary condition of partial or complete inundation of normally dry
land area resulting from: (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

- an overflow of inland or tidal waters;
- an unusual and rapid accumulation or runoff of surface waters from any source, including but not limited
  to rainfall and ice melt; or
- mudflow.

**Excluded from flood definition**: tsunami.

---

### Waiting Period

There is no coverage for a flood occurrence that begins before, or within 360 hours (15 days) after, the
flood coverage inception date shown in the endorsement's schedule. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

A limited exception applies — refer to the endorsement's Waiting Period condition for the terms of that
exception. ⚠️ The specific terms of the waiting period exception are stated in endorsement HO 2710, not
reproduced in this supplement.

---

### Limit Structure

**Per flood occurrence limit**: applies to all loss, cost, and expense caused by any one flood occurrence.
Applies to all Principal Property Coverages. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

**Policy aggregate limit**: applies to all loss, cost, and expense caused by all flood occurrences in the
policy period. Applies to all Principal Property Coverages.

**Separate coverage occurrence limit and coverage aggregate limit** apply to each of the following
coverages, when applicable:
- Coverage C — Basement Personal Property
- Coverage D

**Incidental Coverage for Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, Or Dry Rot** (added by the flood endorsement):
a separate aggregate limit applies to the total of all loss, cost, or expense caused by all flood occurrences.

**Limit hierarchy**: any applicable coverage occurrence limit is part of, and not in addition to, the
aggregate limit that applies to flood losses and the per flood occurrence limit. The aggregate limits for
Coverage C — Basement Personal Property, Coverage D, and the Bacteria/Fungi/Wet Rot/Dry Rot Incidental
Coverage are part of, and not in addition to, the overall flood aggregate limit.

---

### Deductible

A special deductible provision applies to the total of all flood losses, excluding the Incidental Property
Coverage for Loss Avoidance Measures. Available deductible options are those shown in the rating information
section of the supplement. ⚠️ Specific deductible option amounts are in the filed rating information, not
reproduced in this entry.

---

### Endorsement And Entries

Attach endorsement **HO 2710**. Required entries on the endorsement schedule:
- Flood coverage inception date
- Deductible amount
- Per flood occurrence limit
- Policy aggregate limit
- As applicable: coverage occurrence limit and coverage aggregate limit for Coverage C — Basement Personal Property
- As applicable: coverage occurrence limit and coverage aggregate limit for Coverage D
- Coverage aggregate limit for the Incidental Property Coverage for Bacteria, Fungi, Wet Rot, Or Dry Rot

(SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)

---

### Premium Determination

Use the rating information shown in the supplement. The applicable rating information is filed and approved
by the company and shown on the manual page. (SOURCE: 2026-04-14_homeowners-hobp_MULTI_flood-supplement_v06-21 §1)
