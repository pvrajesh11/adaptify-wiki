## Rule 6.9 — Claim Free Discount [FL override]

First paragraph is replaced:

A policy is claim free if, during the three year period just before the date of new application or renewal preparation, all adults insured under the policy had Homeowners or equivalent coverage in force and no claims (excluding **catastrophic claims** as described below) of any dollar amount and for any cause of loss were paid under such coverage.

**Catastrophic claim exclusion (FL-specific):** A catastrophic claim that was the result of an event that caused **$25 million or more** in total insured property losses and affected a significant number of policyholders and insurers shall not be considered when determining if a policy is claim free.

## Rule 6.11 — Risk Group Assignment [FL override]

The multistate rule is replaced in its entirety. Key changes from multistate:

**Catastrophic claim exclusion added:** For claims history purposes, a claim is excluded if it resulted from an event that caused $25 million or more in total insured property losses and affected a significant number of property and casualty insurance policyholders and insurers.

**Risk Group Matrix (Rule 6.10 — FL designation):**

| Minor \ Major | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | G | I | K | M | O | Q | R |
| 1 | H | J | L | N | P | R | S |
| 2 | I | K | M | O | Q | S | T |
| 3 | J | L | N | P | R | T | U |
| 4 | K | M | O | Q | S | U | V |
| 5 | L | N | P | R | T | V | W |
| 6+ | M | O | Q | S | U | W | X |

All other aspects of risk group assignment (experience period, $500 minimum payment threshold, excluded claim types, major/minor claim classification) match the multistate rule, plus the FL catastrophic claim exclusion.

## Rule 4.19 — Owner Occupancy Requirement [FL-specific]

Added in revision 12 13. Applies to forms HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, HO 0006, and HO 0008.

**Permitted at company option:** A policy issued to any of the following can be endorsed to exclude coverage for loss to property occurring **61 days or more** after the date the named insured begins living at a location other than the described location on other than a temporary basis:

- Owner-occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling
- Owner-occupant of a condominium or co-operative unit
- Co-owner-occupant of a 2–4 family dwelling
- Occupant of a 1–4 family dwelling under a life estate arrangement
- Occupant purchasing a dwelling under a long-term contract

**Not permitted for:**
- Dwelling under construction
- Seasonal dwelling
- Additional or secondary location
- Condominium or co-operative unit regularly rented or held for rental to others for use as a residence

Premium: No charge or credit.
Endorsement: HO 2003.

## Rule 8.10 — Roof Surfacing Amendment — ACV For All Covered Property Losses [FL-specific]

Added in revision 03 13. Applies to forms HO 0001, HO 0002, HO 0003, HO 0005, and HO 0006.

**Scope:** Loss settlement for roof surfacing of buildings on the described location can be converted from replacement cost to actual cash value for **any covered peril** (not just windstorm/hail as under multistate Rule 8.9 / HO 4835).

**Not permitted** with:
- ACV loss settlement endorsement (Rule 8.1.1)
- Functional replacement cost endorsement (Rule 8.1.2)

Premium: Use applicable factor from state pages per peril.
Endorsement: HO 4836.

**Note:** This is distinct from the multistate Rule 8.9 (HO 4835) which converts roof surfacing to ACV for windstorm or hail only. FL Rule 8.10 uses HO 4836 and covers all perils.

---

## Rule 8.11 — Cosmetic Damage Exclusion [FL-specific]

Added in revision 03 13. Applies to all eligible forms.

**Scope:** A policy can be endorsed to exclude coverage for **cosmetic damage** to the surfacing of covered buildings caused by windstorm or hail, when such damage consists solely of cosmetic damage.

Applies to:
- Exterior wall surfacing
- Roof surfacing
- Exterior door surfacing and exterior window surfacing (must apply to both together; cannot apply separately)

Premium: Use applicable factor from state pages per peril. Door/window surfacing treated as one type for premium purposes.
Endorsement: HO 2002 (with entry/entries indicating the specific surfacing to which exclusion applies).

## Definitions

Florida state pages contain no exceptions to multistate definitions and no additional definitions beyond the instruction to use the applicable ZIP Code Listing for rating zone assignments.

## Company Action

Filing submissions to adopt, reference, or modify the HOBP are **prohibited from disclosing components of the rating plan** in greater detail than disclosed in the ACME filing. Reference filings need only the filing reference numbers — do not include copies of ACME materials.

Affiliated companies must submit a complete loss cost multiplier filing, referencing ACME loss costs, to the **Florida Office of Insurance Regulation (FLOIR)** within 15 days after their chosen effective date.
