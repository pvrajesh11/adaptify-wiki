# Motor Truck Cargo — Multistate — Rating Manual REV 04 18

## Source Document Summary

Multistate Motor Truck Cargo (MTC) underwriting and rating manual defining rules and rating procedures that apply across all states by default. Edition REV 04 18. The document is a rating manual only — it contains rating steps, commodity classification index, coverage modification rules, and rating procedures. No separate form descriptions document was included in this ingest.

Loss costs (numerical factors, relativities, and rate tables) are contained in a separate Loss Cost Schedule (CIM MTC MU LCS 0823.docx) which was not processed — it is a rate table document.

## Extracted Content

### Rating Methods — Overview

Two primary premium determination methods are defined, selected based on insured size:

- **Per Vehicle Premium Determination (Method A):** Used when insured's gross receipts are $500,000 or less, OR insured has fewer than ten power units. Premium base: per vehicle limit × number of vehicles.
- **Gross Receipts Premium Determination (Method B):** Used when insured's gross receipts exceed $500,000, OR insured has more than ten power units. Premium base: insured's annual gross receipts.

Additional premium determination procedures apply for: mobile equipment, moving equipment, electronic equipment, personal property (Method C); trailers (Method D); adulterated food (refer to company).

---

### Per Vehicle Premium Determination (Method A)

**Step 1.A — Commodity Classification**
Refer to Commodity Classification Index to determine applicable classification (1–5). If commodity is Class 5, Step 6.A also applies.

**Step 2.A — Basic Load**
Determine basic load based on per vehicle limit and the following risk features:
- Commodity classification (1–2, 3–4, or 5)
- Radius of operations: Local (≤50 mi), Intermediate (51–200 mi), Long Haul (>200 mi; also refer to Step 8.A)
- Vehicle protection: guards/following vehicle; vehicle alarms/tracking equipment; no additional protection
- Use of owner-operators: none; limited use with background checks and vehicle inspection; extensive use with limited supervision
- Condition of fleet: newer vehicles with regular documented inspection; mix of newer/older with irregular inspection; older fleet with poor documentation
- Financial stability (via financial statement or CAB report): Excellent, Good, Poor

Limit per vehicle factor ranges: $1–$50,000 → 1.30–1.50; $50,001–$100,000 → 1.10–1.35; $100,001+ → 1.05–1.15

**Step 3.A — Refrigeration Breakdown Load**
If refrigeration breakdown coverage is provided:
- Risk features: distance traveled (Local/Intermediate/Long Haul); temperature alarms installed or not; availability of cold storage contracts/maps/no plan
- Load: 200–350 per vehicle
- Increased deductible modification: $2,500 → factor 0.95; $5,000+ → factor 0.90
- Add refrigeration breakdown load to basic load

**Step 4.A — Contingent Coverage Modification**
If contingent coverage is provided for property in care, custody, and control of subcontractor:
- Risk features: whether subcontractor shipments are incidental or substantial; whether insured is named as additional named insured; written brokerage agreement with hold harmless; established vs. short-term relationship with subcontractor
- Modification range: 0.90–1.25
- Multiply basic/modified load by this modification

**Step 5.A — Apply to Number of Vehicles**
Multiply number of vehicles by the load result from Step 4.A (or 3.A/2.A if prior steps not applicable).

**Step 6.A — Target/Hazardous Cargo Modification**
Applies when commodity is Class 5:
- Risk features: over-the-road exposure (driver presence, overnight parking security); protection of shipments (guards, alarms/tracking, none); pickup/drop-off location risk (low vs. high risk areas such as ports or high-crime localities)
- Modification range: 1.25–2.00

**Step 7.A — Apply Loss Cost Multiplier**
Multiply loss cost rating information by applicable company loss cost multiplier, then multiply by result of Step 5.A (or 6.A).

**Step 8.A — Radius of Operations Modification (Long Haul)**
When long haul coverage includes exposures beyond basic load:
- Risk features: distance (200–500 mi; 501–1,000 mi; over 1,000 mi); vehicle data availability (satellite tracking, black box vs. none); hours driven documentation (documented vs. not)
- Modification range: 0.05–0.50 (additional premium factor)
- Multiply result of Step 7.A by this modification to determine additional premium

**Step 9.A — Additional Terminal Modification**
When terminal coverage includes exposures beyond basic load:
- Risk features: construction (fire resistive; non-combustible; frame); terminal limit vs. vehicle limit (same vs. higher); special occupancy (hazardous materials storage or not); disaster exposure (prone to hurricane/brush fire or not)
- Modification range: 0.05–0.30 (additional premium factor)
- Multiply result of Step 7.A by this modification to determine additional premium

**Step 10.A** — Add additional premiums from Steps 8.A and 9.A to result of Step 7.A.

**Step 11.A — Named Perils Modification**
If Named Perils Endorsement (IM 7488) is written: Modification = 0.90

**Step 12.A — Deductible Modification**
$500 → 1.10; $2,500 → 0.90; $5,000 → 0.80

**Step 13.A — IRPM**
Modify by any applicable Individual Risk Premium Modification.

### Gross Receipts Premium Determination (Method B)

**Step 1.B — Commodity Classification**
Refer to Commodity Classification Index. If Class 5, use Class 4 load for basic load and apply Step 6.B.

**Step 2.B — Basic Load**
Determined based on:
- Radius of operations: Local (≤50 mi), Intermediate (51–200 mi), Long Haul (>200 mi; also refer to Step 6.B)
- Vehicle protection (guards/following vehicle; alarms/tracking; none)
- Use of owner-operators (none; limited with checks; extensive with limited supervision)
- Condition of fleet (newer/documented; mixed/irregular; older/poor documentation)
- Financial stability (Excellent, Good, Poor)

Load ranges (per $100 gross receipts) by gross receipts tier and commodity class:
- $250,000–$500,000: Class 1 → 0.50–0.70; Class 2 → 0.71–0.80; Class 3 → 0.81–0.95; Class 4 → 0.96–1.10
- $500,001–$2,500,000: Class 1 → 0.35–0.50; Class 2 → 0.51–0.72; Class 3 → 0.73–0.80; Class 4 → 0.81–0.85
- $2,500,001–$5,000,000: Class 1 → 0.30–0.42; Class 2 → 0.43–0.62; Class 3 → 0.63–0.75; Class 4 → 0.76–0.85
- Over $5,000,000: Class 1 → 0.22–0.32; Class 2 → 0.33–0.45; Class 3 → 0.46–0.70; Class 4 → 0.71–0.85

**Steps 3.B–10.B** — Parallel to Method A Steps 3.A–10.A; same modification rules apply with different base units (gross receipts per $100 instead of per vehicle).

- Refrigeration Breakdown Load: 0.10–0.15
- Contingent Coverage Modification: 0.90–1.25
- Target/Hazardous Cargo (Class 5): Modification 1.25–2.00
- Radius of Operations (Long Haul): Modification 0.05–0.50
- Terminal Modification: 0.05–0.30

**Step 11.B — Named Perils Form Modification:** 0.90

**Step 12.B — Deductible:** $500 → 1.10; $2,500 → 0.90; $5,000 → 0.80

**Step 13.B — IRPM**

### Additional Coverage — Mobile Equipment, Moving Equipment, Electronic Equipment, Personal Property (Method C)

Used if coverage is provided under IM 7450 (which includes built-in limits of $5,000 for moving equipment and electronic equipment — off-board and on-board). Premium base: limit of insurance.

**Step 1.C — Basic Load**
- Theft potential on road (driver always with vehicle in secured lot vs. not)
- Vehicle protection (alarms with secured lot vs. none)
- Radius (Local/Intermediate/Long Haul)
- Load range: 1.00–1.25

**Step 2.C** — Multiply limit of insurance (per $100) by basic load.
**Step 3.C** — Apply loss cost multiplier, then multiply by Step 2.C result.
**Step 4.C — IRPM**

---

### Additional Coverage — Trailer Coverage (Method D)

Premium base: limit of insurance.

**Step 1.D — Basic Load**
- Theft potential (driver/trailer always together in secured lot vs. not)
- Trailer protection (secured fenced lot when not in use vs. none)
- Radius (Local/Intermediate/Long Haul)
- Load range: 0.50–1.00

**Step 2.D** — Multiply limit of insurance (per $100) by basic load.
**Step 3.D** — Apply loss cost multiplier, then multiply by Step 2.D result.
**Step 4.D — IRPM**

### Reporting Form

When reporting conditions are part of the MTC coverage form:
- Reporting period: monthly, quarterly, or annual
- Premium adjustment period: monthly, quarterly, or annual
- Gross Receipts base can be converted to another reporting base (e.g., sales, mileage)

**Gross Receipts Determination + Gross Receipts Reporting:**
1. Determine premium per Gross Receipts Premium Determination
2. Divide premium by annual gross receipts (per $100) to get gross receipts reporting rate
3. Apply rate per $100 to reported gross receipts

**Per Vehicle Determination + Gross Receipts Reporting:**
1. Determine annual premium per Per Vehicle Premium Determination
2. Divide by annual gross receipts (per $100) to get gross receipts reporting rate
3. Apply rate per $100 to reported gross receipts

**Per Vehicle Determination + Per Vehicle Reporting (monthly):**
1. Determine per vehicle premium per Per Vehicle Premium Determination
2. Divide by 12 to get monthly per vehicle premium
3. Apply monthly premium to each vehicle in report
4. Sum vehicle premiums for monthly earned premium

### Commodity Classification Index

Full index of commodities and assigned classifications (1–5):

**Class 1 (lowest risk):** Canned goods, Cement/sand/gravel, Fertilizer, Grain/hay/feed, Lumber, Paper and paper products, Petroleum products

**Class 2:** Agricultural equipment, Beer and wine, Beverages (nonalcoholic), Building materials, Contractors' heavy equipment, Dairy products, Dry goods, General merchandise, Hardware and paint, Household effects, Livestock and live poultry, Metal and steel, Plumbing supplies, Poultry (dressed), Rugs and carpets

**Class 3:** Appliances (except TV and stereos), China and ceramics, Clothing, Eggs (shell), Electrical supplies and fixtures, Farm products, Food products, Furniture, Glassware, Liquid haulers (bulk nonflammable), Office equipment, Pipe/cable/wire, Shoes

**Class 4:** Automobile parts and accessories, Computers, Drugs (except narcotics), Food products — Frozen or refrigerated, Leather goods (except shoes), Machinery and heavy equipment, Tires and tubes

**Class 5 (highest risk — target/hazardous):** Alcoholic beverages (except beer and wine), Cameras and film, Cigarettes and cigars, Clothing — Ladies' and men's dress apparel, Cosmetics and perfume, Dangerous articles/explosives/corrosives/flammables/acids, Fine arts, Food products — Meat or seafood, Furs, Jewelry, Machinery and heavy equipment — Specialized heavy haulers, Machinery and heavy equipment — Power tools, Narcotics, Petroleum products — Under 140° flashpoint, Precious metals, Rugs and carpets — Oriental, Sporting goods and toys, Textiles, Tires and tubes, TV/radios/stereo equipment, Video equipment and tapes
