# PPA 0001 07 16 — Personal Automobile Policy

## Source Document Summary

Base personal automobile policy form, edition 07 16 (July 2016). Provides Liability (Part A), Medical Payments (Part B), Uninsured Motorist (Part C), and Physical Damage — Comprehensive and Collision (Part D) coverages. Conditions section governs duties, general terms, and termination. Applies as filed in AK; amended by PPA 0154 01 20 for Alaska-specific provisions.

2. **Bodily injury** — bodily harm, sickness, disease, or death
3. **Business** — trade, profession, or occupation (distinct from auto business)

### Part A — Liability Coverage

**Insuring Agreement:**
- Pays compensatory damages for which an insured is legally liable due to bodily injury or property damage from an auto accident
- Includes any prejudgment interest awarded in judgment against insured
- Duty to settle or defend at company option, at company expense, by company-chosen counsel
- Defense duty ends when company has paid an amount equal to the applicable limit via judgment or written settlement

**Insured (Part A):**
- Named insured or family member for ownership, maintenance, or use of any auto or trailer
- Any person using a covered auto
- Any person/entity for vicarious liability of a covered person
- Any person/entity for vicarious liability of named insured or family member in a non-covered auto

**Supplemental Payments (in addition to limit):**
- Up to $250 bail bonds required by auto accident with resulting BI/PD covered by policy
- Appeal bond premiums and bonds to release attachments (up to applicable limit)
- Up to $250/day per person for actual lost earnings at company request
- Post-judgment interest accruing until company offers to pay up to the limit
- Other reasonable expenses at company request

**Key Exclusions (Part A):**
- Expected, directed, or intended bodily injury or property damage
- Vehicle use without reasonable belief of entitlement (family member using owned covered auto: not excluded)
- Property damage to property owned by or being transported by insured
- Property damage to property rented to, used by, or in care of insured (exception: residence/private garage)
- Employee bodily injury in course of employment (domestic employee exception if no workers' comp)
- Public/livery conveyance use — including any period logged into transportation network program as driver
- Delivery/transport of food or other goods in commerce — including any period logged into TNP as driver
- Auto business employment or engagement
- Non-auto-business use (exception: farming/ranching, private passenger autos, pickups/vans, trailers)
- Nuclear energy liability (if also covered under nuclear energy policy)
- Performance activity participation or practice (vehicles inside designated performance area)
- Motorcycles, vehicles with fewer than four wheels, off-road vehicles (exception: medical emergency, trailers, non-owned golf carts)
- Non-covered auto owned by or furnished/available for regular use of named insured or family member
- Covered auto enrolled in personal vehicle sharing program while used by non-insured
- Punitive or exemplary damages

**How Much We Pay — Limit Structure:**
- BI per person limit: all damages (including care, loss of services, death) for one person in one accident; includes all derivative claims
- BI per accident limit (subject to per person): all BI damages for two or more persons in one accident
- PD per accident limit: all property damage in one accident
- Limits apply regardless of number of insureds, claims, vehicles/premiums on declarations, or vehicles involved
- No duplicate payments for same elements under Part A and Part B, Part C, or UIM
- Other insurance: proportional share; coverage for non-owned or temporary substitute vehicle is excess

### Part B — Medical Payments Coverage

**Insuring Agreement:**
- Pays reasonable expenses for necessary medical and funeral services from bodily injury caused by auto accident
- Services must be rendered within 3 years from accident date

**Insured (Part B):**
- Named insured or family member while occupying motor vehicle designed for public roads or trailer, or as pedestrian struck by such
- Any other person while occupying a covered auto

**Key Exclusions (Part B):**
- Bodily injury in course of employment where workers' comp applies
- Nuclear, radiation, war
- Occupying a motorcycle or vehicle with fewer than four wheels
- Vehicle located for use as residence or premises
- Vehicle use without reasonable belief of entitlement
- TNP/livery conveyance use while logged in as driver
- Delivery/transport of food or goods in commerce while logged into TNP
- Non-auto-business use in any business (exception: private passenger auto, pickup/van, trailer with covered auto)
- Performance activity area
- Personal vehicle sharing program while used by non-insured
- Owned or regularly available vehicle not covered under this policy

**Limit:** Per insured per accident; same non-duplication and other insurance rules as Part A.

### Part C — Uninsured Motorist Coverage

**Insuring Agreement:**
- Pays compensatory damages for bodily injury to which insured is legally entitled from owner/operator of uninsured motor vehicle
- Company may settle or defend at its option

**Key Exclusions (Part C):**
- Occupying or struck by motor vehicle owned by insured and not insured for UM under this policy
- Family member struck by named-insured-owned vehicle insured for UM on a primary basis under another policy
- Settlement that prejudices company's right to recover
- Vehicle use without reasonable belief of entitlement
- TNP/livery use while logged in as driver
- Delivery/transport in commerce while logged into TNP
- Any other applicable UM exclusions (full exclusions not fully extracted — verify form for complete list)

### Part D — Physical Damage Coverage

**Insuring Agreement:**
- Comprehensive: pays for direct and accidental loss to covered auto (or non-owned auto) not caused by collision; includes contact with bird or animal, breakage of glass
- Collision: pays for direct and accidental loss caused by collision with another object or overturn
- Automatically included: child safety seat replacement after loss; transportation expenses

**Settlement basis:**
- Lesser of actual cash value, cost of repair, or applicable limit
- Depreciation and physical condition considered in determining ACV
- Single deductible applies when loss from collision results in both collision and comprehensive coverage claim

**Key Exclusions (Part D):**
- Expected, directed, or intended loss
- Mechanical breakdown, road wear (exception: covered auto total loss from theft)
- Radioactive contamination, nuclear, war
- Electronic equipment not permanently installed
- Custom furnishings/equipment in or on pickup or van (exception: factory-installed)
- TNP/livery use exclusion
- Performance activity
- Personal vehicle sharing program while used by non-insured

### Conditions

Key conditions governing the policy (summarized):
- **Duties After Accident or Loss:** Prompt notice to company; cooperate with investigation/defense; send legal papers; submit to examination under oath
- **Legal Action Against Us:** No action against company unless fully complied with policy terms; no action on liability coverage unless amount finally determined
- **Our Right To Recover Payment (Subrogation):** Insured must preserve and transfer subrogation rights to company
- **Policy Period and Territory:** Policy period on declarations; coverage territory: US, territories/possessions, Puerto Rico, Canada, in-transit between these
- **Termination:** Named insured may cancel by returning policy or written notice; company may cancel; nonrenewal notice requirements
- **Transfer of Interest:** Policy transfers not valid without written company consent
