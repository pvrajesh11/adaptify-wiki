# Auto PPA — Alabama — Underwriting & Rating Manual v1.5

## Source Document Summary

This is the Personal Auto Underwriting (PAU) manual for Alabama, version 1.5. It defines the rules and exceptions governing how Personal Auto Policy form PA 0001 is issued, endorsed, and rated in Alabama. It covers coverage eligibility, mandatory and optional endorsements, underwriting rules, driving record point assignment, transportation network coverage requirements, and special vehicle classes.

---

## Extracted Content

### Policy Form (Rule 3.1)

Base form: **PA 0001 — Personal Auto Policy**

PA 0001 may be used to provide any of the following coverage combinations:
- Liability coverage only
- Liability + Medical Payments Coverage
- Liability + Uninsured Motorist Coverage
- Liability + Medical Payments + Uninsured Motorist Coverage
- Physical Damage Coverage (with or without liability and/or other coverages)

A separate premium is charged for each vehicle and each coverage on the declarations.

PA 0001 may also be endorsed to provide:
- Underinsured Motorist Coverage (Rule 3.7)
- First Party Benefits Coverage (Rule 3.8)
- Extraordinary Medical Benefits Coverage (Rule 3.9)
- Pedestrian Basic First Party Benefits Coverage (Rule 3.10)

---

### Uninsured Motorist Coverage (Rule 3.4)

UM Coverage pays for compensatory damages an insured is legally entitled to recover for bodily injury caused by the owner or operator of an uninsured motor vehicle.

**Mandatory offering:** UM Coverage is required when PA 0001 covers the liability exposures of vehicles on the declarations, unless the named insured rejects coverage in writing.

**Stacked vs. Non-Stacked election:** When the named insured does not reject UM, they must elect between:
- Non-Stacked → attach **PA 2137** — Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Non-Stacked - Alabama
- Stacked → attach **PA 2161** — Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Stacked - Alabama

**Combined Single Limit option:**
- Non-Stacked CSL → attach **PA 0737** — Combined Single Limit — Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Non-Stacked - Alabama
- Stacked CSL → attach **PA 0760** — Combined Single Limit — Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Stacked - Alabama

Limits apply on a per person / per accident basis. Available limits: refer to company.

---

### Underinsured Motorist Coverage (Rule 3.7)

UIM Coverage pays for compensatory damages an insured is legally entitled to recover for bodily injury caused by the owner or operator of an underinsured motor vehicle.

**Mandatory offering:** Required when PA 0001 covers liability exposures, unless the named insured rejects in writing.

**Stacked vs. Non-Stacked election:**
- Non-Stacked → attach **PA 2237** — Underinsured Motorist Coverage — Non-Stacked - Alabama
- Stacked → attach **PA 2256** — Underinsured Motorist Coverage — Stacked - Alabama

**Combined Single Limit option:**
- Non-Stacked CSL → attach **PA 0837** — Combined Single Limit — Underinsured Motorist Coverage — Non-Stacked - Alabama
- Stacked CSL → attach **PA 0856** — Combined Single Limit — Underinsured Motorist Coverage — Stacked - Alabama

---

### First Party Benefits Coverage (Rule 3.8)

At the named insured's option, three benefit tiers are available:

- **Basic First Party Benefits** — medical expenses only
- **Additional First Party Benefits** — medical expenses, loss of income, funeral expenses, and accidental death
- **Combination First Party Benefits** — same as Additional with higher available limits, subject to a maximum total benefits limit

Endorsement: **PA 2637** — First Party Benefits Coverage - Alabama

---

### Extraordinary Medical Benefits Coverage (Rule 3.9)

Provides coverage for medical expenses in excess of $100,000.

Endorsement: **PA 3005** — Extraordinary Medical Benefits Coverage - Alabama

---

### Pedestrian Basic First Party Benefits Coverage (Rule 3.10)

Provides coverage for medical expenses resulting from maintenance or use of vehicles excluded from First Party Benefits Coverage.

Endorsement: **PA 3006** — Pedestrian Basic First Party Benefits Coverage - Alabama

---

### Mandatory Endorsements (Rule 4.2)

All policies issued in Alabama must include:
- **PA 0101** — Amendatory Endorsement - Alabama
- **PA 9901** — Insurance Consultation Services Exemption Act Notice
- **PA 9902** — Limited Tort Alternative Information Notice - Alabama

---

### Limited Tort Option (Rule 5.4)

Each insured must be given the opportunity to choose in writing between:
- **Limited Tort Option** — reduced premium; insured gives up right to sue for non-economic damages unless serious injury or applicable exception
- **Full Tort Option** — insured retains unrestricted right to sue for all damages

Base rates are for the Full Tort Option. A Limited Tort Factor applies if the Limited Tort Option is selected (applied in Step 1.b. of the Calculation of Premium).

Endorsement: **PA 9902** — Limited Tort Alternative Information Notice - Alabama

---

### Joint Ownership (Rule 4.9)

A policy may be endorsed to cover multiple owners of a described vehicle, including:
- Two or more persons not married who reside in the same household
- Two or more persons not in the same household who are related by blood, marriage, or adoption

Endorsement: **PA 1237** — Joint Ownership Coverage - Alabama
Premium: No charge or credit applies.

---

### Intra-Family Liability Exclusion (Rule 10.2)

The multistate Intra-Family Liability Exclusion rule **does not apply** in Alabama.

---

### Named Driver Exclusion (Rule 10.5)

A policy may exclude any or all coverages for any named driver(s).

Endorsement: **PA 4324** — Named Driver Exclusion - Alabama
Premium: Excluded driver is not included in premium determination (Rule 5).

---

### Driving Record Points (Rule 6.4.1)

**Experience period:** 3 years immediately preceding the policy inception date or renewal effective date.

**Points are assigned for:**
- Convictions for motor vehicle violations
- At-fault accidents where a claim was paid in part or whole by the company and exceeds the approved dollar threshold (refer to company for threshold)

When multiple violations result from the same occurrence, use only the violation with the highest point assignment.

A 2nd and any subsequent violation within the experience period is assessed both the age-of-violation points AND the additional violation surcharge.

**Additional points:**
- **4 points** — lapse in mandatory/compulsory insurance of 30+ days immediately preceding policy inception, unless:
  - Driver did not own a vehicle requiring mandatory coverage during that period, OR
  - Lapse was due to driver's active military service or deployment
- **1 point** — conviction not classified in the table below that results in suspension or revocation of the driver's license

**Point assignment table:**

| Classification | 0–<6 Mo | 0–<1 Yr | 1–<2 Yr | 2–<3 Yr | Add'l Violation Surcharge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speeding Violation (non-Major) | N/A | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Minor Violation (non-Speeding) | N/A | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Major Violation | N/A | 4 | 3 | 3 | 1 |
| At-Fault Accident | N/A | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| No Prior Insurance | 4 | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A |

Violation classifications follow the AAMVA Code Dictionary.

**Points are NOT assigned for:**
- Violations/accidents for a driver who is the named insured of a separate policy or principal driver of a vehicle insured under a separate policy
- Non-moving violations (e.g., vehicle equipment violations)
- A single conviction for a violation listed under 75 Pa. C.S. § 1535 during the experience period ⚠️ *(flagged: possible Pennsylvania statute artifact — verify AL applicability)*
- Two or more convictions under 75 Pa. C.S. § 1535 unless committed by the same insured driver ⚠️ *(same flag)*
- At-fault accidents eliminated by Accident Forgiveness (Rule 4.7.4)
- Accidents where the insured auto was lawfully parked and stationary (if a parked auto rolls and causes an accident, points are assigned to the driver who parked it)
- Accidents where the insured driver is reimbursed by or has a judgment against another person determined responsible
- Accidents covered by UM or UIM coverage
- Accidents where damage is covered under Comprehensive Coverage
- The insured auto was struck in the rear and the insured driver had no moving violation conviction
- The operator of the other auto was convicted of a moving violation and the insured driver was not
- Accidents during emergency response (paid or volunteer police, fire, first-aid, law enforcement) — exception ends when auto ceases to be used in the emergency
- Hit-and-run accidents reported to proper authority within 24 hours
- Accidents involving damage by contact with animals or fowl
- Accidents involving physical damage from flying gravel, missiles, or falling objects
- Comprehensive claims unless the loss was intentionally caused by an insured

**Financial Responsibility Filing (Rule 6.4.2):** Rule does not apply in Alabama.

### Transportation Network / Ride Sharing Coverage (Rule 7.3.2)

Per **Act 164** (Alabama statute), the following minimum liability requirements apply to transportation network drivers:

**Phase I** — driver is logged into a transportation network program, prior to accepting a request, no passenger:
- Liability minimum: $50,000/person BI, $100,000/accident BI, $25,000 PD
- First party medical minimum: $5,000 for driver, $25,000 for pedestrians
- The policy maintained by the driver may provide some or all of these amounts; if provided, must be at least the Alabama financial responsibility minimums
- Endorsement: **PA 6602** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Accepted Passenger

**Phase II** — driver has accepted a request, no passenger yet occupying the vehicle:
- Liability minimum: $500,000 BI and PD combined
- First party medical minimum: $5,000 for driver, $25,000 for pedestrians
- Endorsement: **PA 6603** — Transportation Network Program Coverage — No Passenger

**Phase III** — passenger is occupying the vehicle:
- Liability minimum: $500,000 BI and PD combined
- First party medical minimum: $5,000 for driver, $25,000 for passengers and pedestrians
- Endorsement: **PA 6604** — Transportation Network Program Coverage

Premium determination for all phases: Use primary use classification (Rule 7.3.1) plus applicable ride-sharing use factor from state pages.

## Key Differences from Alaska (AK) v1.5

The AL manual is structurally identical to the AK manual with two notable distinctions:
- **Mandatory endorsement:** AL uses PA 0101 (Amendatory Endorsement - Alabama); AK uses PA 0154
- **Driving record point table:** AL table is identical to AK table
- **Transportation Network statute:** Both cite "Act 164" but apply it to Alabama financial responsibility minimums vs Alaska minimums
