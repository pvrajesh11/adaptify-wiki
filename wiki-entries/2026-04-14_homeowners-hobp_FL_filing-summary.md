## Summary Of Filing

This filing introduces two documents for the Florida Homeowners program:

1. **HO 0003 09 20 — Special Form (HO-3)**: Open perils coverage on dwelling and other structures (Coverages A and B); named perils coverage on personal property (Coverage C).

2. **HO 0809 02 22 — Amendatory Endorsement — Florida**: Addresses:
   - Innocent insured protection under the Florida Domestic Violence statute
   - Subrogation provisions related to domestic violence
   - Renewal notice requirements
   - Suit Against Us limitations (substitutes statutory time period for contractual two-year period)

## Key Observations

- The base HO 0003 09 20 Special Form is a standard ISO-derived open-perils homeowners form used nationally.
- HO 0809 02 22 is a Florida-specific mandatory endorsement that overrides four base policy provisions.
- No new rating plan or manual revision is referenced in this filing summary — it covers forms only.
