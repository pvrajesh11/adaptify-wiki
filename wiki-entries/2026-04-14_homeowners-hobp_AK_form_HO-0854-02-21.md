# Homeowners HOBP — AK — Form HO 0854 02 21

Source: HO 0854 02 21 Amendatory Endorsement Alaska.docx
Form: HO 0854 02 21 — Amendatory Endorsement — Alaska

## Overview

HO 0854 02 21 is the Alaska Amendatory Endorsement for the Homeowners HOBP program, edition February 2021. It makes 18 numbered amendments to the base HO policy forms covering: definitions, property coverage modifications, liability coverage modifications, claims handling procedures, and policy conditions (cancellation, misrepresentation, appraisal, suit against us, premium change notice).

---

## Amendment-by-Amendment Summary

### 1. Definitions — "Pollutant" Revised `[AK-specific]`

Replaces the multistate definition of "pollutant":

> "Pollutant" means any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, or radioactive irritant or contaminant, including acids, alkalis, chemicals, fumes, smoke, soot, vapor, and waste. Waste includes materials to be disposed of as well as recycled, reclaimed, or reconditioned.

### 2. Incidental Property Coverages — Credit Card Coverage: Attorney Fee Provision `[AK-specific]`

The Credit Card; Electronic Fund Transfer Card Or Access Device; Forgery; And Counterfeit Money incidental coverage is amended to add:

- In any suit the company defends, defense expense includes attorney's fees for which the insured is liable under **Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82**.
- The company will not pay attorney fees in excess of fees calculated by applying the schedule for contested cases in **Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82(b)(1)** applied to the applicable limit.

### 3. HO 0003 Only — Settling, Cracking, Shrinking, Bulging, Or Expanding Exclusion: Revised `[AK-specific]`

Replaces the exclusion in HO 0003 (Perils Insured Against, Coverages A/B Exclusions) with an enumerated list of covered structural elements:

Exclusion applies to settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expanding of: (a) bulkheads; (b) ceilings; (c) floors; (d) footings; (e) foundations; (f) patios; (g) paved areas; (h) roofs; or (i) walls — **whether resulting from a natural cause, manmade cause, or combination thereof.**

### 4. HO 0005 Only — Settling, Cracking, Shrinking, Bulging, Or Expanding Exclusion: Revised `[AK-specific]`

Same modification as Amendment 3, applied to HO 0005 (Coverages A, B, and C Exclusions). Enumerated list uses items 1)–9) rather than a)–i) but is substantively identical.

### 5. All Forms Except HO 0003 and HO 0005 — Property Exclusions Introductory Paragraphs: Replaced `[AK-specific]`

Replaces the first two introductory paragraphs of Property Coverages Exclusions:

> "We" will not pay for loss or damage caused directly by any of the following excluded events. A loss may be caused by a chain of causes. If a covered peril is the dominant cause of such a loss, "we" will not deny coverage on the basis that a secondary cause in that chain is not a covered peril.
>
> These exclusions apply whether or not an extensive area suffers damage from or is affected by the excluded cause or event.

This is the "efficient proximate cause / dominant cause" rule — coverage is not denied if a covered peril is the dominant cause even if a secondary excluded cause is also in the chain.

### 6. HO 0003 and HO 0005 Only — Property Exclusions Introductory Paragraphs: Replaced `[AK-specific]`

Same replacement language as Amendment 5, applied to HO 0003 and HO 0005 (item 1. of the exclusions section for these forms).

### 7. All Forms — Water Exclusion, Item 5) Replaced `[AK-specific]`

Replaces item 5) of the Water exclusion:

> The exclusions set forth in 1) through 4) above:
> a) apply whether the excluded event results from a natural cause, a manmade cause, or a combination thereof; and
> b) apply to, but are not limited to, water and matter present in or carried or otherwise moved by water, whether driven by wind or not, that: (1) overtops; (2) escapes from; (3) is released from; or (4) is otherwise discharged from; a dam, levee, dike, floodgate, or other device or feature designed or used to retain, contain, or control water.

### 8. Liability Coverages — Claims And Defense Cost: Attorney Fee Provision `[AK-specific]`

Amends Claims And Defense Cost under Incidental Liability Coverages to add:

- Costs taxed to an insured include attorney's fees for which an insured is liable under **Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82**.
- Company will not pay attorney fees in excess of fees calculated by applying the contested cases schedule in **Alaska Rule of Civil Procedure 82(b)(1)** to the applicable limit.

### 9. Liability Coverages — War Exclusion (Coverage L and M): Revised `[AK-specific]`

Replaces exclusion a. (bodily injury or property damage caused by war/military action/insurrection). The replacement deletes the original and substitutes a three-part enumeration:

1. War, including undeclared or civil war;
2. Warlike action by a military force, including action in hindering or defending against an actual or expected attack, by any government, sovereign, or other authority using military personnel or other agents;
3. Insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action taken by governmental authority in hindering or defending against any of these.

Discharge of a nuclear weapon is deemed a warlike action even if accidental.

### 10. What Must Be Done In Case Of Loss — Examination Under Oath: Right to Counsel `[AK-specific]`

Adds to the Examination Under Oath provision:

> An "insured" has the right to have legal representation present when examined under oath.

### 11. What Must Be Done In Case Of Loss — Damage To Property Of Others: Sworn Statement Deadline `[AK-specific]`

Replaces Other Duties – Damage To Property Of Others:

> "We" must be given a sworn statement of loss within **60 days** after "our" request. The damaged property must be shown to "us" if it is within an "insured's" control.

### 12. How Much We Pay — Coverage L Anti-Stacking Rule `[AK-specific]`

Replaces Insurance Under More Than One Policy provision for Coverage L — Personal Liability:

> If there is other valid and collectible insurance, other than insurance written specifically to provide coverage in excess of the limits that apply in this policy, "we" pay only "our" share of the loss. "We" pay only that part of the loss that the applicable limit under this policy bears to the total amount of insurance covering the loss.

(Proportional sharing rather than stacking multiple policies.)

### 13. Payment Of Loss — Property Coverages: Undisputed Payment Timing `[AK-specific]`

Replaces item a. of Payment Of Loss, Property Coverages:

- Company adjusts each loss with the named insured ("you").
- Undisputed portion paid within **30 working days** after acceptable proof of loss is received and amount of loss is agreed to in writing.
- Disputed portion paid within **30 working days** after: (1) entry of a final judgment; or (2) filing of an appraisal award with the company.
- Payment made to named insured unless a loss payee or other person is named or legally entitled.

### 14. Cancellation And Nonrenewal: Replaced `[AK-specific]`

Entirely replaces the multistate Cancellation and Nonrenewal condition. Key AK provisions:

**Cancellation — mid-term (first 60 days):** Company may cancel for any reason.

**Cancellation — after 60 days / renewal policies:** Company may cancel only at anniversary date unless:
1. Premium not paid when due (20 days notice required)
2. Insured convicted of crime with element increasing hazard insured against (10 days notice)
3. Fraud or material misrepresentation discovered in obtaining insurance or pursuing a claim (10 days notice)
4. Discovery of grossly negligent act/omission substantially increasing hazards (30 days notice)
5. Physical change in property making it uninsurable (30 days notice)
6. Entire abandonment of property increasing a hazard (30 days notice + notice to lender on file)

**"Entire abandonment" defined:** Property no longer occupied by insured and lacks contents of substantial utility — but not considered abandoned if insured or agent demonstrates the property is being reasonably maintained and monitored.

**Notice method:** First class mail with USPS certificate of mailing, or electronic means with confirmation of receipt.

**Named insureds age 70+:** Notice also sent to any person the insured has designated in writing.

**Unearned premium return:**
- Cancellation for nonpayment, conviction, or fraud: return within 45 days after notice
- Cancellation for other reasons: return before effective date of cancellation
- Insured-initiated cancellation: return within 45 days after receipt of cancellation request or effective date, whichever is later; or within 45 days after audit completion (with audit completed within 45 days)

**Nonrenewal:** At least 20 days notice required.

No nonrenewal notice required if: (1) company has in good faith manifested willingness to renew; (2) expiring policy premium unpaid; or (3) renewal premium not paid as required.

### 15. Misrepresentation, Concealment, Or Fraud: Replaced `[AK-specific]`

Replaces the multistate condition. No coverage is provided for any insured where there has been misrepresentation, omission, concealment of fact, or incorrect statement if:
a. Such misrepresentation/omission is **fraudulent**;
b. Such misrepresentation/omission is **material** to acceptance of the risk or hazard assumed; or
c. Company **would not have issued the policy** (or would have issued it at different terms) if true facts had been known.

Applies even to an insured who was not involved in the misrepresentation.

### 16. Premium Or Coverage Changes: Notice Requirement Added `[AK-specific]`

New condition added under Conditions Applicable To All Coverages:

If company decides to increase renewal premium by more than **10%** for any reason other than an increase in coverage or exposure base, or to **materially restrict or reduce coverage** not specifically requested by the named insured, the company must provide **at least 20 days' notice** before expiration date by:
a. First class mail to named insured at last known address and to agent/broker of record; or
b. Electronic means to named insured and agent/broker at last known electronic addresses.

### 17. Appraisal: Replaced `[AK-specific]`

Replaces the appraisal condition with a detailed AK-specific version:

- Either party may demand appraisal for disputes over value or amount of loss.
- Each party selects a competent and impartial appraiser within **10 days** of written demand.
- Appraisers select a competent and impartial umpire; if they disagree on umpire, either party may ask a judge of a court of record of the state where the described location is located to make the selection.
- Each appraiser must separately state the amount of loss in writing no later than **15 days** after the umpire is selected (unless extended by umpire).
- Agreement of two appraisers is binding. If appraisers disagree, they submit differences to umpire; agreement of one appraiser and umpire is binding.
- **Cost allocation:** Named insured pays expense of their counsel and adjuster; company pays its own. All other expenses determined by umpire.
- **Scope limitation:** Appraisal may not be used to interpret policy terms, determine causation, or determine whether a loss is covered.

### 18. Suit Against Us: Replaced `[AK-specific]`

Replaces the Suit Against Us condition (Property Coverages):

> No suit may be brought against "us" unless all of the "terms" that apply to the Property Coverages have been complied with and the suit is brought within **3 years** after the date that the claim was denied.
>
> If a law of the state where the "described location" is located makes this time period invalid, the suit must be brought within the time period allowed by the law.
