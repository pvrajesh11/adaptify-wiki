# Motor Truck Cargo — AL — Form CIM 2001 08 15

Form: CIM 2001 08 15 | Edition: August 2015
Title: Amendatory Endorsement — Alabama
Pages: 1

---

## Overview

CIM 2001 08 15 is a one-page Alabama-specific amendatory endorsement to the Motor Truck Cargo policy. It makes three changes: adds an abuse exception to the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion for innocent insureds; amends the Subrogation condition for abuse situations; and replaces the Suit Against Us condition with a statute-of-limitations-based version (in most coverage forms).

---

## Section 1 — Perils Excluded: Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts — Abuse Exception

**What changes:** Adds an exception for innocent insureds when loss is caused by an act of abuse.

**Rule:** If loss is caused by an act of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute) committed by or at the direction of an insured, the Criminal/Fraudulent/Dishonest/Illegal Acts exclusion does NOT apply to an otherwise covered loss suffered by another insured who:
- Is the subject of the abuse, AND
- Did not cooperate with or contribute to the act that caused the loss, AND
- Either:
  - Files a complaint under the **Alabama Protection From Abuse Act** against the abuser and does NOT voluntarily dismiss the complaint, OR
  - Seeks a warrant for the abuser's arrest for the act causing the loss and cooperates in prosecution

**Payment cap:** Payment to the innocent insured may be limited to that person's insurable interest in the property, less any payment made to a mortgagee or other party with a legal secured interest in the property.

---

## Section 2 — Other Conditions: Subrogation — Abuse Carve-Out

**What changes:** Innocent insured subject to abuse cannot waive their right to recover.

**Rule:** An innocent insured who is the subject of abuse (as defined by Alabama statute) cannot waive the right to recover. The company retains all subrogation rights up to the amount paid for any loss caused by an act of abuse.

---

## Section 3 — Other Conditions: Suit Against Us (Replacement)

**Scope:** Applies in all coverage forms **except**: Cold Storage Locker Coverage, Contingent Cargo Coverage, Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability Coverage, Riggers' Legal Liability Coverage, and Warehouse Legal Liability Coverage.

**What changes:** Deletes the base form's Suit Against Us condition and replaces it.

**Rule:** No legal action may be brought against the company under this coverage unless:
1. All terms of the coverage have been complied with, AND
2. The suit has been brought within the **time period prescribed by law** for the commencement of such action

The base form used a fixed contractual time period; this Alabama endorsement defers to the applicable statute of limitations instead.
